## Guiding Principles

1. **Source PDFs are authority.** Do not infer values from current wiki text.
2. **Public PDFs stay available.** Do not remove or hide them.
3. **Separate source truth from exam notes.** Operator/exam interpretations must be marked as application notes, not authoritative TS wording.
4. **Use explicit direction language.** Avoid ambiguous bare arrows. Prefer text such as `Trip occurs at or above 92%`.
5. **Make pages learner-first.** Each LCO should answer: what is required, when it applies, what actions/clocks happen, why it matters, and what exam traps exist.
6. **Audit before broad rewrite.** For setpoints and LCO data, extract/source-check first, then edit.
7. **Stage exact files only.** Do not use `git add .`.

## Proposed Layout Standard for Tech Spec Pages

Use this structure for each major Tech Spec wiki page or section:

```md
# TS 3/4.X — Section Title

## Source PDFs
- TS PDF: [filename](...)
- Bases PDF: [filename](...)

## Operator Summary
- Required equipment / condition
- Applicability
- Required action / completion time highlights
- Why it matters

## LCO Index
| LCO | Title | Applicability | Key Action/Clock | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## LCO 3.X.X — Title

### Requirement
Plain-language restatement with exact TS source link.

### Applicability
Modes/conditions.

### Actions and Completion Times
Concise table.

### Surveillance Requirements
Concise table.

### Basis
Source-backed basis summary.

### Watchouts / Exam Traps
Clearly marked learner notes.

## Setpoints / Numeric Values
| Item | Trip/Limit Direction | Value | Plain-English Meaning | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
```

For TS 3/4.3 tables specifically, use a stricter table format:

```md
| Function | Condition Type | Allowable Value | Direction Meaning | Source Table |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pressurizer Water Level — High | High trip | >= 92% | Trip must occur before/at high level reaching 92% span, per source convention | Table 3.3-1 |
```

Exact wording should be source-verified before insertion.
