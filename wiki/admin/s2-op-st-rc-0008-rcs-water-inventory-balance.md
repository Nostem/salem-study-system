# S2.OP-ST.RC-0008 — RCS Water Inventory Balance

> ⚠️ DRAFT — Reconstructed from exam materials

## Purpose

Surveillance procedure that performs the RCS Water Inventory Balance required by Tech Spec Surveillance 4.4.7.2.1.d to demonstrate RCS operational LEAKAGE is within limits in Modes 1–4.

## Key Actions / Information

### Duration Rules (Precautions & Limitations)

- **P&L 3.15** — A routine RCS Water Inventory Balance performed to satisfy T/S Surveillance Requirement 4.4.7.2.1.d should normally be performed over a <span class="hi">two hour duration</span>.
- **P&L 3.16** — IF an RCS Water Inventory Balance is performed for reasons OTHER than to satisfy T/S Surveillance Requirement 4.4.7.2.1.d, THEN the time interval may be determined at the discretion of the SM/CRS (abbreviated duration).

**Exam & operating coverage:**

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2014 Q86</div>
Performance of S2.OP-ST.RC-0008(Q), RCS Water Inventory Balance, required by T/S 4.4.7.2.1.d is <span class="hi-exam">NOT required to enter Mode 4</span> — the surveillance is not required to be completed until <span class="hi-exam">12 hours after establishment of steady state operation</span> (per S2.OP-IO.ZZ-0002 P&L 3.8). TS Surveillance 4.4.7.2.1.d specifically states the provisions of <span class="hi-exam">Tech Spec 4.0.4 are not applicable</span> for entry into Mode 4, so the unit may proceed to Mode 4 without performing the balance, and TS 4.0.3 does not apply (4.0.3 covers missed/overdue surveillances).
</div>
