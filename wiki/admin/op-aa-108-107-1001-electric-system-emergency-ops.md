# OP-AA-108-107-1001 — Electric System Emergency Operations

## Purpose

Administrative procedure governing Electric System Emergency Operations and Electric System Operator (ESO) Interface. Directs Station Load Curtailment actions when grid conditions require it.

## Key Actions / Information

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2020 Q81</div>
AB.GRID-0001 Attachment 3 directs initiating <span class="hi-exam">Station Load Curtailment IAW OP-AA-108-107-1001</span> when 500 KV Switchyard Voltage is < 493 KV on any line.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2014 Q78</div>
When a unit goes off-line (e.g., a planned Rx trip to enter a refueling outage), the following are directly informed within 24 hours: the <span class="hi-exam">ESOC (Electric System Operations Center)</span> per step 2.2.1, and <span class="hi-exam">PSEG Energy Resources &amp; Trade (ER&amp;T)</span> per step 2.3.2.2 (PSEG Nuclear informs them of plant status twice per day). The NRC is also notified (morning phone call if not sooner). <span class="hi-trap">NERC (North American Electric Reliability Corporation) is NOT directly informed</span> — Annual Integrity Training specifies what PSEG Power employees can and cannot tell other companies under the PSEG umbrella.
</div>
