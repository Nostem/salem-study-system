# AD-AA-101-1003 — Implementing Procedure Writers Guide

## Purpose

Fleet implementing procedure that establishes the standards for writing Salem implementing procedures, including how acceptance-criteria steps are designated.

## Key Actions / Information

**Exam & operating coverage:**

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2012 Q68</div>
Acceptance-criteria designators (Step 4.5.4): <span class="hi-exam">a dollar sign ($) — or the Technical Specification number — at a step marks a Technical Specification acceptance-criteria value</span>, placed where the value is given or where verification for that value is checked. <span class="hi-trap">UFSAR acceptance criteria use a cent symbol (¢) or the UFSAR number instead — NOT a dollar sign.</span> Each surveillance procedure containing $ signs carries a Precaution and Limitation: "Steps identified with a dollar sign ($) are those items required to meet Technical Specification acceptance criteria. Such steps, if not satisfactorily completed, may have reportability requirements and should be brought to the <span class="hi-exam">immediate attention of the SM/CRS</span>."
</div>
