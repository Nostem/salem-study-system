# OP-AP-300-1001 — PWR Control Rod Movement Requirements

> ⚠️ DRAFT — Reconstructed from exam materials

## Purpose

Provides requirements for manual control rod movements during non-transient conditions, including minimum communication requirements prior to rod manipulation.

## Key Actions / Information

**Exam & operating coverage:**

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2020 Q68</div>
Per step 4.4.3, during non-transient conditions the RO shall STATE the <span class="hi-exam">selected control rod bank initial position, target control rod position, and the direction of movement</span> just prior to manually manipulating control rods. <span class="hi-trap">The minimum required information is bank position/target/direction — NOT T<sub>AVG</sub>, NOT NIS power level, NOT expected number of steps.</span>
</div>

<div class="callout callout-jpm">
<div class="callout-label">JPM — 2016 Sim-a</div>
Peer check applied during S2.OP-ST.RCS-0001 Shutdown Bank surveillance: prior to each manual rod motion (insert and restore for SBA, SBB, etc.), the operator <span class="hi-exam">states the initial Shutdown Bank position, target control rod position, and direction of rod movement</span>, and the peer-checker confirms "Peer check SAT" before motion. The peer check is required for ALL non-emergency manual rod motion at Salem.
</div>
