<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
When responding to multiple stuck rods during the performance of EOP-TRIP-2, Reactor Trip Response, why are there two different boration times associated with borating the RCS for those stuck rods?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> As the number of stuck out rods increases, the amount of boration required to be added per rod also increases.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> If a cooldown is in progress a higher boration total is required vs if the plant is stable in Hot Standby.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> The time in core life (BOL vs EOL) require different specific boration times to be used.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> The different available borated water sources contain different ppm boron.</div>
</div>

<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(10) Step 7 of TRIP-2 requires boration for 2 or more control rods not fully inserted. It first attempts rapid boration from the Boric Acid Storage Tanks via a Boric Acid Transfer pump in high speed, for 35 minutes per rod. If this is unsuccessful, then boration from the Refueling Water Storage Tank via a charging pump running at greater than 87 gpm is performed for 120 minutes per rod. The BAST minimum Cb is 6550 ppm per Tech Specs, while the RWST minimum Cb is 2300 ppm.</div>
