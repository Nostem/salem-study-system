<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given the following conditions:</strong><br><br>
- Unit 2 is performing a normal Liquid Release from 21 CVCS Monitor Tank via 22 SW header to Unit 1 CW IAW S2.OP-SO.WL-0001, Release of Radioactive Liquid Waste from 21 CVCS monitor Tank.<br>
- The Radwaste Overboard Discharge Flow Recorder is OPERABLE.<br>
- The 2R18 and the 2R41D rad monitors are OPERABLE.<br>
- After commencing the release, the operator is compiling the Initial Data IAW appropriate attachment.<br>
- When recording the 2R18 reading, it reads 10E6 cps.<br>
- The 2R18 red alarm light is lit on the 104 panel.<br>
- The 2WL51 indicates OPEN in the control room.<br><br>
Which of the following describes what these indications mean, and how the operating crew should proceed?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> The 2WL51 should have automatically closed on the valid 2R18 high radiation alarm. The NEO should shut 2WL51 locally.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> The 2WL51 should have automatically closed on the valid 2R18 high radiation alarm. The NCO in the control room should shut 2WL51.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 2R18 has failed high. Verify the flow recorder and 2R41D are OPERABLE, block the R-18 input to the 2WL51 on 2RP1, and continue the liquid release.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> The 2R18 reading is well below the expected Hi Rad Alarm setpoint. Verify the flow recorder and 2R41D are OPERABLE, block the R-18 input to the 2WL51 on 2RP1, and continue the liquid release.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(11,13)6.82E5 cps is the ALARM setpoint which will automatically shut the 2WL51 (S2.IC-CC.RM-0028) The current reading is above the setpoint at which the 2WL51 should have automatically shut, and the NCO should shut the valve remotely. Additionally, S2.OP-SO.WL-0001 for releasing the tank, Step 5.5.9, says if the 2R18 alarms, then the NEO is to inform the NCO to shut the 2WL51. There is no provision for closing the valve locally.</div>
