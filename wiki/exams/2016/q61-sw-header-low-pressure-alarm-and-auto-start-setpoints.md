<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
<br>
- Unit 2 is operating at 100% power during a summer heat wave.<br>
- 21, 24, and 26 SW pumps are in service.<br>
- SW header pressure is 106 psig.<br>
- The 2SW308 #2 Bay Overpressure Protection valve slowly fails open.<br>
<br>
Which of the following describes how the plant will respond with NO operator action?<br>
<br>
OHA's B-13 21 SW HDR PRESS LO and B-14 22 SW HDR PRESS LO will annunciate at 1) ______ psig, and the standby SW pump will auto start at 2) _____ psig.
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 1) 99.5<br>2) 95.5.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 1) 99.5<br>2) 99.5.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 1) 105.0<br>2) 95.5.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 1) 105.0<br>2) 99.5.</div>
