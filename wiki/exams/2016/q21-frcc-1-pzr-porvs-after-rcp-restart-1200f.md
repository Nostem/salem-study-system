<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong> the following condition:<br><br>
- Operators are performing actions in 2-EOP-FRCC-1 , Response to Inadequate Core Cooling.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies how the PZR PORVs will be operated after starting ALL available RCPs with CET temperatures above 1200°F?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Shut BOTH PORVs.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Shut ONLY ONE PORV.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Open ONLY ONE PORV.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Open BOTH PZR PORVs.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer & Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(10) RCPs will be started at step 25 if CETs are still &gt;1200°F, to clear the water in the RCS intermediate leg and permit the circulation of hot gases from the overheated core to circulate through the steam generators. If RCP(s) restart is not effective in decreasing CET temperature &lt;1200°F (it is not per stem), the PZR PORVs (BOTH) will be opened at step 25.3 to help reduce RCS pressure to allow ECCS injection.</div>
