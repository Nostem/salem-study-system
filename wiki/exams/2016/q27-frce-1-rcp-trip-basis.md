<strong>Given:</strong> Following a SBLOCA inside containment concurrent with a faulted SG inside containment, the following conditions are noted:<br><br>
- RCS subcooling is indicating 10°F.<br>
- RCS pressure is 1380 psig.<br>
- Containment spray actuation has occurred, but neither Containment Spray pump started.<br>
- The crew is implementing 2-EOP-FRCE-1, Response to High Containment Pressure.<br><br>
Which of the following describes why the Reactor Coolant Pumps (RCP) are tripped under these conditions?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> To prevent RCP motor damage due to lack of cooling.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> To minimize additional loss of reactor coolant from RCP seal leakoff.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> To prevent subsequent thermal barrier damage due to backflow of RCS fluid.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> To minimize possible subsequent core damage due to pumping a two-phase mixture.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(10,3) CS actuation occurs at 15 psig in containment, as well as Phase B isolation, which shuts RCP CCW cooling inlet and outlet valves. Per FRCE-1 basis document page 4, "...RCPs are tripped since component cooling water to the RCP seals and motors is isolated." B is incorrect but plausible if RCP seal package operation is not understood (running pump to secured pump). C is incorrect but plausible based on having no CCW flow to thermal barrier. D is incorrect but plausible if it is thought a two phase mixture could be present (subcooling is indicated in stem).</div>
