<strong>Given:</strong><br>
With Unit 2 at power, which of the following failures would cause a <strong>SINGLE</strong> control rod to drop fully into the core?

<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Loss of Power to a single Power Cabinet during demand for rod movement.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Loss of Power to a single Logic Cabinet with no demand for rod movement.</div>
