<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q93 — Containment Purge Reinstatement After CVI Signal</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">G2.1.23 (RO 4.3 / SRO 4.4)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following Initial Conditions at 0800:<br><br>
- Unit 2 is in MODE 5.<br>
- Containment Purge is in service.<br><br>
At 0830, Containment Purge is stopped to support a maintenance visual inspection of purge ductwork inside containment.<br><br>
At 1015, a Containment Ventilation Isolation (CVI) signal inadvertently actuates due to a rad monitor switch mis-positioning.<br><br>
At 1030:<br>
The duct inspection is complete.<br>
Radiation Protection reports containment was not breached in the past 2 hours except to allow entry and exit of workers through the airlock.<br>
Chemistry reports there is operational assurance that no radiological changes to containment environment have occurred and requests containment purge be restored.<br><br>
IAW S2.OP-SO.WG-0006, Containment Purge to Plant Vent, is a new containment purge release form required and why?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Yes, because of the CVI signal.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Yes, because the purge was secured.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> No, because the termination was &lt;4 hours and there is operational assurance of unchanged radiological conditions.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> No, because the termination was &lt;12 hours (one shift) and containment has not been breached except as allowed for worker ingress/egress through containment airlocks.</div>
</div>

Correct.</strong> 55.43(4)Procedure S2.OP-SO.WG-0006 allows for reinstatement of purge if of short duration (~4 hours) and containment rad conditions have not changed ( P&amp;L 3.3) Short duration is NOT 12 hours, and distracter does not address rad conditions. New effluent permit not always required. Can block CVI signal IAW Att 2, Temporary Termination and Reinstatement of Containment Purge.</div>
