# 2016 Q85 — MSLB Mixing Bottle — LOSC-2 CAS after Step 20, GO TO EOP-LOSC-1 with one nonfaulted SG

<div style="background:#111827;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:8px;padding:20px;margin-bottom:24px;">
<div style="display:flex;justify-content:space-between;align-items:center;margin-bottom:12px;">
<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q85 — Uncontrolled Depressurization of all Steam Generators</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">00WE12A201 EA2.1 (SRO 4.0)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given the following conditions:</strong><br>
- Unit 2 has experienced a MSLB at the Mixing Bottle.<br>
- All attempts at MSLI have failed, and 21-24MS167s remain open.<br>
- Operators have just completed SI termination steps in EOP-LOSC-2, Multiple Steam Generator Depressurization, and PZR level is being maintained stable.<br>
- AFW flow to each SG is 1.0E4 lbm/hr.<br>
- The RO reports rising pressure in 22 SG.<br>
<br>
Which of the following describes how the CRS should proceed, and why?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Transition to EOP-LOSC-1, Loss of Secondary Coolant, since one SG is now available for subsequent recovery actions.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Transition to EOP-LOSC-1 Loss of Secondary Coolant and stop RCPs if RCS pressure is &lt;1350 psig, since RCPs cannot be stopped in LOSC-2.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Remain in EOP-LOSC-2 since returning to EOP-LOSC-1, Loss of Secondary Coolant will require a transition to EOP-LOCA-1, Loss of Reactor Coolant, upon completion.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Remain in EOP-LOSC-2 until positive control can be established over the cooldown after the remaining Steam Generators have fully depressurized, then transition to EOP-LOSC-1, Loss of Secondary Coolant.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> 55.43(5) LOSC-2 CAS states that upon a pressure rise in any SG except when performing SI termination in Steps 8-20, GO TO EOP- LOSC-1. The stem states that it is after Step 20. LOSC-1 Basis Document, page 7, states that.."Any cooldown operations that are performed as subsequent recovery actions will require at least one nonfaulted SG."</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> Source explanation does not separately address — see correct-answer block above (LOSC-2 CAS directs GO TO EOP-LOSC-1 on the SG pressure rise after Step 20; the basis is the availability of a nonfaulted SG, not RCP-stop criteria).</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> Source explanation does not separately address — see correct-answer block above (LOSC-2 CAS directs going to LOSC-1 once the pressure rise in any SG occurs after Step 20).</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> Source explanation does not separately address — see correct-answer block above (LOSC-2 CAS directs going to LOSC-1 immediately upon the SG pressure rise after Step 20, not after the remaining SGs have fully depressurized).</div>
