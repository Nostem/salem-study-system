<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 1 is shutdown during a refueling outage.<br>
- A normal release of 14 Waste Gas Decay Tank to the plant vent is scheduled to be performed on day shift IAW S1.OP-SO.WG-0011, Discharge of 14 Gas Decay Tank to Plant Vent.<br><br>
When reviewing the schedule, which of the following activities is allowed to be scheduled during the period 14 WGDT is being released?
