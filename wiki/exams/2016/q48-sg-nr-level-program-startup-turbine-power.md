<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
Unit 2 is performing a plant startup to full power.<br><br>
Predict the Steam Generator Narrow Range Levels for the following <strong><u>TURBINE</u></strong> power levels.<br><br>
Assume a normal power ascension.<br><br>
10% Turbine Power = ____ % NR level.<br>
60% Turbine Power = ____ % NR level.
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 38.5%, 46%.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 38.5%, 44%.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 33%, 46%.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 33%, 44%.</div>
</div>

Correct.</strong> 55.41(4) SG NR level is programmed so that as Turbine power rises from 0-20% (Turbine steamline inlet pressure) it rises from 33-44%. On Unit 1 only, 20-100 it rises from 44-48. A is incorrect but plausible if candidate thinks programmed level is based Turbine power but uses Unit 1 ramp from 20-100 (calculated at 60% power). B is correct.
