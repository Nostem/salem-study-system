<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">006000A212 A2.12 (4.8)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 was operating at 100% power when a SBLOCA occurs.<br>
- Operators transitioned from 2-EOP-LOCA-1, Loss of Reactor Coolant, to 2-EOP-LOCA-2, Post LOCA Cooldown and Depressurization with RCS pressure of 1050 psig.<br>
- When performing RCS Depressurization to Minimize Subcooling with a single PORV, RCS subcooling is lost when the PZR PORV fails to shut and is isolated by closing its Block Valve.<br>
- Charging flow through the BIT is in service due to no RCS subcooling.<br><br>
Which of the following conditions, if any, describes the action the CRS will direct after the depressurization with regards to ECCS Accumulator isolation?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Accumulators will be isolated if RCS Thots are &lt;375&deg;F.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Accumulators will be isolated if RCS pressure is &lt;1,000 psig.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Accumulators will be isolated if PZR level is &gt; 11% (19% adverse).</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Accumulators will NOT be isolated since SI has not been terminated.</div>
</div>

Correct.</strong> 55.43(b)5. SRO level because question requires knowledge of a section of procedure, LOCA-2, where ECCS Accumulators will be isolated when RCS Thots &lt;375 (Step 35.3.) With no subcooling, RCS Thots must be below 375&deg;F for Accumulators to be isolated, otherwise the step to do it is bypassed. Accumulators WILL be isolated &lt;1,000 psig, BUT ONLY after IOP-6 has been entered, the initiation of which is performed later in LOCA-2 (Step 42)</div>
