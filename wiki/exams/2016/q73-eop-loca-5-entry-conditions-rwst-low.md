<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Which of the following contains conditions that warrant entry to EOP-LOCA-5 "Loss of Emergency Coolant Recirculation"?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> RWST LOW LEVEL alarm annunciates with &lt;62% level in Containment Sump.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> The hot leg recirculation flow path is unavailable due to blockage of flow paths.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> RWST LOW-LOW LEVEL alarm annunciates requiring all ECCS pumps with suction from the RWST to be stopped.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> LOCA outside containment is indicated, and both RHR pumps were stopped IAW EOP-LOCA-6, LOCA OUTSIDE CONTAINMENT.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer & Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(10) EOP-LOCA-5 can be entered from 5 places. 1. LOCA-1 step 16 with no RHR pump and associated SJ44 available; 2. LOCA-3 step 2, Containment sump level less than 62%; 3. LOCA-4 step 5, No RHR pumps running; 4. LOCA-6 step 6.2, with RH1,2,26 shut, 21 and 22 RH19 shut, 21 and 22SJ49's shut and RCS pressure not rising; 5. Loop from LOCA-5 step 28 when RWST level is still above LO-LO (1.2') setpoint. Answer is correct because containment sump level should be rising as RWST level is lowering.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> B is incorrect because there is no provision in LOCA-4 to check if the flowpath is blocked or not. It only has the alignment performed, then return to procedure in effect.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C is incorrect because it is a condition that would be expected to occur as RWST level dropped during CL recirc as 1 CS pp continued to draw water from RWST.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> D is incorrect because LOCA 5 only requires RHR pumps to be available, not in service.</div>
