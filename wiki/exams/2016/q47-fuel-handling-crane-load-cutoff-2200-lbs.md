<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
Which of the following describes an interlock on the <strong><u>Unit 2</u></strong> Spent Fuel Handling Crane?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> A high load weight cutoff prevents lifting and movement of loads &gt;2200 lbs. over the spent fuel pool.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> A high radiation signal on 2R32A, Fuel Handling Crane, will lockout ALL crane motion to prevent excessive radiation dose rate on bridge.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> A bridge over travel limit will stop the bridge from contacting the hard stops even if the direction button is held down, to prevent damage to the bridge.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> A high radiation signal on 2R5, FHB-SFP, OR 2R9, FHB-New Fuel Storage Area prevents upward crane movement to prevent excessive radiation does rate on bridge.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(10,12) The overload cutout is provided to prevent lifting loads &gt;2200 lbs. If they can't be lifted, then they can't be moved over the spent fuel in the pool IAW TR 3.9.7, and is tested IAW TR 4.9.7.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> B is incorrect because downward crane movement is still available to lower the fuel bundle.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C is incorrect because on Unit 2, the proximity switches only transfer the crane to slow, but movement will continue if the direction PB is continued to be depressed. On unit one, it stops movement.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> The 2R5 and 2R9 are not interlocked with the crane, but are interlocked with FHB ventilation. Technical Requirements Manual, 3.9.7, surveillance TR 4.9.7 applicable with fuel in the SFP.</div>
