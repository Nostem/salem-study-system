<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
A Containment Entry must be made in Mode 1.<br><br>
When is Radiation Protection Supervisor (RPS) approval required AND what is the limit for the number of personnel that may enter containment?<br><br>
Assume the Shift Manager has NOT granted permission to exceed the procedural limit<br><br>
The RPS must give prior approval if the entry is made during &hellip;.
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> a power ascension at &gt;5% / hr ONLY, and no more than 10 people may be in containment at one time.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> any reactor power change at &gt;5% / hr, and no more than 10 people may be in containment at one time.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> a power ascension at &gt;5% / hr ONLY, and no more than 20 people may be in containment at one time.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> any reactor power change at &gt;5% / hr, and no more than 20 people may be in containment at one time.</div>
