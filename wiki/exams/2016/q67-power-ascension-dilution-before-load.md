<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">G2.1.2 (RO 4.1 / SRO 4.4)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; Unit 2 is operating at 60% power.<br>
&bull; Power was reduced when 22 SGFP tripped 2 days ago, and has remained at this level for 2 days.<br>
&bull; Operators are preparing to start a Main Turbine power ascension using dilution and automatic rod control to maintain Tavg and AFD on program.<br>
&bull; Tavg-Tref deviation is 0&deg;F.<br><br>
IAW OP-AA-300, Reactivity Management, how should the power ascension be started?<br><br>
Assume this is a normal power ascension with all equipment available.
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> The crew concurrently initiates the Main Turbine load ascension and a RCS dilution.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> The crew initiates a RCS dilution. As soon as the dilution is in progress the Main Turbine load ascension is initiated.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> The crew initiates a RCS dilution and waits until a RCS temperature rise is detected. Then the Main Turbine load ascension is initiated.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> The crew initiates the Main Turbine load ascension and waits until a RCS temperature lowering is detected. Then the RCS dilution is initiated.</div>
</div>
