<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q15 — SGTR-1 AFW Flow Control to Ruptured SG</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">000038 A1.44 (3.4)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong> the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 has experienced a tube rupture on 22 SG.<br>
- After the reactor was tripped, the Main Turbine failed to automatically trip, and was manually tripped using the Turbine Trip handle on the control console.<br>
- All feedwater flow was isolated to 22 SG during performance of EOP-TRIP-1, Reactor Trip or Safety Injection.<br>
- 21, 23, and 24 SG NR levels are off-scale low.<br>
- 22 SG NR level is 1% and rising slowly.<br>
- Operators are currently performing actions in EOP-SGTR-1, Steam Generator Tube Rupture.<br>
- Prior to performing the RCS cooldown to Target Temperature, operators are assessing AFW flow status.<br><br>
Which of the following describes how AFW flow to 22 SG should be controlled?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Leave AFW flow isolated to 22 SG for the remainder of the event regardless of 22 SG NR level.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Establish AFW flow to 22 SG until its NR level is &gt; 9%, then isolate AFW flow to 22 SG. Maintain 22 SG NR level &gt;9%.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Establish AFW flow to 22 SG until its NR level is &gt;19%, then isolate AFW flow to 22 SG. Maintain 22 SG NR level &gt;19%.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Leave AFW flow isolated to 22 SG until the cooldown to Target Temperature is complete, then establish AFW flow as required to keep the SG tubes covered.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer & Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(4,10) Step 6 of SGTR-1 directs AFW flow be established to the ruptured SG if its NR level is &lt;9%.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A is incorrect but plausible if it is thought that you never feed a ruptured SG if intact SG's are available.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C is incorrect but plausible since it is the correct action, but the incorrect level. Note: 19% is the new (REV. 30 of EOPs) SG NR level above which intact SGs are directed to be maintained at.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> D is incorrect but plausible if it is thought that the purpose of leaving ruptured SG isolated during cooldown is to minimize spread of contamination IF intact SGs are available for cooldown (which is asked for in step 10.1 for initiating CD to Target Temp).</div>
