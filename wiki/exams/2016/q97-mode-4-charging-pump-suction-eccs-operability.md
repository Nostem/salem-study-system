<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q97 — Mode 4 Charging Pump Suction Closed Causing ECCS Inoperable</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">194001G2.2.37 2.2.37 (4.6)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Salem Unit 2 is in Mode 4.<br>
- All RCS Tcolds are 280°F.<br>
- 21 Charging pump is in service.<br>
- 22 RHR loop is in service in SDC.<br><br>
While hanging a tagout for 23 Charging pump, the suction valve for 21 charging pump is closed instead of the suction valve for 23 Charging pump.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies how the CRS is required to respond?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Restore at least one ECCS subsystem to operable status or maintain RCS Tavg <350°F.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Immediately suspend all operations which may result in a change in RCS boron concentration.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Within 1 hour action shall be initiated to place the unit in Cold Shutdown within the next 24 hours.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Restore at least one ECCS subsystem to operable status within 1 hour or be in Cold Shutdown within the next 20 hours.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> 55.432.b(2) This question meets the KA by having to determine that with RCS Tcolds<312°F, all but 1 charging pump must be rendered inoperable, as well as both SI pumps, and that one ECCS loop is required to be operable with that 1 charging pump, and closing the suction valve of that pump makes it inoperable. Distracter a is incorrect because it is the action (for the same LCO 3.5.3) if a required operable RHR pump were to become inoperable. Distracter b is incorrect because only positive reactivity changes are suspended, not ALL boron changes. Distracter c is incorrect because it is the action for Tech Spec 3.0.3, which is not applied in this case because there is an action for no operable ECCS subsystem inoperable. D is correct per LCO 3.5.3 Action a.</div>
