- Control room operators have progressed to the point where the SECs have been reset.<br>
- Containment pressure was 4.5 psig when the SECs were reset.<br><br>
Which of the following describes the Containment Spray System response should a HI-HI containment pressure signal be generated at this point in the accident?<br><br>
CS system valves...

Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(7,8,9)The SEC controller operates the CS pumps at 2 different point in the sequence UNTIL the SEC is reset. The SEC ONLY control the CS pumps, not the CS valves. The valve realign on the hi-hi containment pressure signal whenever it is received, but once the SEC is reset, it will not start the CS pumps since the sequencer is no longer active.</div>
