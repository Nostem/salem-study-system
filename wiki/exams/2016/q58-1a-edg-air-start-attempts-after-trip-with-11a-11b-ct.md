<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given the following conditions:</strong><br><br>
- Unit 1 is operating at 100% power.<br>
- 1A EDG starting air receiver 11A is C/T.<br>
- 11B EDG starting air compressor is C/T.<br>
- A Loss of Off-Site Power occurs.<br>
- 1A EDG trips shortly after starting.<br>
- 11A starting air compressor will NOT run.<br><br>
By design, what is the minimum number of subsequent 1A EDG start attempts available with the air start system in this configuration?
</div>
