<div style="margin-top:12px;padding:10px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;">
<strong style="color:#22c55e;">Key Decision Point:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">The BAST Volume step is the discriminating step — the applicant must use TS 3.1.2.6.a (one BAST as borated water source) AND/OR 3.1.2.6.b (two BASTs combined as the borated water source), together with Figure 3.1-2, to evaluate whether the recorded BAST volume / boron concentration combination is SAT. With 21 and 22 BASTs cross-connected and each at 49.5% (combined ~99%) at 6610 ppm, the operating point falls within the acceptable region of Figure 3.1-2 — surveillance is SAT. Compare with the 2019 RO-A2 JPM where similar inputs (48 + 48 = 96% at 6650 ppm) fell BELOW the Figure 3.1-2 line and made the surveillance UNSAT.</span>
</div>
