<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong> the following conditions:<br><br>
- A LBLOCA occurred on Unit 2.<br>
- 2C 4KV vital bus locked out on Bus Differential.<br>
- Both 22 and 24 CFCUs failed to start in low speed and cannot be started.<br>
- After entering 2-EOP-LOCA-3, Transfer to Cold Leg Recirculation when required, the crew has transitioned to EOP-LOCA-5, Loss of Emergency Recirculation when neither SJ44 valve opened.<br>
- Containment pressure is 18 psig.<br>
- The crew is performing Step 9 to determine the required number of Containment Spray pumps to be operated<br><br>
Which of the following identifies the Containment Spray pump(s) which will be in service if Step 9 is completed seven minutes after the RWST Lo Level alarm was received?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> BOTH Containment Spray pumps.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> NEITHER Containment Spray pump.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 21 Containment Spray pump ONLY.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 22 Containment Spray pump ONLY.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(10) Table C uses RWST level, Containment pressure, and the number of operating CFCUs in Low speed to determine how many containment spray pumps are required to be run. LOCA-3 was entered with RWST level at 15.2', and with 2C 4KV vital bus deenergized will be depleting at ~13,000 gpm, with 2 RHR, 1 charging, 1 SI, and 1 CS pump running. This will require over 9 more minutes of injection before RWST level would be lower than 1'. With the stem stating that Step 9's complete 7 minutes after 15.2' in RWST, then Table C will indicate that ONE CS pump will be required.. 22 CS pump does not have power with C vital bus deenergized, so 21 will be run. If RWST drain down calculation is incorrectly performed and it is assumed RWST level is <1', then NO CS pumps would be required. The BOTH distracter is for RWST level between 1-15.2' if either containment presure is >47 psig, or if no CFCUs were running.</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> If RWST drain down calculation is incorrectly performed and it is assumed RWST level is <1', then NO CS pumps would be required.</div>
