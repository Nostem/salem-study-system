<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q82 — LOCA Cooldown and Depressurization [OUTDATED]</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">00WE03A201 EA2.1 (SRO 4.2)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given the following conditions:</strong><br>
- Unit 2 is responding to a LOCA coincident with a LOOP, and has transitioned from EOP-LOCA-1, Loss of Reactor Coolant, to EOP-LOCA-2, Post LOCA Cooldown and Depressurization.<br>
- RCS cooldown has been initiated.<br>
- The RCS has been depressurized as required with a PZR PORV.<br>
- All RCS Thots are 450&deg;F.<br>
- When performing Step 19 RCP Status, the RO reports that RCS subcooling is NOT &gt;0&deg;F.<br>
<br>
Which of the following identifies how the CRS should proceed?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Initiate SI to restore RCS subcooling and return to LOCA-1, step 1.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Start ECCS pumps as necessary to restore PZR level and return to LOCA-1, step 1.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Initiate SI to restore PZR level, and ensure BIT isolation valves open if previously shut.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Start ECCS pumps as necessary to restore RCS subcooling, and open BIT isolation valves if previously shut.</div>
</div>

Correct.</strong> 55.43.(b) This question is SRO level based on having to assess conditions, then select a section of procedure, in this case going from Step 19.1 RNO to Step 34, bypassing charging and SI pump reduction steps based on inadequate RCS subcooling.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> The initiate SI distracters are incorrect because only specific required ECCS components would be operated after the initial SI actuation has occurred.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> The distracters stating to restore PZR level are incorrect because the stem states that RCS depressurization has been performed, which is only stopped when PZR level is adequate (&gt;25%).</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> The initiate SI distracters are incorrect because only specific required ECCS components would be operated after the initial SI actuation has occurred. The distracters stating to restore PZR level are incorrect because the stem states that RCS depressurization has been performed, which is only stopped when PZR level is adequate (&gt;25%).</div>
