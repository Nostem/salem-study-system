<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong> the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 is operating at 100% power, steady state.<br>
- The Charging System Master Flow Controller is in MANUAL.<br>
- A malfunction in the H2 pressure regulator supplying cover gas to the VCT occurs, and VCT pressure rises from 25 to 35 psig.<br><br>
Which of the following describes the effect this will have on CVCS when steady state conditions are established?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Charging flow will rise.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Letdown flow will rise.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Charging flow will lower.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Letdown flow will lower.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(5,7) Raising VCT pressure will cause NPSH available to the charging pumps to rise. With the flow controller in manual, and more suction head available, pump flow will rise. The letdown pressure control valve will respond in automatic and letdown flow will remain the same. Static pressure at the pump suction: An increase in surge tank level, increase in the pressure above the liquid in the surge tank, increase in booster pump output, or opening of the pump suction valve would increase suction pressure.</div>
