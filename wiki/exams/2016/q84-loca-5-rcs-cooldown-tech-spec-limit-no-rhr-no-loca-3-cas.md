<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q84 — Loss of Emergency Coolant Recirculation [EDITED]</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">00WE11A202 EA2.2 (SRO 4.2)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given the following conditions:</strong><br>
- Unit 2 experienced a LOCA from 100% power.<br>
- At step 11 of 1-EOP-LOCA-1, Loss of Reactor Coolant, a transition to LOCA-5, Loss of Emergency Recirculation, was made with both RHR pumps stopped and unable to be started.<br>
- Just prior to initiating a cooldown, the RWST LO level alarm is received.<br>
- CONT SUMP CH A/B LEVEL &gt;62% lights are lit.<br>
- RCS subcooling is &lt;0&deg;F.<br>
<br>
Which of the following describes how the CRS is required to proceed?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Remain in LOCA-5 and initiate makeup to the VCT at maximum rate.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Remain in LOCA-5 and initiate a RCS cooldown at less than Tech Spec limit.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Transition to LOCA-3, Transfer to Cold Leg Recirculation, perform valve realignment steps, return to procedure in effect.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Transition to LOCA-3, Transfer to Cold Leg Recirculation, align cont sump to RHR suction, then remain in LOCA-3 until direction is provided by the TSC.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> 55.43.(b)5 The stem says the transition to LOCA-5 was at step 11 of 1-EOP-LOCA-1 so RWST level &gt;15.2'. The cooldown in LOCA-5 is done at &lt;100&deg;F/hr, which is the TS RCS cooldown limit to prevent an unwanted Thermal Shock condition, which would complicate matters since CFSTs are still in effect in LOCA-5.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Makeup to the RWST is directed at step 10, not to the VCT.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> There is no LOCA-3 CAS in LOCA-5. Restoring any Train of recirc is the concern with no RHR pumps, the transfer to CL recirc will occur only after a RHR pump has been restored.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> There is no LOCA-3 CAS in LOCA-5. Restoring any Train of recirc is the concern with no RHR pumps, the transfer to CL recirc will occur only after a RHR pump has been restored.</div>
