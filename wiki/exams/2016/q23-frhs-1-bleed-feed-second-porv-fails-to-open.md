<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q23 — FRHS-1 Bleed and Feed consequence of second PORV failing to open</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">00WE05K301 EK3.1 (3.4)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong> the following conditions:<br><br>
- A loss of heat sink has occurred.<br>
- The operating crew is establishing RCS Bleed and Feed in accordance with EOP-FRHS-1, Loss Of Secondary Heat Sink.<br>
- The RO opens one PORV. He reports that the second PORV will NOT open.<br><br>
Which one of the following describes the consequences of the PORV failure?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> ALL SGs will require depressurization to inject the alternate source of feedwater.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> The RCS will rapidly re-pressurize when the SGs empty, resulting in a violation of a RCS Safety Limit.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Bleed and Feed cooling of the RCS must be terminated and secondary depressurization to inject condensate pump flow must be immediately initiated.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> The RCS may not depressurize quickly enough to ensure sufficient SI flow to provide RCS heat removal, and other RCS openings may have to be established.</div>
</div>
