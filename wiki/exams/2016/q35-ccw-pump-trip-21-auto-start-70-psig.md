<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q35 — 22 CCW Pump Trip System Response</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">008000K401 K4.01 (3.1)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong> the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 is operating normally at 65%, steady state.<br>
- 22 and 23 CCW pumps are in service.<br><br>
Subsequently 22 CCW pump trips.<br><br>
Which of the following describes how the CCW system will respond or be manipulated by the control room crew?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 21 CCW pump will auto start when CCW header pressure lowers to 70 psig.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 21 CCW pump will auto start immediately since less than 2 CCW pump 4KV breakers are shut.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> The NCO will start 21 CCW pump immediately since the 3rd CCW pump is normally aligned in MANUAL to prevent 3 CCW pumps from operating simultaneously.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> The control room crew will enter S2.OP-AB.CC-0001, Component Cooling Abnormality, isolate the non-safeguards header, and start 21 CCW pump if CCW header pressure lowers to 80 psig.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(7) Normal CCW system alignment is 2 CCW pumps running in MANUAL, and one CCW pump in AUTO and stopped. A CCW pump selected to AUTO will start when either 21 or 22 CCW header pressure lowers to 70 psig. There is no interlock based on breaker positions, but other system DO have a breaker anticipatory function. AB.CC could be entered, but no actions will be performed other than ensuring the backup pump started.</div>
