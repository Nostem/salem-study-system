<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q75 — Loss of All OHA — Control Room Response</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">G2.4.32 (RO 3.6 / SRO 4)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
- Unit 2 is operating at 100% power steady state.<br>
- The unit has achieved "black board" status on the OHA's with NO OHA's currently in alarm.<br>
- An expected OHA is NOT received when opening a panel which provides an OHA.<br><br>
Which of the following describes the control room response IAW S2.OP-AB.ANN-0001, Loss of Overhead Annunciator System, if it is determined that a complete and total loss of ALL Overhead Annunciators has occurred and cannot be restored within 15 minutes?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Initiate alternate alarm check of the OHA system every 15 minutes by opening and closing a RPS or SSPS cabinet door.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Initiate a controlled shutdown at &lt;5% per minute to bring the unit off line. Line.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Initiate continuous control console walkdown.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Trip the Rx and GO TO EOP-TRIP.</div>
</div>

Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(7) With a loss of all annunciators, tripping the Rx or initiating a power reduction are not warranted unless overriding plant status would direct it, not just from the annunciator problem. Initiating continuous walkdowns of control consoles (and P-250, etc.) is directed. With a total loss of OHA's, you don't check functionality by trying to initiate an alarm every 15 minutes, that's performed to verify status if some functionality is present.</div>
