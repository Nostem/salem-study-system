<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q10 — Excess Letdown HX Valve Response to SI Signal</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">026 K3.02 (RO 0 / SRO 0)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong> the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 initiates a Safety Injection from 100% power due to a LOCA.<br>
- 15 minutes after the SI is initiated, containment pressure peaks at 5 psig.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies an automatic response which has occurred for Component Cooling Water System components, and why?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 2CC215 and 2CC113, Excess Letdown Heat Exchanger CCW isolation valves, received a close signal to ensure this non-essential containment flow path is isolated.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 21 and 22CC16, RHR HX CCW isolation valves, received an open signal to ensure that long term cooling of the RCS is in service when the swap to Cold Leg Recirc is required.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 2CC215 and 2CC113, Excess Letdown Heat Exchanger CCW isolation valves, received a close signal to ensure ALL CCW supply and return from the containment is isolated.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 21 and 22CC16, RHR HX CCW isolation valves, received an open signal to ensure that RHR pumps do not overheat if the RCS remains above the shutoff head of the RHR pumps.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer & Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(7,8) The SI signal sends a close signal to the 2CC215 and CC113, Excess Letdown HX isolation valves, as they are Containment Phase A isolation valves. The purpose of closing Phase A isolation valves is to ensure all non-essential containment penetrations are isolated on a SI signal. The RCP CCW is still being supplied until a Phase B signal at 15 psig in cont.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> B is incorrect because closing CC16s do not receive an open signal until the ARM PB is depressed and the RWST level is 15.2' and manual alignment is required to place ECCS in CLR.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C is incorrect because ALL CCW supply and return are not isolated on a SI signal. The RCP CCW is still being supplied until a Phase B signal at 15 psig in cont.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> D is incorrect because the CC16s do not receive an open signal until the ARM PB is depressed and the RWST level is 15.2', and the RHR pumps are cooled by either flow through the pump from RWST (LBLOCA) or recirc flow (SBLOCA until pp is S/D).</div>
