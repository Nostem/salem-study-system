<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q16 — Steam Line Rupture RCS cooldown rate after MSLI / EOP-TRIP-1 transition</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">040 AK1.02a (RO 3.3 / SRO 3.3)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong> the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 has experienced a rupture of the 24 main steamline in the mechanical penetration area upstream of 24MS167.<br>
- The Rx is tripped and a MSLI performed.<br>
- Feed flow is isolated to 24 SG.<br>
- Operators are preparing to transition out of EOP-TRIP-1.<br><br>
Which of the following describes how the RCS cooldown <u>rate</u> will be affected over the next 30 minutes?<br><br>
The RCS cooldown rate will...
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> remain constant until 24 SG blows dry.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> lower as the faulted SG pressure lowers.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> remain constant until AFW flow is re-initiated to 24 SG, at which time it will increase.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> lower until at least one intact SG NR level rises above 9%, then remain constant as AFW flow is lowered.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer & Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(4,5,14) As the faulted SG pressure lowers due to the steam break, break flow will lower, and the rate at which the RCS is being cooled due to break flow will lower as a result.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A is incorrect but plausible if it is thought that a static break will pass the same lbm of steam flow during the entire event.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C is incorrect but plausible if it is thought that feed flow is initiated to keep the SG tubes wet, which only would be performed in LOSC-2 if ALL SGs were faulted.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> D is incorrect but plausible if it is thought that the lowering of AFW flow, which would put LESS cold FW flow into the SGs, would offset the lowering steam flow effect on RCS cooldown.</div>
