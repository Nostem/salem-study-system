<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q83 — Pressurized Thermal Shock</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">G2.4.6 (RO 3.7 / SRO 4.7)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given the following conditions:</strong><br>
- Unit 2 was operating at 15% power prior to synchronizing the Main Generator.<br>
- A Main Steamline rupture occurred that resulted in multiple Steam Generators depressurizing in containment before 2 steam generators could be isolated from the 2 faulted SGs.<br>
- The 2 faulted SG Tcolds are reading 270&deg;F and lowering.<br>
- The intact 2 SG Tcolds are 330&deg;F and stable.<br>
- RCS pressure is 500 psig and slowly lowering.<br>
- Containment pressure is 16 psig and slowly lowering.<br>
- All SG NR levels are &lt;9%.<br>
- Total AFW flow is 24E4 lbm/hr.<br>
- Source Range NIs are NOT energized.<br>
- Intermediate Range SUR is 0.0 DPM.<br>
<br>
With CFST's in effect, which of the following identifies the procedure entry required, and actions which will be performed in that procedure?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 2-EOP-FRTS-1, Response to Imminent Pressurized Thermal Shock Conditions. Maintain AFW flow &gt;22E4 lbm/hr until at least ONE intact SG NR level is &gt;15%, stop all ECCS pumps except 21 or 22 charging pump.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 2-EOP-FRTS-1, Response to Imminent Pressurized Thermal Shock Conditions. Isolate any faulted SGs, depressurize RCS with ONE PORV to within 100&deg;/hr Cooldown Curve.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 2-EOP-FRSM-2, Response to Loss of Core Shutdown. Energize Source Range channels and verify SR SUR is 0 or negative.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 2-EOP-FRSM-2, Response to Loss of Core Shutdown. Establish AFW flow &gt;44E4 lbm/hr, borate RCS until IR SUR is negative.</div>
</div>

<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> 55.43(5) A correct because the stem conditions result in a PURPLE path on FRTS. Actions for maintaining AFW flow (Step 3.5) and ECCS pump reduction (Step 12) are correct.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> B is incorrect because depressurizing the RCS to restore conditions within the 100&deg;F/hr curve is performed in FRTS-2 in response to a Yellow Priority Condition. FRTS-1 is entered from either RED or PURPLE conditions, and with SG NR levels &lt;9% (which means you're less than 15% adverse) you are directed to maintain AFW flow &gt; 22E4 lbm/hr.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C is incorrect because it is the wrong procedure with the correct actions of that procedure. With the SR NIs not energized, IR SUR is required to be more negative than -0.2 dpm, or a YELLOW path exists for FRSM. 2. The FRTS is a higher priority, and is a PURPLE path.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> D is incorrect because it is the wrong procedure, and the actions are performed in FRSM-1.</div>
