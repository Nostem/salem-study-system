<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
When performing a Source Check of a Process radiation monitor, which of the following describes how to initiate the Source Check, and why?<br><br>
Activate the Source Check……
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> for 30 seconds or less to prevent solenoids from overheating.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> just until indication of rising level is detected. This will prolong source life.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> for 30 seconds or less to prevent unwanted automatic action on high radiation level.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> just until indication of rising level is detected. This will ensure radiation level outside monitor remains less than detectable.</div>
</div>

Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(11) Both the system operating and surveillance procedures contain precautions limiting check source operation to 30 seconds or less.</div>
