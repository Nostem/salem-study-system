<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given the following conditions:</strong><br><br>
- Unit 2 is operating at 46% power steady state.<br>
- The Main Generator trips due to a ground fault.<br><br>
Which of the following describes how the Main Generator trip affects the plant with <strong><u>NO</u></strong> operator action?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> A reactor trip will NOT occur. Main steam dumps will open and control rods will insert. Tavg will stabilize ~547 °F.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> A reactor trip will NOT occur. Main steam dumps will open, and control rods will insert. Tavg will stabilize ~551 °F.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> A reactor trip will occur due to the Main Turbine tripping from the Generator Protection trip. The crew will perform TRIP-1 stabilization and diagnostics actions, then transition to TRIP-3.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> A reactor trip will occur due to the Main Turbine tripping from the Generator Protection trip. The crew will perform Immediate Actions of TRIP-1 then transition to TRIP-2.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(4,5,6,7) The generator trip causes a MT trip, but at less than P-9 (49% Rx power), a Reactor trip will NOT occur. With no operator action, rods will insert in auto to lower Tave. The steam dumps will open in Average Temperature Control Load Reject Control Mode. With the reference signal being derived from Main Turbine Steamline inlet pressure, and the Main Turbine is tripped, the reference signal is set to 551°F. (There is a 5°F deadband in which the output to the steam dumps is zero.) Tave rises during the load rejection, and the dumps will modulate to maintain Tave 5 deg above no load Tref of ~547 °F. Control rods insert in auto until Rod Control T-error is &lt;1.0°F from program, which in this case will be 547-548 since it's program signal is based on Turbine Steamline inlet pressure, which is zero.</div>
