<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given the following conditions:</strong><br><br>
- 2A EDG is running and develops a fuel oil leak.<br>
- 2A EDG Fuel Oil Day Tank level lowers below the start setpoint for both the REGULAR (33") and BACKUP (27") EDG Fuel Oil Transfer Pumps.<br>
- The leak is isolated, and 2A EDG Fuel Oil Day Tank level is recovering.<br><br>
With 2A EDG Fuel Oil Day Tank level now at 35", which of the following identifies which EDG Fuel Oil Transfer Pumps will be running, if any?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> NEITHER.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> BACKUP ONLY.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> REGULAR ONLY.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> BOTH REGULAR AND BACKUP.</div>
</div>

<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> 55.41(7) Regular starts at 33" and Backup at 27", as stated in stem. Once started, they BOTH run until the FULL level is reached (43"). The Backup does NOT turn off when the normal level is reached (33"). It is not require to know the exact setpoint at which the pumps turn off, only that it is substantially higher that when the Regular pump turns on, and that the backup pump will turn off at the same level as the Regular pump.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Source explanation does not separately address — see correct-answer block above (Regular and Backup both run until FULL level (43") is reached; at 35" both remain running).</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> Source explanation does not separately address — see correct-answer block above (Regular does not turn off at the normal/start level — it runs until FULL).</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> Source explanation does not separately address — see correct-answer block above (the backup pump will turn off at the same level as the Regular pump — not at 33").</div>
