<strong>Initial Conditions:</strong><br>
Salem Unit 2 is operating at 99% power, coasting down into a refueling outage which will start in 2 weeks.<br>
Irradiated fuel movement is in progress in the Spent Fuel Pool IAW S2.OP-IO.ZZ-0010, Spent Fuel Pool Manipulations, and will continue without interruption for the next 8 hours, at which time it will terminate.<br>
The unit has no active Tech Spec LCOs.<br>
21 SW pump is C/T for emergent strainer work.<br>
The current time is 0800.<br><br>
<strong>Initiating Cue:</strong><br>
You have been directed to review the upcoming work for the shift and determine which activities may proceed as scheduled while continuing the fuel movement in the Spent Fuel Pool uninterrupted.<br><br>
Work scheduled for today — circle whether each activity may or may not be performed; if any of the activities cannot proceed, explain your reason(s):<br>
- 0900 — 22 SW pump packing replacement (MAY / MAY NOT)<br>
- 1000 — 2A EDG tagout for maintenance (MAY / MAY NOT)<br>
- 1300 — 21 Chiller compressor oil change (MAY / MAY NOT)<br>
- 1400 — Delivery of New Fuel into the Fuel Handling Building for upcoming refueling outage (MAY / MAY NOT)

<div style="margin-top:12px;padding:10px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;">
<strong style="color:#22c55e;">Key Decision Point:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">The discriminating step is the New Fuel delivery determination. Three of the four scheduled activities are allowed by the relevant Tech Spec / IOP-10 P&amp;L combinations — the trap is that an SRO might assume "new fuel" delivery is independent of "irradiated fuel" movement and approve it. The blocking constraint is the <strong>FHB Truck Bay Door</strong> requirement on IOP-10 page 19: the door must be CLOSED while irradiated fuel movement is in progress to maintain FHB ventilation boundary integrity. Opening the door to receive new fuel would defeat the FHB filtration credit assumed in the radiological dose analysis. The other three activities each survive a "minimum equipment" check: 22 SW (two SW loops still operable), 2A EDG (TSAS 3.8.1.2 only requires two EDGs during fuel moves), 21 Chiller (TSAS 3.7.10 allows OOS once moves are in progress).</span>
</div>
