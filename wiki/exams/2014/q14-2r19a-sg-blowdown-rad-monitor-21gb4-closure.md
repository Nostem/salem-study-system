<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Which of the following describes when rising radiation levels on 2R19A, STM GEN BLOWDOWN RAD MONITOR, will automatically close the 21GB4, SG B/D OUTLET ISOL VALVE, and why?<br><br>
2R19A in <span class="blank">______</span> will close the 21GB4 <span class="blank">______</span>.
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Warning, to prevent the spread of contamination from a Steam Generator Tube Rupture (SGTR) on 21 Steam Generator to secondary systems.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Alarm, to prevent the spread of contamination from a Steam Generator Tube Rupture (SGTR) on 21 Steam Generator to secondary systems.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Warning, to prevent backfeeding contamination from 21 Steam Generator to any other Steam Generator through the unaffected Steam Generators blowdown lines.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Alarm, to prevent backfeeding contamination from 21 Steam Generator to any other Steam Generator through the unaffected Steam Generators blowdown lines.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> B is correct because isolating the blowdown path from the S/G to the condenser will prevent the spread of contamination, and also will prevent any type of release from the main condenser to atmosphere. The auto closure occurs upon an Alarm signal, not warning.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A is incorrect because the auto closure occurs upon an Alarm signal, not warning.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C is incorrect because the S/Gs each have its own blowdown line, so backfeeding contamination is not possible through the blowdown lines. (Also incorrect on the Warning signal — auto closure occurs upon an Alarm signal.)</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> D is incorrect because the S/Gs each have its own blowdown line, so backfeeding contamination is not possible through the blowdown lines.</div>
