<strong>Initial Conditions:</strong><br>
An ALERT has been declared at Salem due to the Unit 2 reactor failing to trip when a manual trip signal was initiated. The Rx tripped when the second trip handle was used.<br>
The unit is currently stable in MODE 3.<br>
The Emergency News Center (ENC) has not activated yet.<br><br>
<strong>Initiating Cue:</strong><br>
You are the Secondary Communicator. You are directed to perform EP-SA-111-F8, Attachment 8 Secondary Communicator Log.

<strong style="color:#22c55e;">Key Decision Point:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">Two discriminating elements. (1) Step A.4.b.3 — the applicant must select the correct unit (<strong>Salem 2</strong>) EROS Link Control and Status Display Screen, because the ALERT is on Unit 2; selecting Salem 1 is an incorrect action. (2) The <strong>media call</strong> — when an outside caller (CNN) requests plant status, the Secondary Communicator must <strong>refuse to release any information to the News Media</strong> per Attachment 8, Section C, Incoming Calls. Providing plant information to the caller fails the task. EROS is then terminated only when directed by the NRC.</span>
