<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q28 — Control Rod Withdrawal Effect on Plant Parameters</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">001000 K5.06 (RO 3.8 / SRO 4.1)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 1 is operating at 85% power steady state, MOL.<br>
- Rod control is in AUTO.<br>
- Control Bank D is at 185 steps.<br>
- Control rods begin withdrawing with no demand signal present.<br>
- Operators place rod control in MANUAL and rod motion stops.<br>
- A Rx trip is not generated, nor is one required by plant conditions.<br>
- Operators determine that Control Bank D rods have withdrawn a total of 10 steps.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies the effect of the rod motion?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Overpower Delta Temperature trip (OPDT) setpoint has risen.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Axial Flux Difference (AFD) has become less negative.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Quadrant Power Tilt Ratio (QPTR) has risen.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Shutdown margin has lowered.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> Salem normally operates with a negative AFD except for very late in core life. As rods move out, more power will be produced in the upper half of the core, and indicated AFD will become less negative.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A is incorrect because the OP/DT setpoint is not dependent on rod position.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> QPTR should be unaffected because the change in power will be seen on all planes equally.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> D is incorrect because SDM is not affected by rod position, since the rods are still trippable.</div>
