<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Salem Unit 1 is offline.<br>
- Salem Unit 2 operating at 95% power, 1150 Mwe, with its Power System Stabilizer (PSS) out of service.<br>
- Unit 2 Main Generator gas pressure is 75 psig.<br>
- Hope Creek is operating at 100% power, with its PSS out of service.<br>
- The Hope Creek 5-6 breaker is out of service.<br>
- A 500KV grid disturbance results in lower than normal grid voltage.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies Main Generator loading which is outside the allowable for Salem Unit 2 IAW A-5-500-EEE-1686, Artificial Island Operating Guide?<br><br>
Trip-A-Unit is NOT armed.<br><br>
Salem Unit 2 operating at <span class="blank">______</span> Mwe with MVAR loading <span class="blank">______</span> out.
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">

<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 1100, 525.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 1150, 225.</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> With Unit 1 O/S and the HC 5-6 breaker O/S, the correct curve is 2S2H-5-6 on page 291. With both Units PSS O/S, the red dashed line will be used for allowable generator excitation. A is incorrect because the PSS is O/S. If either units PSS was IN service, then it would be correct. The 2 distracters with higher MVARS are both within the limit. Since there are two different Mwe loading conditions, and the choices for each are high/low, the answer cannot be obtained by ruling out 2 of the choices because there would have to be 2 correct answers for them to be correct. Not a direct lookup because several different Figures are given.</div>
