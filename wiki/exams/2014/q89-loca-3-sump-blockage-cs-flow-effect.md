<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- A LOCA has occurred on Unit 2.<br>
- Operators are performing 2-EOP-LOCA-3, Transfer to Cold Leg Recirculation with all equipment and off site power available.<br>
- The crew has isolated the charging pump and SI pump suction from the RWST.<br>
- RWST level is 3.5' and lowering slowly when debris in containment clogs the containment sump, causing all pumps taking suction from it to begin cavitating severely.<br><br>
What affect will this have on Containment Spray flow, and how will the CRS proceed?<br><br>
Containment Spray flow will...
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> remain above 0 gpm due to one CS pump still taking suction from the RWST. GO TO EOP-LOCA-5, Loss of Emergency Coolant Recirculation, add makeup to the RWST, stop any safeguards pump which has lost its suction source.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> remain above 0 gpm due to one CS pump still taking suction from the RWST. GO TO EOP-APPX-7, Containment Sump Blockage Guideline, and stop all operating Charging, SI, and RHR pumps.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> lower to 0 gpm due to CS flow being supplied solely from RHR pump discharge. GO TO EOP-LOCA-6 and add makeup to the RWST, stop any safeguards pump which has lost its suction source.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> lower to 0 gpm due to CS flow being supplied solely from RHR pump discharge. GO TO EOP-APPX-7 and stop all operating Charging, SI, RHR, and CS pumps.</div>

<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> The correct transition from LOCA-3 when containment sump blockage is causing a loss of ECCS flow is to APPX-7. There is a CAS item in LOCA-3 to go to LOCA-5 if emergency recirculation cannot be established or maintained ONLY if the reason is OTHER than containment sump blockage. With RWST level above 1.2' as stated in stem, 22 CS pump will have been stopped at step 8. The stem states that charging and SI pump suction has been isolated, which occurs at step 14. The crew will be waiting at step 21 until RWST level lowers to 1.2' at which time they would stop the remaining CS pump. CS flow therefore remains above 0 gpm because one CS pump (21 CS) still takes suction from the RWST.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> The CS flow assessment is correct (CS flow remains above 0 gpm with one CS pump still on the RWST), but the transition is wrong. The CAS item in LOCA-3 directs LOCA-5 only if emergency recirculation cannot be established or maintained for a reason OTHER than containment sump blockage. Since the loss of flow here IS caused by sump blockage, the correct transition is to APPX-7, not LOCA-5.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> CS flow does NOT lower to 0 gpm — with RWST level still above 1.2', the 21 CS pump continues to take suction from the RWST, so CS flow remains above 0 gpm. EOP-LOCA-6 (LOCA Outside Containment) is not the procedure for a containment sump blockage; the correct transition is APPX-7.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> CS flow does NOT lower to 0 gpm — the 21 CS pump still takes suction from the RWST until RWST reaches 1.2'. The procedure transition (APPX-7) is correct, but APPX-7 directs stopping the operating Charging, SI, and RHR pumps; the remaining CS pump is not stopped until RWST reaches 1.2' at step 21.</div>
