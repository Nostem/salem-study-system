<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q100 — Short-Duration UE Corrected Before Notifications</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">194001 G2.4.29 (RO 3.1 / SRO 4.4)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 is at 100% power.<br>
- An Unusual Event is declared due to a loss of all overhead annunciators for greater than 15 minutes.<br>
- Prior to the Primary Communicator making any of the 15 minute notifications for an Unusual Event (UE), the problem is corrected and the annunciators are restored to operable.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies how the Emergency Coordinator should proceed?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Terminate making the 15 minute notifications and ensure the 1 hour After-the-Fact report is made.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Direct the Primary Communicator to make all required 15 minute notifications, then retract the UE IAW the proper attachments.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Direct the Primary Communicator to make all required 15 minute notifications, then terminate the UE IAW the proper attachments.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Terminate making the 15 minute notifications and ensure the 3 hour report for Major Loss of Emergency Assessment Capability is made.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> EP-SA-111-101 discusses Short Duration Events which are corrected before declaration. This requires declaration then termination, so the actual declaration in question above should be no less restrictive. "For some events, the condition may be corrected before a declaration has been made. The key consideration in this situation is to determine whether or not further plant damage occurred while the corrective actions were being taken. In some situations, this can be readily determined, in other situations further analyses (e.g. coolant radiochemistry sampling) may be necessary. Classify the event as indicated and terminate the emergency once assessment shows that there were no consequences from the event and other termination criteria are met." The Emergency Coordinator therefore directs the Primary Communicator to make all required 15 minute notifications, then terminates the UE IAW the proper attachments.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Incorrect — the required 15 minute notifications are still made (not terminated/skipped), because a declared UE requires declaration then termination, not no-less-restrictive treatment of the notifications.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> Incorrect — the all-required 15 minute notifications are made, but the UE is terminated (not retracted) IAW the proper attachments once assessment shows there were no consequences and other termination criteria are met.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> Incorrect — the required 15 minute notifications are still made (not terminated/skipped); a declared UE requires declaration then termination, not no-less-restrictive treatment of the notifications.</div>
