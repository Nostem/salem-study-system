<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> The CCW line supplying the RCPs is a single line supplying both bearing cooling and Thermal Barrier cooling. Once the line inside containment splits, the CCW from the Thermal Barriers has its own, separate return line, which is isolated by the 2CC190 (inside containment) and 2CC131 (outside containment.) The Thermal Barrier CCW flow acts to cool reactor coolant flowing upwards through the thermal barrier upon a loss of seal injection flow. With normal seal injection, the loss of CCW to the thermal barrier would not affect any RCP components.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Lower motor bearing temperatures will not rise: motor bearing cooling is supplied by the bearing-cooling branch of the CCW line, which is on the separate return path and is not isolated by 2CC190. With normal seal injection the loss of thermal-barrier CCW would not affect any RCP components.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> #1 seal leakoff temperatures will not rise: the Thermal Barrier CCW flow acts to cool reactor coolant flowing upwards through the thermal barrier only upon a loss of seal injection flow. With normal seal injection present, loss of thermal-barrier CCW does not affect RCP components.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> Motor winding temperatures will not rise: motor winding/air cooling is unaffected by isolation of the separate thermal-barrier return line via 2CC190. With normal seal injection, loss of CCW to the thermal barrier does not affect any RCP components.</div>
