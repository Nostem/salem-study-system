<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q73 — Approval to Issue a Very High Radiation Area Key</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">G2.3.13 (RO 3.4 / SRO 3.8)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Which of the following identifies who must approve issuing a key which will unlock a Very High Radiation Area?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Shift Radiation Protection Technician (SRPT) AND Radiation Protection Manager (RPM).</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> RPM AND Plant Manager / Shift Manager.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Shift Manager AND Plant Manager.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> SRPT AND Shift Manager.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> Attachment 1, High Radiation Area Key Approval Authority, of RP-AA-463 states that BOTH the RPM and Plant Manager/Shift Manager must approve issuing the key for a Very High Radiation Area.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Per Attachment 1, key approval for a Very High Radiation Area requires the RPM AND the Plant Manager/Shift Manager — the SRPT is not the second required approver (the SRPT level applies to lower area classifications).</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> Per Attachment 1, the RPM must be one of the two required approvers for a Very High Radiation Area key; Shift Manager AND Plant Manager omits the RPM.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> Per Attachment 1, the RPM (not the SRPT) is required along with the Plant Manager/Shift Manager for a Very High Radiation Area key; SRPT AND Shift Manager is not the required combination.</div>
