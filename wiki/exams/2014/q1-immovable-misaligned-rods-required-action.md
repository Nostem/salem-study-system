<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q1 — Immovable/Misaligned Rods Required Action</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">005 AA2.03 (RO 3.5 / SRO 4.4)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 is operating at 100% power.<br>
- A degraded stator water cooling system condition causes a Main Generator Stator Water runback to occur.<br>
- The runback terminates at 80% power when the initiating signal clears.<br>
- During the runback, the RO reported 2 control bank D rods have stopped moving at 215 steps.<br>
- The CRS entered S2.OP-AB.ROD-0001, Immovable / Misaligned Control Rods, and rod control is placed in manual.<br>
- Control Bank D Group Demand is 185 steps.<br>
- A Rx trip is not required.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies the minimum required action for how the crew will proceed?<br><br>
Assume the 2 inoperable control rods will NOT be restored to operable status, and the remaining control rods will NOT be realigned to the inoperable rods.
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Place the unit in Hot Standby.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Place the unit in Hot Shutdown.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Reduce power to &lt;50%, power operation at up to 50% may continue.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Reduce power to less than 75%, power operation at up to 75% may continue.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> With more than one rod misaligned, the crew is directed to place the Unit in Hot Standby at step 3.37 of AB.ROD-1. This is derived from TSAS 3.1.3.1 action b, which states that with more than one rod inoperable or misaligned from its associated group step counter demand position by more than 18 steps &lt;85% power, be in Hot Standby within 6 hours. The question is RO appropriate since it does not ask for the 6 hour time frame, rather it is asking for knowledge of a procedure step which requires HSB if 2 or more rods are affected.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> The required action for more than one misaligned rod is Hot Standby per AB.ROD-1 step 3.37 / TS 3.1.3.1 action b — not Hot Shutdown.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> The 50% distractor is plausible because &lt;50% power there are other rod-induced problems which do not apply, i.e., AFD and QPTR TS are only applicable &gt;50% power. It is not the required action for more than one misaligned rod.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> The 75% distractor is the action for a single inoperable rod, not for more than one inoperable/misaligned rod.</div>
