<strong>Estimated Time:</strong> 20 minutes<br><br>
<strong>Initial Conditions:</strong><br>
13 GDT is in holdup in preparation for performing a gas release. Chemistry has performed all required sampling and authorized the gas release. The release procedure, S1.OP-SO.WG-0010, Discharge of 13 Gas Decay Tank to Plant Vent has just been handed to you by the Unit 1 PO, who informs you the release is ready for CRS approval.<br><br>
<strong>Initiating Cue:</strong><br>
Review the entire S1.OP-SO.WG-0010 procedure for completeness, accuracy, and release approval. Approve the release or provide justification for NOT approving the release, and also note any discrepancies found in the procedure, in the comments section of the procedure.
</div>

<strong style="color:#34d399;">Task Standard:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">1. Review Gas Release and identify that:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;a. Pre-release verification has not been performed at step 5.2.8<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;b. Attachment 2, Step 4.1, second bullet, calculated Maximum Allowable Release rate from Section 3.4 has been entered as 32 SCFM instead of the correct value of 100 scfm.</span>

<strong style="color:#22c55e;">Key Decision Point:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">This is an alternate-path review JPM with <strong>two embedded errors</strong>, and the trap is the candidate who finds the first flaw and stops reviewing. The CRS must review the <strong>entire</strong> procedure and identify BOTH discrepancies before deciding on approval: (1) the <strong>pre-release valve position verification (step 5.2.8) was not performed</strong> before the package was given to the CRS; and (2) the Maximum Allowable Release rate calculated as <strong>100 scfm</strong> at Att. 2 step 3.4 was incorrectly transcribed as <strong>32 scfm</strong> at Att. 2 step 4.1. The candidate must withhold approval and record both discrepancies. Data in Section 3.0 of Attachment 2 (entered by Chemistry) is assumed correct.</span>
