<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Which of the following describes the bases for maintaining an OPERABLE Auxiliary Feedwater System in Modes 1-3 IAW Tech Spec 3.7.1.2?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Ensures the capability to cooldown and maintain the RCS at &lt;500 °F for 8 hours following a SGTR assuming failed fuel.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Remove decay heat and maintain the RCS at HSB conditions for 24 hours following a complete loss of off-site power.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Ensures that the RCS can be cooled down to &lt;350 deg F from normal conditions following a complete loss of off-site power.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Provide the RCS heat removal capability necessary to prevent a challenge to the pressurizer safety valves during a full power ATWT.</div>
</div>

<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> C is correct per the Tech Spec Bases. The TS 3.7.1.2 Bases document states the OPERABLE AFW System ensures that the RCS can be cooled down to less than 350 deg F from normal conditions following a complete loss of off-site power.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> The Tech Spec Bases document does not contain this reason. A is plausible because the RCS cooldown during a SGTR to &lt;500 degrees is to limit the potential off-site dose — but that is not the 3.7.1.2 bases reason.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> The Tech Spec Bases document does not contain this reason. B is plausible because the operability of the AFWST is for 8 hours maintaining HSB with steam discharge to the atmosphere — not 24 hours of decay heat removal at HSB as stated.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> The Tech Spec Bases document does not contain this reason. D is plausible because the ATWT bases document for AFW flow is for decay heat removal.</div>
