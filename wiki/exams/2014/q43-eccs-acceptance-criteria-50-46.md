<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Which of the following identifies a 10 CFR 50.46, Acceptance criteria for emergency core cooling systems for light-water nuclear power reactors, which may not be met if the ESF Actuation System fails to initiate ECCS components during a LOCA?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> The peak fuel element cladding temperature may not be maintained below 1800° F.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Reactor vessel water level may initially lower below the top of the active fuel.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Cladding oxidation may exceed 17% of the total clad thickness at any location in the core.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> The hydrogen generated from the Zirc-water reaction may exceed 10% of the hydrogen generated if all of the zirconium surrounding the fuel reacted.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> ECCS is a system which ESFAS actuates. 10CFR50.46 paragraph b delineates the Acceptance Criteria for a LOCA. Also Salem FSAR contains the same criteria copied from 10CFR50.46 in section 15.4.1.1. The maximum cladding oxidation criterion is that the calculated total oxidation of the cladding shall nowhere exceed 0.17 times (17% of) the total cladding thickness before oxidation — so an ECCS failure that allows oxidation to exceed 17% is a criterion that may not be met.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Choice A is incorrect because the fuel cladding temperature criteria is 2200°F (not 1800°F). The peak cladding temperature acceptance criterion is &lt; 2200°F.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> B is not correct because the Accident Analysis for the blowdown phase of a LBLOCA states that the top half of core is uncovered for lengthy period — the reactor vessel water level lowering below the top of the active fuel is an expected/analyzed condition, not a 50.46 acceptance criterion that fails to be met.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> The hydrogen generation acceptance criterion is that the calculated total hydrogen generated shall not exceed 1% (not 10%) of the hypothetical amount generated if all of the metal in the cladding cylinders surrounding the fuel were to react.</div>
