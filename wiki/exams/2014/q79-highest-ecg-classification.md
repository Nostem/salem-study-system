<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Which of the following sets of conditions would result in the highest (most severe) ECG classification?<br><br>
Use only the conditions currently present in each choice.
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> A leak in the SFP liner which results in lowering SFP level with all available SFP makeup in service.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> The Rx fails to trip when demanded, does not trip from the control room, and is tripped locally by opening the RTBs.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> An earthquake activates the Hope Creek Seismic Switch and is felt in the Control Room, and causes a trip of 2 of the 3 operating SW pumps.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> The Main Turbine has oversped during a functional test causing turbine blade disintegration, with reports of visible impact damage to the containment outside wall.</div>
</div>

<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> An ATWT in which all attempts to trip the Rx from the control room fail is a SAE (SS3.1). An ATWT in which the Rx can be tripped from the control room is an ALERT (SA3.1). This choice is the only SAE among the four — the others are all ALERTs — making it the highest (most severe) classification.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Lowering SFP level with all available makeup will result in an ALERT under RA2.2, which can only be escalated by means of a different RAL, probably radiation levels rising. RA2.2 says "lowering SFP level that will result in irradiated fuel becoming uncovered."</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> An earthquake as described above is an ALERT (HA1.1).</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> The turbine becoming a missile and causing visible damage to containment is ALERT (HA1.2). This happened at Salem, and an ALERT was declared, even though at the time it was only a UE. Salem operators should be familiar with this.</div>
