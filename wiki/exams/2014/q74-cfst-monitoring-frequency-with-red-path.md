<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following:<br><br>
- Unit 2 has tripped and experienced a loss of all AFW<br>
- The crew has just transitioned from step 20.1 of EOP-TRIP-1 to EOP-FRHS-1, Response to Loss of Secondary Heat Sink.<br>
- The STA has just completed his first pass through the CFST's and reports the following Status Tree conditions:<br><br>
Shutdown Margin - GREEN<br>
Core Cooling - GREEN<br>
Heat Sink - RED<br>
Thermal Shock - GREEN<br>
Containment Environment - GREEN<br>
Coolant Inventory - GREEN<br><br>
Assuming conditions do not change, what is the required monitoring frequency for the CFST's IAW OP-AA-101-111-1003, Use of Procedures?<br><br>
CFST monitoring...

<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> must be continuous.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> is required every 30 minutes.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> is required every 10-20 minutes.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> may be suspended with CRS concurrence until FHRS-1 is completed.</div>

<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> Step 4.2.11, page 22 of OP-AA-101-111-1003, USE OF PROCEDURES, requires continuous monitoring of CFST's when there is any RED or PURPLE path indicated. The STA is not required to be in the control room area at all times. In cases where an event occurred and the STA was not readily available to monitor CFSTs, it would fall to the Plant Operator (Licensed RO) to initiate and conduct CFSTs. For example, a situation where a Rx trip occurred, and operators transitioned out of TRIP-1 to TRIP-2 based on no SI would mean CEST's were in effect ~3 minutes after the Rx trip. Then RO's are required to perform CEST's and monitor as required. An example would be a condition which results in no SI but Heat Sink RED path occurs shortly after entering TRIP-2.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> 30 minutes is not the required CFST monitoring frequency; with a RED (or PURPLE) path indicated, monitoring must be continuous per step 4.2.11.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> 10-20 minutes is the allowed frequency only when no condition more serious than YELLOW exists; the Heat Sink RED path here requires continuous monitoring.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> CFST monitoring may not be suspended; with a RED path indicated, continuous monitoring is required and cannot be deferred until FRHS-1 is completed.</div>
