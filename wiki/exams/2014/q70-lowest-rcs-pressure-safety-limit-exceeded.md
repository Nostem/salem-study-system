<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q70 — Lowest RCS Pressure Exceeding a Safety Limit and Restoration Time</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">2.2.22 (RO 4.0 / SRO 4.7)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 has experienced an inadvertent Safety Injection, which resulted in a Reactor Trip.<br>
- Following the trip, the Pressurizer becomes water solid, numerous failures occur, and RCS pressure rises.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies the LOWEST RCS pressure at which a Tech Spec Safety Limit is exceeded, and the required time allowed for pressure to be reduced below that value IAW Salem Tech Specs?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 2440 psig, 5 minutes.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 2440 psig, 1 hour.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 2735 psig, 5 minutes.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 2735 psig, 1 hour.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> Tech Spec 2.1, Safety Limits, for Mode 3 (which is the mode in after a Rx trip) 2.1.2, states RCS pressure shall not exceed 2735 psig, and allows 5 minutes to restore.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> 2440 psig is the top line of figure 2.1-1 — it is not the RCS pressure Safety Limit (2735 psig). The 5-minute restoration time is correct for Mode 3, but the pressure value is wrong.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> 2440 psig is the top line of figure 2.1-1 (not the RCS pressure Safety Limit of 2735 psig), and the 1-hour time frame is for Modes 1 or 2, not Mode 3.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> 2735 psig is the correct RCS pressure Safety Limit, but the 1-hour time frame is if in Modes 1 or 2; in Mode 3 (after a Rx trip) the required time is 5 minutes.</div>
