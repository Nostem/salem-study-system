<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q36 — 1PR1 PORV Opens and Cannot Be Shut — Effect and AB.PZR Actions</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">007000 A2.01 (RO 3.9 / SRO 4.2)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
With Unit 1 operating at 100% power, 1PR1 opens in automatic with no demand to open, and cannot be shut.<br><br>
Which of the following describes the effect of this failure, and how the actions the crew should perform IAW S1.OP-AB.PZR-0001, Pressurizer Pressure Malfunction will affect this event?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> The PRT rupture disk will rupture when pressure reaches 10 psig if the 1PR6 Block Valve is not shut.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> The PRT rupture disk will rupture when pressure reaches 100 psig if the 1PR6 Block Valve is not shut.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> If PZR heaters cannot restore pressure, the Rx will be manually tripped before an auto trip is generated on OT/DT at 2100 psig.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> If PZR heaters cannot restore pressure, the Rx will be manually tripped before an auto trip is generated on low PZR pressure at 1985 psig.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> AB.PZR directs closing the PORV block valve if the PORV cannot be shut. If it is not shut the PRT rupture disk will rupture at 100 psig. 10 psig is the high pressure alarm setpoint. There are steps in AB.PZR for operating PZR heaters, but for a PORV failure the heaters will be unable to maintain PZR pressure.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> 10 psig is the high pressure alarm setpoint, not the rupture-disk setpoint; the PRT rupture disk ruptures at 100 psig if the 1PR6 Block Valve is not shut.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> The 2 trip setpoints are incorrect. The OT/DT trip setpoint is not a psig value, but its equivalent value is ~ 2,000 psig (actual Salem data on actual Rx trip), not 2100 psig.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> The 2 trip setpoints are incorrect. The low PZR pressure Rx trip is at 1865 psig, not 1985 psig.</div>
