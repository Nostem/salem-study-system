<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">033000A2.01 (RO 3.0 / SRO 3.5)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Which of the following addresses items 1 and 2 below:<br><br>
1. A reason for maintaining Unit 2 Cycle 21 SFP boron concentration at 2127 ppm or greater in Mode 6 IAW Salem Unit 2 COLR<br>
2. The preferred water source to establish or restore that boron concentration level if less than required IAW S2.OP-SO.SF-0001, Fill and Transfer of the Spent Fuel Pool.
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Ensures SFP boron concentration is always &gt; RCS and Refueling Cavity boron concentration. CVCS Holdup Tanks.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Ensures SFP boron concentration is always &gt; RCS and Refueling Cavity boron concentration. Refueling Water Storage Tank</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Ensures Keff of 0.95 or less at All Rods In, Cold Zero Power conditions with a 1% delta k / k uncertainty added. CVCS Holdup Tanks.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Ensures Keff of 0.95 or less at All Rods In, Cold Zero Power conditions with a 1% delta k / k uncertainty added. Refueling Water Storage Tank.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> Salem COLR, page 9, Section 2.6 states the 3 criteria for maintaining 2127 ppm, which ensures the most restrictive of those 3 is met. One of those is the Keff &lt; 0.95 as listed in choices c and d. The CVCS Holdup Tanks are the preferred makeup source to raise SFP boron concentration.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A is incorrect because while the boron concentration is MAY be higher than that in RCS, it is not the reason for the 2127 limit. (Also the preferred source for raising SFP boron is CVCS Holdup Tanks, not as the basis given.)</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> B is incorrect because while the boron concentration MAY be higher than that in RCS, it is not the reason for the 2127 limit. (And Demineralized water — not the RWST — is the normal makeup; the preferred source to RAISE boron is the CVCS Holdup Tanks.)</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> D is incorrect but plausible because Demineralized water is the preferred makeup source to the SFP under normal circumstances, but would NOT be used to raise boron concentration because it has no boron in it. (The basis Keff &lt; 0.95 is correct, but the source — RWST — is wrong; the CVCS Holdup Tanks are the preferred boron makeup source.)</div>
