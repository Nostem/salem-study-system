<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q82 — Condition Requiring Termination of GDT Release</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">G2.1.32 (RO 3.8 / SRO 4)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Unit 1 is releasing 11 Gas Decay Tank (GDT) IAW S1.OP-SO.WG-0008, Discharge of 11 Gas Decay Tank to Plant Vent.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies a condition which would require termination of the release, and why?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> The Auxiliary Building pressure turns positive. This could result in an unmonitored release of radiation from the Aux Building atmosphere.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> A transfer of waste gas from 13 GDT to 14 GDT is performed. This would result in the actual release rate exceeding the 32 scfm maximum allowed.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> A valve lineup to place 12 GDT in holdup is performed. This would result in an unmonitored release of radiation from the tank being put in holdup into the normal tank release path.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> The pressure downstream of 1WG38, Gas Decay Tank Vent Pressure Control Valve, is 7.5 psig once the gas release has stabilized following the initial start. This could result in the actual release rate exceeding the 32 scfm maximum allowed.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> A is correct per P&amp;L 3.9, and TSAS 3.7.7 action e, which requires negative aux building pressure or suspend all operations involving radioactive gaseous release via the aux building immediately.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> B is incorrect because the reason is wrong. Transfer of Waste Gas between tanks would result in an unapproved release, as the transfer path puts gas in the release header. The WG8 would react to maintain pressure &lt;8.0 psig which is what maintains the release rate &lt;32 scfm.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C is incorrect because aligning a tank to holdup isolates the tank from input and output, and would not put any of its contents into the release path.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> D is incorrect because the downstream pressure &lt;8.0 psig maintains release rate &lt; max allowed of 32 scfm.</div>
