<strong>Initial Conditions:</strong><br>
A severe accident has occurred at Salem Unit 2. You have declared a 13 point General Emergency in the Control Room at 1535 hrs for loss of all three fission product barriers under EAL's FB3.L, RB2.L, and CB.1.L.<br>
The weather conditions are as follows:<br>
• Wind direction is from 050 degrees at 15 mph.<br>
• Overcast skies with rain showers.<br>
• Ambient temp = 62°F<br>
• 2R41D is reading 2.5 E3 uCi/sec<br><br>
<strong>Initiating Cue:</strong><br>
Continue performing the provided Salem ECG Attachment 4, General Emergency. I am the Primary Communicator. This is a time critical JPM.

<div style="margin-top:12px;padding:10px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;">
<strong style="color:#22c55e;">Key Decision Point:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">Two discriminating determinations under a <strong>15-minute time-critical clock</strong>. (1) <strong>Correct PAR</strong>: with wind FROM 050° (NE) and a 13-point General Emergency, the Predetermined PAR Flowchart requires Evacuate ALL sectors 0-5 miles, Evacuate the downwind +/- 1 sector group <strong>(SSW, SW, WSW) 5-10 miles</strong>, and Shelter all remaining sectors 5-10 miles. The candidate must read the Recommended Protective Action Worksheet correctly for the 034-056° (NE) row to get SSW-SW-WSW. (2) <strong>No radiological release</strong>: 2R41D reads 2.5E3 uCi/sec, which is below the Federal Limit of 2.42E+05 uCi/sec Noble Gas, so the candidate must check "NO RADIOLOGICAL RELEASE IS IN PROGRESS" on the ICMF. The ICMF must be completed within 15 minutes of the EAL assessment clock. Wrong downwind sectors, checking a release in progress, or exceeding 15 minutes fails the task.</span>
