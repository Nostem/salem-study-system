<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q34 — Small-Break LOCA With 1A Vital Bus Lockout — Containment Pressure Response</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">006 A4.01 (RO 4.3 / SRO 4.3)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 1 is operating at 100% power.<br>
- 11 CFCU is C/T.<br>
- 1C EDG is paralleled to 1C 4KV Vital Bus for monthly run.<br>
- A 1" line connected to RCS loop 11 shears off.<br>
- Operators initiate a Rx trip and SI.<br>
- 1A 4KV Vital bus locks out on Bus Differential.<br><br>
Which of the following describes the difference in containment pressure response between this LOCA, and one with the same conditions except 1C EDG was initially aligned for normal standby operation, and why?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> the same since all required pumps will be running.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> higher since only ONE Containment Spray pump will be operating.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> higher since NEITHER Containment Spray pump will be operating.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> lower because ECCS will inject faster due to the EDG already being running.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> A is correct since a 1" break will be within the capability of the two high pressure injection pumps (11 and 12 CVCS pps) to prevent a major lowering of PZR pressure. The loss of 1A vital bus would affect 11 RHR, 11 CS, and 13 Charging pp, (and 11 CFCU which is C/T) none of which would be injecting for ECCS or for containment pressure control, since the small size of the leak would not cause RCS pressure to drop to their shutoff heads, or containment pressure to rise for CS requirement.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> Containment pressure would not be higher because of one CS pump: the small (1") break would not raise containment pressure to the CS requirement, so neither containment spray pump is needed in either case, and the response is the same.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> Containment pressure would not be higher because neither CS pump is operating: the 1" break does not raise containment pressure to the CS actuation requirement, so CS is not needed and the containment response is the same regardless of CS availability.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> Containment pressure would not be lower because the 1C EDG was already running: the two high-pressure injection pumps (11 and 12 CVCS pumps) handle the small break in both cases, and RCS pressure does not drop to the shutoff heads of the pumps lost on 1A bus, so a faster-available EDG makes no difference.</div>
