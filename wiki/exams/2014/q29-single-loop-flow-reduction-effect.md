<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q29 — Single RC Loop Flow Reduction Effect on the Primary Plant</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">002000 A3.03 (RO 4.4 / SRO 4.6)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 1 is operating at 100% power.<br>
- Reactor Coolant flow measurements determine that 11 RC loop has experienced a 5% reduction in flow from its expected 100% flow.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies how this 5% flow reduction in 11 loop has affected the primary plant in relation to the previous 100% flow conditions?<br><br>
Assume the 3 other loop flows remain the same.
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Delta T in the 11 RCS loop will be lower.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Steam pressure in 11 SG will be higher.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> The reactor core will be operating closer to DNB.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Demand on the Pressurizer variable heaters at 2235 psig will be lower.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> A lower single loop flow will cause total flow through the core to lower. Using Q=mc(D/T) if mass flow rate lowers, then the D/T has to go up if power remains the same, which it will due to MT gov valve reaction. This will cause the core to be operating closer to DNB.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Delta T in the 11 RCS loop will be HIGHER, not lower: with total core flow reduced and power held constant by MT gov valve reaction, Q=mc(ΔT) requires ΔT to rise as mass flow rate falls.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> Steam pressure in 11 SG would not be higher: power (heat transferred to the secondary) is unchanged, so SG steam pressure is not raised by the loop flow reduction.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> Pressurizer variable heater demand is not lowered by the loop flow reduction; the effect of the flow reduction is to raise core ΔT and move the core closer to DNB, not to reduce heater demand.</div>
