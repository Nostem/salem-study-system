<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 is operating at 100% power.<br>
- B 4KV Vital bus Undervoltage (UV) testing is in progress.<br>
- A problem results in the "B" SEC loading 2B 4KV bus in the II* Mode.<br><br>
With NO operator action, choose the conditions below which will be observed 3 minutes following the SEC actuation.<br><br>
I. Rx power &gt;100%<br>
II. 22 RHR Pp running<br>
III. Steam dump demand signal of 100%<br>
IV. 22 CC Pp running<br>
V. 22 CS Pp running<br>
VI. MTLO outlet temp rising<br>
VII. SGBD flows = 0 gpm<br>
VIII. 22 CCHX outlet temp rising
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> 22 RHR Pp (II), 22 CS Pp (V), and 22 CCHX outlet temp rising (VIII) are not the expected conditions; 2SW26 closes causing MTLO cooling water to lower to none, so MTLO outlet temp rising (VI) is valid but the set is otherwise incorrect.</div>
