<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q85 — Subsequent Action During MSLB Depressurization of All SGs</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">WE12 EA2.01 (SRO 4.0)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 has experienced a MSLB at the Main Turbine inlet steam piping.<br>
- All attempts at Main Steamline Isolation have failed.<br>
- Operators have just transitioned out of EOP-TRIP-1.<br>
- 22 and 23 AFW pump are in service.<br>
- RCS pressure is 1300 psig and dropping.<br>
- Charging system SI flowmeter indicates 280 gpm.<br>
- RCS cooldown rate is 105°/hr.<br>
- The RCS cooldown is NOT being controlled.<br><br>
Which choice identifies a subsequent action that must be performed, and why?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Isolate AFW in LOSC-2, Multiple Steam Generator Depressurization, to any SG with NR level >9% to minimize cooldown while still ensuring the SG tubes remain wet.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Reset SGBD Sample Isolation in LOSC-2, Multiple Steam Generator Depressurization, to allow sampling of SGs and transition to SGTR sorter if required.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Trip all RCP's in LOSC-1, Loss of Secondary Coolant, to prevent seal package damage if RCS pressure continues to lower.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Trip 23 AFW pump in LOSC-1, Loss of Secondary Coolant, as part of isolating all steam flow paths from faulted SGs.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> B is correct because it is performed at step 10, and if a SGTR were identified here, the CRS would transition to SGTR-1 and not stay in LOSC-2.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A is incorrect because LOSC-2 step 5 states to maintain no less than 1.9E4 lbm/hr to each SG. The CAS 3 which talks about SGs with NR levels >9% does not apply to a CD > 100°/hr. That CAS would allow isolating AFW to any SG if the CD rate was less than 100°/hr, and all SG NR levels are >33%.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C is incorrect because RCPs are tripped less than 1350 psi, ONLY when a CD is NOT in progress.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> Tripping 23 AFW pump is not the directed subsequent action; AFW flow of no less than 1.9E4 lbm/hr must be maintained to each SG per LOSC-2 step 5, and the SGBD Sample Isolation reset (step 10) is the action that must be performed here.</div>
