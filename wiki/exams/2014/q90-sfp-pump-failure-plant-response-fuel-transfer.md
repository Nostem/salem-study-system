<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">033 A2.02 (RO 3.8 / SRO 3.2)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 is 7 days into a refueling outage on June 15th.<br>
- The core is partially offloaded with 7 bundles remaining in the Rx.<br>
- Spent Fuel Pool (SFP) temperature is 124&deg;F.<br>
- SFP level is 5" above normal.<br>
- 21 SFP trips on motor OL, and can NOT be restarted.<br>
- 22 SFP pump is placed in service with SFP temperature at 138&deg;F.<br><br>
Which of the following contains both an expected plant response to this failure, and a condition which must be met prior to transferring the remaining fuel bundles into the spent fuel pool?<br><br>
The SFP...
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> High level OHA annunciates. 21 SFP cooling pump must be restore to operable status.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> High Level OHA annunciates. The 22 SFP must have normal and emergency power supply availability verified.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Demineralizer automatically bypassed flow when 22 SFP was placed in service and demin inlet temp reached 136&deg;F. 21 SFP cooling pump must be restore to operable status.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Demineralizer automatically bypassed flow when 22 SFP was placed in service and demin inlet temp reached 136&deg;F. The 22 SFP must have normal and emergency power supply availability verified.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> OHA C-27 SFP Lvl Hi will occur at 6" above normal, and would be expected to occur with a pool heatup of 14 degrees, as noted in the CAUTION in AB.SF on top of page 2. (Pool level is 5" above normal at 124&deg;F; the ~14&deg; heatup to 138&deg;F raises level past the 6" High Level point.) Per IOP-10 (Spent Fuel Pool Manipulations) P&amp;L 3.4, transfer of spent fuel into the SFP is to be suspended until BOTH SFP pumps are OPERABLE — so the 21 SFP cooling pump must be restored to operable status before the remaining fuel bundles can be transferred.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> The expected plant response (High Level OHA annunciates) is correct, but the fuel-transfer condition is wrong. IOP-10 P&amp;L 3.4 requires BOTH SFP pumps OPERABLE — that is satisfied by restoring the failed 21 SFP pump, not by verifying normal and emergency power supply availability of the in-service 22 SFP pump.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> The SFP demineralizer does NOT auto bypass. This distractor is plausible because the CVCS demineralizer auto-bypasses at 136&deg;F, but the SFP demin would have been MANUALLY removed from service IAW AB.SF CAS item 2.0 when actual or projected SFP temperature is 130&deg;F.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> Both elements are wrong: the SFP demin does not auto bypass (it is manually removed IAW AB.SF CAS 2.0 at 130&deg;F — the 136&deg;F auto-bypass belongs to the CVCS demin), and the fuel-transfer condition per IOP-10 P&amp;L 3.4 is that BOTH SFP pumps be OPERABLE, not verification of 22 SFP power supply availability.</div>
