<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q84 — Procedure Transition from LOCA-6 for Unisolable Break</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">00WE04EA2.2 (SRO 4.2)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- A SBLOCA has occurred on Unit 2 outside containment.<br>
- Actions of EOP-LOCA-6, LOCA OUTSIDE CONTAINMENT, have failed to isolate the break.<br>
- RCS pressure is 1440 psig and continues to lower.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies the procedure that will be used upon transition from EOP-LOCA-6 and the actions that will be directed in that procedure after the transition?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> EOP-LOCA-5, Loss of Emergency Coolant Recirculation. Check for subsequent failure and conserve makeup inventory.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> EOP-LOCA-1, Loss of Reactor Coolant. Check for subsequent failure and conserve makeup inventory.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> EOP-LOCA-1. Add makeup to RWST, initiate a cooldown, and minimize injection flow.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> EOP-LOCA-5. Add makeup to RWST, initiate a cooldown, and minimize injection flow.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> D is correct, because an unisolable break will transition out to LOCA-5. The actions directed in LOCA-5 are to add makeup to RWST, initiate a cooldown, and minimize injection flow. The SRO is required to understand the actions of the LOCA-6 procedure to identify the appropriate procedure to transition to when it is complete, and the actions performed in that procedure.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> The procedure (LOCA-5) is correct, but the actions are wrong — LOCA-5 directs adding makeup to RWST, initiating a cooldown, and minimizing injection flow, not "check for subsequent failure and conserve makeup inventory."</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> LOCA-1 is incorrect because it would be the appropriate transition if the break were isolated; the break here is unisolable, so the transition is to LOCA-5. The actions are also wrong.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> LOCA-1 is incorrect because it would be the appropriate transition if the break were isolated; the break here is unisolable, so the transition is to LOCA-5 (even though the actions listed match LOCA-5).</div>
