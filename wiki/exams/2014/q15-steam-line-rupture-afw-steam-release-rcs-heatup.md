<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
During an event in which a steamline rupture cause ALL SG's to blow down completely, why is AFW flow and steam release used to prevent the RCS from heating up?<br><br>
RCS heatup will...
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> result in a larger Delta-T between the AFW injection flow and the internal temperature of the SG J-tubes, which can cause water hammer in the feed ring when incoming AFW flashes to steam.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> cause PZR level to rise, which will repressurize the RCS. The severe cooling of the RPV downcomer combined with the pressure rise can cause a flaw in the vessel to propagate threatening the integrity of the vessel.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> result in a larger Delta-T between the ECCS injection water from the RWST and the RCS cold leg injection points, which can cause a flaw at the ECCS to cold leg piping weld to propagate threatening the integrity of the RCS.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> cause PZR level to rise which will repressurize the RCS. The thermal stress on the SG secondary side components from excessive heat transfer during the blowdown, combined with the pressure rise across the SG tubes can cause tubesheet deformation and leakage.</div>
</div>
