<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">WE03 EK1.02 (RO 3.6 / SRO 4.1)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- A Small Break LOCA has occurred.<br>
- The crew is performing the actions of EOP-LOCA-2, POST LOCA COOLDOWN AND DEPRESSURIZATION.<br>
- All SI pumps are running.<br>
- All RCPs are running.<br>
- RCS cooldown via Main Steam Dumps is ongoing.<br>
- RCS Tave is 510°F and lowering at a rate of 90°F/Hr.<br>
- PZR level indicates 26% and rising.<br>
- The RCS depressurization has been secured and RCS pressure is 1310 psig and stable.<br><br>
Which of the following describes the next major action to be implemented in the EOP to mitigate the current conditions?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Stop ALL RCPs due to pressure &lt; 1350 psig and ECCS flow established.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Stop the cooldown. Energize all PZR heaters to collapse voids and stabilize PZR level.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Stop all but one RCP and begin the SI flow reduction process by stopping ECCS pumps.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Recommence the RCS depressurization using normal spray to collapse voids and refill the PZR.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> The idea for depressurization is to refill the pressurizer. Since the pressurizer is already filled (&gt;25%), go directly to flow reduction. There will be no voids if RCPs are running, and there is no CAS transition to TRIP-3.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> The next major action is to stop all but one RCP and begin SI flow reduction — not to stop ALL RCPs.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> There will be no voids if RCPs are running, so energizing all PZR heaters to collapse voids is not the action — the pressurizer is already refilled (&gt;25%) and the procedure proceeds to flow reduction.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> Since the pressurizer is already filled (&gt;25%), the idea of depressurization (to refill the PZR) is already satisfied; the procedure goes directly to flow reduction rather than recommencing depressurization.</div>
