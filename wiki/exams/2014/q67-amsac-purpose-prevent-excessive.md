<strong>Given:</strong><br>
The purpose of the ATWS Mitigation System Actuation Circuitry (AMSAC) is to prevent excessive ___________ should the reactor trip breakers fail to open on demand.
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> feed flow</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> reactor power</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> RCS pressure</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> SG tube differential pressure</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> Per the AMSAC Lesson Plan (NOS05AMSAC0-02), page 11, and Salem FSAR Section 7.8: AMSAC trips the turbine and actuates auxiliary feedwater to prevent excessive RCS pressure should the reactor trip breakers fail to open on demand (an ATWS). Maintaining the secondary heat sink with AFW and removing the heat-input mismatch by tripping the turbine limits the RCS overpressurization that would otherwise occur during an anticipated transient without scram.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> feed flow — AMSAC actuates auxiliary feedwater (it increases AFW flow); it does not prevent excessive feed flow. The protected parameter is RCS pressure.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> reactor power — AMSAC does not insert rods or directly reduce reactor power (it cannot trip the reactor). Its function is to limit the RCS pressure consequence of the failure to scram by tripping the turbine and starting AFW. (This choice replaced an implausible distractor per the question source comment.)</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> SG tube differential pressure — Not the design purpose of AMSAC. SG tube differential pressure is not the parameter AMSAC protects against during an ATWS.</div>
