<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q98 — Containment Entry Power Limit and Authorization</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">G2.3.12 (RO 3.2 / SRO 3.7)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 is performing a Tech Spec required shutdown from 100% power IAW Tech Spec 3.0.3., at 20% / hr.<br>
- Current Rx power is 85%.<br>
- A containment entry is being planned in an effort correct the condition which has caused 3.0.3 entry.<br>
- The personnel entering containment will be going ONLY to the 78' elevation outside the bioshield.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies the Rx power limitation, if any, IAW SC.SA-ST.ZZ-0001, Salem Containment Entries in Modes 1-4, which must be met for this containment entry, AND a Salem position who is REQUIRED to authorize this containment entry?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> No power limitation. Radiation Protection Supervisor.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Rx power &lt;50%. Radiation Protection Supervisor.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> No power limitation. Operations Manager.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Rx power &lt;50%. Operations Manager.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> Normal Containment entries at power are governed by SC.SA-ST.ZZ-0001 and RP-SA-102. Authorization to access containment is provided by the SM/CRS/Designee. However, when power is being changed &gt;5%/hr (here, 20%/hr), the Radiation Protection Supervisor (RPS) approval is required. Since the SM/CRS/Designee is not one of the available choices, the only correct answer is the RPS. Additionally, there is no power limitation associated with containment entries.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> The authorizer (Radiation Protection Supervisor) is correct, but there is no Rx power &lt;50% limitation associated with containment entries.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> There is no power limitation (this part is correct), but the Operations Manager is not the required authorizer. Since the stem asked whose specific approval is required, the Operations Manager is not correct, even though he/she signs the approval of all ops procedures.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> Both elements are incorrect: there is no Rx power &lt;50% limitation, and the Operations Manager is not the required authorizer (the RPS approval is what is required for a &gt;5%/hr power change). The RPM approves access to certain areas of containment, but the stem states they are not going into those.</div>
