<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q13 — Fuel Handling Incident Evacuation Condition</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">036 AK1.01 (RO 3.5 / SRO 4.1)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
During movement of irradiated fuel in the Spent Fuel Pit with the Rx in Mode 1, a Spent Fuel Assembly is not fully withdrawn from its rack before the Spent Fuel Crane is moved. The Spent Fuel Assembly is visibly damaged when the crane moves.<br><br>
Which of the following conditions would require ALL personnel to evacuate the Fuel Handling Building IAW S2.OP-AB.FUEL-0001, Fuel Handling Incidents?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Radiation level in the FHB reaches 1 R/hr.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Bubbles coming from the damaged fuel assembly.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Fuel Handling Crane motion locked out upon reaching Radiation Monitor 2R32A alarm setpoint.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Automatic re-alignment of the Fuel Handling Building exhaust filter train to HEPA plus Charcoal has occurred.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> AB.FUEL-1 CAS 1.0 states to evacuate at 1 R/hr.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> Bubbles coming from the fuel assembly may be present, but are not cause for evacuation until the radiation from them, or any other cause, reaches 1 R/hr.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> The R32A distracter is plausible if it is thought that because the crane can't be moved everyone should evacuate. This is not the evacuation criterion — evacuation is at 1 R/hr.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> Ventilation realignment is expected to occur either on high local rad level or manual actuation, but is not the evacuation criterion. (Not LCO 1 — AB.FUEL-2, Loss of Refueling Cavity or Spent Fuel Pool Level, does not evacuate the FHB until 2 R/hr.)</div>
