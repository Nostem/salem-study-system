<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 has experienced a reactor trip from 100% power.<br>
- 22 AFP Pressure Override Protection circuit has malfunctioned, causing the AF21's (Auxiliary Feedwater Isolation Valve) supplied from this pump to remain shut.<br><br>
With NO operator action, choose the indications which would be present 2 minutes after the reactor trip.
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> AFW flow indication reading 0 gpm for 21 and 22 SGs.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> AFW flow indication reading 0 gpm for 23 and 24 SGs.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 21 and 22 SG levels rising slower than 23 and 24 SG levels.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 23 and 24 SG levels rising slower than 21 and 22 SG levels.</div>
</div>
