<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q93 — EDG Fuel Oil Transfer Pump Tech Spec Entry and Bases</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">064000 G2.2.22 (RO 4.0 / SRO 4.7)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 is in Mode 3, NOP, NOT.<br>
- 2A EDG is being run for a monthly surveillance, and is currently loaded to 2550 KW.<br>
- 21 CCW pump trips.<br>
- 5 minutes after the CCW pump trip, the field NEO reports the breaker for 21 Diesel Fuel Oil Transfer Pump is tripped.<br><br>
Which of the following describes the Tech Spec entry required and its Bases?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Enter Tech Spec 3.8.1.1.b.2 for EDG Fuel Oil Modes 1-4 since BOTH Fuel Oil Transfer pumps are required to be operable. This ensures that sufficient power would be available for the safe shutdown of the plant and for the mitigation and control of accident conditions.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Enter Tech Spec 3.0.3 because all redundant equipment in the CCW system is not available, making both loops of CCW inoperable. This ensures that sufficient safety related CCW would be available for the safe shutdown of the plant and for the mitigation and control of accident conditions.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Enter Tech Spec 3.8.1.1.b.2 for EDG Fuel Oil Modes 1-4 since BOTH Fuel Oil Transfer pumps are required to be operable. This is to ensure a timely unit shutdown is performed when the plant operation cannot be maintained within the limits of safe operation defined by the Limiting Conditions for Operation and its action requirements.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Enter Tech Spec 3.0.3 because all redundant equipment in the CCW system is not available, making both loops of CCW inoperable. This is to ensure a timely unit shutdown is performed when the plant operation cannot be maintained within the limits of safe operation defined by the Limiting Conditions for Operation and its action requirements.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> A is correct because it is gleaned from the TS bases section for electric power sources. With the 21 Diesel Fuel Oil Transfer Pump breaker tripped, BOTH fuel oil transfer pumps are required to be OPERABLE in Modes 1-4 per TS 3.8.1.1.b.2, so that Tech Spec is entered. The correct bases is from the electric power sources (EDG) bases — OPERABILITY of the AC electrical power sources ensures sufficient power is available for the safe shutdown of the plant and for the mitigation and control of accident conditions.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> The bases in choice b is from the AC power sources (CCW) — the tripped 21 CCW pump does not make both loops of CCW inoperable, and the controlling Tech Spec for the tripped fuel oil transfer pump breaker is TS 3.8.1.1.b.2, not TS 3.0.3.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C is incorrect because even though the Tech Spec (3.8.1.1.b.2) is correct, the reason given is the 3.0.3 bases — the timely-unit-shutdown wording is the LCO 3.0.3 bases, not the electric power sources bases.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> D is incorrect because both the Tech Spec and the bases are incorrect — the controlling Tech Spec is 3.8.1.1.b.2 (not 3.0.3), and the timely-unit-shutdown wording is the LCO 3.0.3 bases.</div>
