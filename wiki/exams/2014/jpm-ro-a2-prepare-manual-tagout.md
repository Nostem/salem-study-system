<strong>Initial Conditions:</strong><br>
• Unit 1 is operating at 100% power.<br>
• While performing a surveillance on 11 Safety Injection Pump, a leak was observed on the pump outlet flange discharge piping.<br>
The Work Clearance Module is not available due to an emergent power outage.<br><br>
<strong>Initiating Cue:</strong><br>
You are directed to perform the following:<br>
1. Determine the correct blocking points which will allow repair of the 11 SI pump.<br>
2. Sequence those blocking points in the correct order.<br>
3. Determine the correct tag type for each blocking point.<br>
4. Determine the required positions necessary to allow repair on 11 SI pump.<br>
5. Enter all the above information on the provided OP-AA-109-115, Safety Tagging Operations Form 4.
</div>

<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">1. Identifies correct blocking points.<br>2. Sequence tagging in order by 1: Bezels, 2: Electrical, 3: Mechanical isolation, 4: Vents and drains.<br>3. Identifies correct tag types for components.<br>4. Identifies desired positions.</span>

<div style="margin-top:12px;padding:10px;border-left:3px solid #60a5fa;background:rgba(96, 165, 250, 0.06);border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;">
<strong style="color:#60a5fa;">Tagging Rules (OP-AA-109-115, Attachment 2):</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">A minimum of 1 vent or drain is required to be opened, with no limit to how many can be used inside the boundary (this is why all vents and drains share sequence #16). MOVs are allowed as Blocking Points: 11SJ113 AND 12SJ113 may be used instead of the single manual valve 1SJ114 — if so, the 11/12SJ113 breakers (RBT-OFF), bezels (INFO), and valve handwheels (RBT-SHUT) must also be tagged. The electrical power to a component must be cleared and tagged before that component's manual operator is tagged, but may be sequenced within the tagout after other manual valves have been tagged.</span>
</div>

<div style="margin-top:12px;padding:10px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;">
<strong style="color:#22c55e;">Key Decision Point:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">The discriminating element is sequencing the blocking points in the correct order — <strong>1. Bezels → 2. Electrical Isolation → 3. Mechanical Isolation → 4. Vents and Drains</strong> — and assigning the correct tag type and desired position to each (Bezels = INFO; electrical = RBT OFF/D1; isolation valves = RBT SHUT; vents and drains = RBT OPEN). Several provided drawings (205234 Sheets 1-4, 203002, 207910-207912, 207931-207933) are NOT required; the applicant must select only the components on the 11 SI pump boundary. The applicant must also establish at least one vent and one drain inside the boundary.</span>
</div>
