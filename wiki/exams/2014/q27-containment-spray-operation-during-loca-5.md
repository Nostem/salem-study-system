<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q27 — Containment Spray Operation During LOCA-5 With Rising Containment Pressure</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">00WE11 EA2.1 (RO 3.4 / SRO 4.2)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions on Unit 2:<br><br>
- A LBLOCA has occurred.<br>
- Operators are performing 2-EOP-LOCA-5, Loss of Emergency Recirculation.<br>
- Containment pressure is 15.1 psig and is rising slowly.<br><br>
Which of the following describes how the Containment Spray system will be operated, and why?<br><br>
The Containment Spray System is operated as directed in...
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 2-EOP-FRCE-1, Response to Excessive Containment Pressure, since restoration of the critical safety function takes precedence.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> LOCA-5 because it establishes minimum required containment spray flow and conserves RWST inventory.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 2-EOP-FRCE-1 because actions concerning Containment Spray operation are more restrictive.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> LOCA-5 since FRPs are NOT implemented during the performance of LOCA-5.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> Upon entering FRCE-1, step 3.1 asks if LOCA-5 is in effect. The yes path states that CS pumps are to be operated IAW LOCA-5. The basis document states that this is because in FRCE, maximum available heat removal system operability is warranted to reduce containment pressure, whereas in LOCA-5 a less restrictive criteria permits reduced spray pump operation depending on RWST level, containment pressure, and # of CFCU's operating. The less restrictive criteria in LOCA-5 is used because recirculation flow to the RCS is not available, and it is very important to conserve RWST water, if possible, by stopping containment spray pumps. So while the operator WILL enter FRCE-1 due to PURPLE path of containment pressure &gt; 15 psig, the containment spray pumps will be operated IAW LOCA-5.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Although the operator WILL enter FRCE-1 due to the PURPLE path of containment pressure &gt; 15 psig, FRCE-1 step 3.1 directs that with LOCA-5 in effect the CS pumps are operated IAW LOCA-5 — not by FRCE-1 criteria.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> The FRCE Containment Spray actions are the MORE restrictive (maximum available heat removal warranted), but FRCE-1 step 3.1 yes-path specifically defers to LOCA-5; the less restrictive LOCA-5 criteria is used because recirculation flow to the RCS is not available and RWST water must be conserved.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> CS is operated IAW LOCA-5, but the reason given is wrong: the operator WILL enter FRCE-1 (an FRP) on the PURPLE path of containment pressure &gt; 15 psig — FRPs ARE implemented during LOCA-5. FRCE-1 step 3.1 then directs CS operation per LOCA-5.</div>
