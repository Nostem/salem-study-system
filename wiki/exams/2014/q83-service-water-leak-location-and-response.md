<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 is operating at 100% power.<br>
- Operators receive the following alarms:<br>
- OHA B-13 21 SW HDR PRESS LO<br>
- OHA B-14 22 SW HDR PRESS LO<br>
- SW header pressure indication in the control room reads 98 psig for both headers.<br><br>
No other OHA's have annunciated.<br><br>
Which of the following describes both the possible location of a Service Water System leak which would cause these indication, and how the CRS should respond?<br><br>
Assume each of the leaks is large enough to cause the indications present in the control room.
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 4 Service Water Bay. Split SW Bays by closing 21SW17 and 22SW17 IAW S2.OP-AB.SW-0003, Service Water Bay Leak.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Nuclear header x-over line between the 21SW23 and the 22SW23. Shut EITHER SW23 using Attachment 6, Service Water Valve Malfunctions, of S2.OP-AB.SW-0001.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 2B EDG Lube Oil Cooler. Isolate BOTH SW supply header isolation valves and BOTH return header isolation valves to 2B EDG IAW S2.OP-AB.SW-0001 Loss of Service Water Header Pressure.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 21 CCW HX end bell. Ensure 22 CC HX controller set lower than 21 CCW HX, and isolate 21 Service Water Header using Attachment 4, Service Water Header Isolation, of S2.OP-AB.SW-0001.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct (keyed — question DELETED).</strong> The TGA SW header pressure is maintained at 80 psig by 2ST1. The low pressure alarm (which is not present) is 70 psig. In order for a leak to be large enough to cause both SW Nuc headers to lower to 99.5%, the TGA low pressure alarm would be in also. The EDG's each have 2 supply isolation valves, one from each header, and 2 return header isolation valves, going to 11 or 12 discharge path.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A leak in the Service Water Bay would also cause the bay sump high level alarm, as well as the TGA lo pressure alarm — neither of which is present.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> The cross over line is normally isolated by the shut SW23's, so a leak there would not lower both nuclear header pressures as described.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> 21 ccw hx CAN BE MANUALLY ISOLATED WITH MANUAL VALVES, AND DOES NOT REQUIRE ISOLATING THE WHOLE NUC HEADER.</div>
