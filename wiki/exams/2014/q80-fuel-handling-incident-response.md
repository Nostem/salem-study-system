<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 is in Mode 6, with refueling actively in progress.<br>
- Emergency core position P-10 is full.<br>
- The fuel transfer cart is at the Spent Fuel Pool.<br>
- The manipulator crane has an irradiated fuel assembly in the mast, and is near the lower internals stand, moving toward the core.<br>
- Gas bubbles are observed in the vicinity of the fuel assembly last placed in the core, which had experienced difficulties getting placed into the vessel.<br>
- The refueling SRO orders all non-essential personnel to evacuate containment.<br><br>
Which of the following describes how the CRS should respond IAW S2.OP-AB.FUEL-0001, Fuel Handling Incident?<br><br>
Place the fuel assembly in the mast into...
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> the first available position, send the Fuel Transfer Cart to containment, shut the fuel transfer canal gate valve.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> the first available position, shut the fuel transfer canal gate valve, start at least one Iodine Removal Unit.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> its designated core position, shut the fuel transfer canal gate valve, start at least one Iodine Removal Unit.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> its designated core position, send the Fuel Transfer Cart to containment, shut the fuel transfer canal gate valve.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> With a fuel assembly in the mast tube, it is placed into its position in the core. WHEN AVAILABLE, it could be placed in emergency position P-10, but the stem states that position P-10 is not available (because it may have already been filled during reload). Fuel loading considerations preclude putting it willy-nilly into any available position. The Fuel transfer cart must be sent to containment before the gate valve is shut.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Fuel loading considerations preclude putting the assembly willy-nilly into "the first available position" — it is placed in its designated core position (P-10 emergency position is full).</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> Wrong position ("first available") and the IRU is not started unless specifically requested by Rad Pro when iodine is present in containment, and no indication of that condition is presented in the stem.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> The IRU is not started unless specifically requested by Rad Pro when iodine is present in containment, and no indication of that condition is presented in the stem.</div>
