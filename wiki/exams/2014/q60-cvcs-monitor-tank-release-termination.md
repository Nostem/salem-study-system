<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Unit 2 is releasing 22 CVCS Monitor Tank through the cross-connect line to Unit 1 SW, then to Unit 2 CW system.<br><br>
If a high radiation condition occurs, how will the release will be terminated?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 1WL115 Waste Discharge Hdr x-conn valve will be manually shut.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 2WL115 Waste Discharge Hdr x-conn valve will automatically shut.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 1WL51 Liquid Radwaste Overboard Stop Valve will automatically shut.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 2WL51 Liquid Radwaste Overboard Stop Valve will automatically shut.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> The unit initiating the release will have the flow through its own R18 radiation monitor, and it will auto close on high radiation. Use of the cross connect line does not put flow through the opposite units R18 rad monitor, nor will its isolation valve 1WL51 be opened or in the flowpath.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> The 1WL115 is a normal locked shut manual valve.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> The 2WL115 is a remotely operated valve but does not have an auto close function.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> Use of the cross connect line does not put flow through the opposite units R18 rad monitor, nor will its isolation valve 1WL51 be opened or in the flowpath.</div>
