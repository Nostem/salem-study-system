<div style="margin-top:12px;padding:10px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;">
<strong style="color:#22c55e;">Key Decision Point:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">This is an <strong>alternate-path</strong> JPM. The applicant first performs a routine liquid radwaste release: open 2WL51 (release stop valve), throttle open 2WR59 (MT pumps overboard stop valve) toward the 150 gpm max release rate, and — because full flow cannot be reached — throttle closed the recirc valve 21WR53 to raise discharge flow to 55 gpm (Step 5.5.6). The discriminating event is at <strong>Step 5.5.9</strong>: during the release the <strong>2R18 monitor ALARMS with the RED HI RAD light lit (~10⁵ counts)</strong>. The applicant must recognize this as one of the listed release-termination conditions and immediately <strong>direct the NCO to CLOSE 2WL51</strong> to terminate the release, then notify the control room of the high-radiation condition. Continuing the release after the 2R18 high-radiation alarm — rather than promptly terminating it by shutting the liquid release stop valve — fails the task.</span>
</div>
