<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q9 — CCW Leak Component Identification</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">026 AA1.02 (RO 3.5 / SRO 3.5)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 is at operating normally at 100% power.<br>
- A Component Cooling Water leak results in entry into S2.OP-AB.CC-0001, Component Cooling Abnormality.<br>
- Make-up can maintain CC surge tank level &gt; 38%<br>
- The crew has implemented ATTACHMENT 4, Leak Isolation Method.<br>
- When make-up is stopped, surge tank level lowers with either CC header in service.<br><br>
Of the following, which is the only component that could be leaking to cause these indications?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 22 CCW HX.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Spent Fuel Pool HX.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 23 Charging pump mechanical seal HX.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Boric Acid Evaporator Distillate Cooler HX.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> With the stem stating that the leak continues with EITHER CC header in service, that means the leak must be on the Non-Safeguards header, which is supplied from both CC headers. Of the 4 choices, 2 are on the safeguards header, and 2 are not. Of the 2 possible answers, the Boric Acid Distillate Cooler HX is not normally in service. That leaves the SFP HX, and SF cooling pressure is &lt; CCW pressure, meaning the leak would be out of the CCW system. (This question is different from Question 5 in that it requires knowledge of system operation during performance of the AB to locate the leak, whereas Question 5 is a Thermal Barrier question.)</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> The 22 CCW HX is on the safeguards header; a leak there would not continue with either header in service.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> The 23 Charging pump mechanical seal HX is on the safeguards header; a leak there would not persist with either header in service.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> The Boric Acid Evaporator Distillate Cooler HX is on the Non-Safeguards header but is not normally in service, so it cannot be the leaking component.</div>
