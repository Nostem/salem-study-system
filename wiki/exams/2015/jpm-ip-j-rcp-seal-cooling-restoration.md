<div style="margin-top:12px;padding:10px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;">
<strong style="color:#22c55e;">Key Decision Point:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">All five valve closures are critical, but the discriminating elements are <strong>CV116 (SEAL WATER TO VCT)</strong> and <strong>CC131 (RCP THERMAL BARRIER VALVE)</strong> located in the 78 ft Mech Pen Area SG B/D HX Area inside a <strong>roped-off (radiologically controlled) area</strong>. CV116 also requires a different technique — it is a motor-operated valve that must be closed locally by <strong>depressing and holding the motor operator declutch lever</strong> before turning the handwheel, whereas CV83/CV89/CV95 (Seal Water Injection Filter Valve Room, 84 ft Aux Bldg) are simple clockwise handwheel closures. During a station blackout (loss of all AC), seal injection and the thermal barrier CCW are lost, so seal cooling must be locally isolated per EOP-LOPA-1 Step 27 to prevent backflow/diversion paths. Failing to isolate any of the five valves — or failing to recognize the declutch requirement / contamination controls for the roped-off valves — fails the task.</span>
</div>
