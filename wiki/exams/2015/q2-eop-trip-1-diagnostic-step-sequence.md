<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Which of the following describes the reason for the sequence of diagnostic steps in EOP-TRIP-1 Reactor Trip or Safety Injection?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> SGTR diagnosis has the higher priority because of the highest probability of radioactive release.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> SGTR diagnosis has the higher priority to minimize the potential for component failure due to water in the Main Steam lines.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Main Steam Line break diagnosis has the higher priority because a high energy steam break could potentially mask other failures.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Main Steam Line break diagnosis has the higher priority because AFW must be isolated to remain within accident analysis assumptions for containment pressure.</div>
</div>
