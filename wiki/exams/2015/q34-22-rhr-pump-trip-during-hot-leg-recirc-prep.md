<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q34 — Effect of a 22 RHR Pump Trip During Hot Leg Recirc Prep (EOP-LOCA-1 Step 26)</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">005000 K3.05 (3.7/3.8)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; Unit 2 has experienced a Large Break LOCA.<br>
&bull; 2-EOP-LOCA-3, Transfer to Cold Leg Recirculation, is complete with NO abnormalities encountered.<br>
&bull; Operators are currently at step 26, "Preparation for Hot Leg Recirc", of 2-EOP-LOCA-1, Loss of Reactor Coolant.<br>
&bull; Off-site power is supplying all 4KV Vital busses.<br><br>
If BOTH RHR pumps are operating, what would be the effect if 22 RHR Pp were to trip?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 21 and 22 SI pumps would begin to cavitate.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 22 Containment Spray Pump would lose NPSH.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 21 and 22 charging pumps would begin to cavitate.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Flow to the Containment Spray header would be lost.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer & Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> <em>(10 CFR 55.41.b(5))</em> D is the correct answer. LOCA-3 explicitly states that if BOTH RHR pumps are operating, then 22CS36 is opened to supply containment spray from 22 RHR pp discharge.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Distracter A is incorrect because the closure of the RH19's is done to prevent RHR pump runout if only a single RHR pump is operating, so the SI pumps will not lose suction.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> Distracter B is incorrect because all containment spray pumps are stopped in LOCA-3, which has been completed as stated in stem.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> Distracter C is incorrect charging pumps, as well as the SI pumps, will not lose suction.</div>
