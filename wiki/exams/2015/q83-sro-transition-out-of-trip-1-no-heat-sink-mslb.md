<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q83 (SRO 8) — Procedure and Action Transitioning Out of EOP-TRIP-1 With No Heat Sink (MSLB/SI)</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">WE05 EA2.02 (3.7/4.3)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; Unit 1 has experienced a Main Steamline Break (MSLB) inside containment from 100% power.<br>
&bull; Main Steam Line Isolation has failed to close any MS167.<br>
&bull; Safety Injection was manually initiated, with all components operating as expected.<br>
&bull; 11 AFP is C/T.<br>
&bull; 12 and 13 AFP's tripped after starting.<br>
&bull; Reactor Coolant System pressure is 1100 psig.<br>
&bull; All Reactor Coolant Pumps have been tripped.<br>
&bull; Containment pressure is 16 psig and rising.<br>
&bull; All Wide Range (WR) Steam Generator (SG) levels are 35% and dropping.<br>
&bull; All Steam Generator pressures are 465 psig and dropping.<br>
&bull; Reactor Coolant System Tc's have dropped from 540 to 438°F in 40 minutes.<br><br>
If these condition were present when transitioning out of 1-EOP-TRIP-1, Reactor Trip Response, which procedure must be entered and what action must be taken upon that transition?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 1-EOP-FRTS-1, Response to Imminent Pressurized Thermal Shock Conditions. Reset Safeguards Actuation and restore normal charging and letdown.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 1-EOP-FRHS-1, Response to Loss of Secondary Heat Sink. Initiate feed and bleed ONLY when 3/4 SG WR levels have dropped &lt;32%.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 1-EOP-FRTS-1. Response to Imminent Pressurized Thermal Shock Conditions. Shut all MS10's and steam dump valves.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 1-EOP-FRHS-1, Response to Loss of Secondary Heat Sink. Initiate feed and bleed immediately.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.43(5) 55.43.b(5) This question is SRO level based on having to asses the conditions given in stem and determine both what procedure will be entered and the action(s) required in that procedure. D is correct because the conditions given in stem would transition to FRHS-1 due to a RED path of no AFW flow and &lt;9% NR level. The Bleed and Feed initiation criteria are when S/G WR levels are &lt;36%(adverse), NOT 32% (normal) as in distracter B. Distracters A &amp; C are incorrect because it is a lower priority RED path, though it's action is correct for the procedure.</div>
