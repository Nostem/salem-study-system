<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(7,8,10) H2 recombiners are placed in service when directed in the EOPs with containment H2 concentration between 2-4%. The hydrogen recombiners use electric heating elements to elevate the temperature of the containment atmosphere. As shown in Attachment 2, RECOMBINER POWER CORRECTION FACTOR CURVE, a higher containment pressure would result in a higher power correction factor, which would cause recombiner power setpoint to rise. So initially the higher pressure would cause the recombiner to become less efficient, and the power setpoint would be raised as directed at Step 5.1.9 C which says if H2 has risen or remained constant and Containemnt pressure has changed, perform attachment 1 and adjust power.</div>
