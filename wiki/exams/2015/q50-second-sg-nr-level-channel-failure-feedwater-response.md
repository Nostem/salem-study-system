<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(7) SG NR level is programmed from 33-44% up to 100% power. As the downpower continues, the BF19 will be closing in response to the lower steam flow requiring less feed. The 11BF19 and 11BF40 (expected to be shut at this power level) swap to manual upon the second NR level channel failure. The SGFPs do not. When the 11BF19 swaps to manual, it will have a certain demand on it. As power (and steam flow) continues to lower, the demand will be higher than required, and SG NR level will become higher than program level.</div>
