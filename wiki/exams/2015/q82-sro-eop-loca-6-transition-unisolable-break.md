<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q82 (SRO 7) — Procedure Transition and Actions from EOP-LOCA-6 With an Unisolable Break</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">WE04 EA2.01 (3.4/4.3)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; A SBLOCA has occurred on Unit 2 outside containment.<br>
&bull; Actions of EOP-LOCA-6, LOCA OUTSIDE CONTAINMENT, have failed to isolate the break.<br>
&bull; RCS pressure is 1440 psig and continues to lower.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies the procedure that will be used upon transition from EOP-LOCA-6 and the actions that will be directed in that procedure after the transition?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> EOP-LOCA-1 Loss of Reactor Coolant. Add makeup to RWST, initiate a cooldown, and minimize injection flow.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> EOP-LOCA-5 Loss of Emergency Coolant Recirculation. Add makeup to RWST, initiate a cooldown, and minimize injection flow.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> EOP-LOCA-1, Loss of Reactor Coolant. Check for subsequent failure and conserve makeup inventory.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> EOP-LOCA-5, Loss of Emergency Coolant Recirculation. Check for subsequent failure and conserve makeup inventory.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.43.b(5) This question is SRO level based on having to assess facility conditions, select the correct operating procedure, and know specific actions taken in that procedure. B is correct, because an unisolable break will transition out to LOCA-5. LOCA-1 is incorrect because it would be the appropriate transition if the break were isolated. 43.5 because the SRO is required to understand the actions of the LOCA-6 procedure to identify the appropriate procedure to transition to when it is complete, and actions performed in that procedure.</div>
