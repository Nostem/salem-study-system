<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">075 A2.02 (RO 3.9 / SRO 3.4)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; Unit 2 is operating at 60% power, steady state.<br>
&bull; 21A Circulator is C/T.<br>
&bull; The Condensate Polisher is O/S and in standby.<br>
&bull; Subsequently, the 13 KV South ringbus breaker D-E opens causing a loss of 23 SPT.<br><br>
Which of the following describes:<br>
1) The plant status 15 minutes after the loss of 23 SPT<br>
2) How the operators will respond to this event
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 1) The reactor remains at power. 2) Operators will be performing S2.OP-AB.CW-0001, Circulating Water System Malfunction, and establishing Low Pressure Turbine Hood Spray.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 1) The reactor was manually tripped. 2) Operators will be utilizing S2.OP-AB.COND-0001, Loss of Condenser Vacuum, in conjunction with the TRIP series of EOP's, to break condenser vacuum.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 1) The reactor remains at power. 2) Operators will be performing S2.OP-AB.CHEM-0001, Abnormal Secondary Chemistry, and placing the Condensate Polisher in service for the expected rise in Dissolved O2.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 1) The reactor was manually tripped. 2) Operators will be utilizing S2.OP-AB.CN-0001, Main Feedwater / Condensate System Abnormality, in conjunction with the TRIP series of EOP's to respond to the loss of condenser hotwell levels and possible Condensate pump cavitation.</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.43.b(5) This question is SRO level based on knowing the appropriate procedure or section of procedure used in applying the conditions which will occur based on the initial conditions in the stem. 23 SPT powers 23 CW bus, and 24 SPT powers 24 CW bus. 23 CW bus powers the "A" circulatros and 24 SPT powers the "B" circulators. The loss of 23 SPT will cause a loss of 2 additional circulators in addition to 21A which is C/T. The operator must know how many circulators are now not running, 3 or 4. If it is thought 4 are O/S there is a CAS to trip the Rx &gt;P-9 (49% power). Additionally, the 2 Rx trip distracters AB's may be used if it is thought condenser vacuum would degrade or DO increased based on losing hotwell levels and its effect on condensate pump seals. Manually bypassing the Turbine Hood Spray is allowed (and directed) in AB.CW-1, even though normally it remains secured &gt;15% power. (AB.CW-1 page 5)</div>
