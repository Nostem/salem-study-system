<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q94 (SRO 19) — Action Required for a Predicted Critical Rod Height vs ECC During a Startup</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">G2.1.23 (RO 4.3 / SRO 4.4)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; Unit 2 is performing a reactor startup by control rods IAW S2.OP-IO.ZZ-0003, Hot Standby to Minimum Load.<br>
&bull; Estimated Critical Conditions are:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&ndash; Cb= 500 ppm<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&ndash; Control Bank D = 77 steps<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&ndash; Xe free<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;&ndash; 11,756 EFPH<br>
When the ICRR value reaches 0.125, the Predicted Critical Rod Height is 122 steps on Control Bank D.<br><br>
What action(s), if any, is/are required to be taken in response to this Predicted Critical Rod Height?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Continue with no special actions are required.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Continue the reactor startup, and evaluate the post startup data for trend.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Initiate rapid boration, insert Control Rod Banks, and recalculate the ECC.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Insert the Control Rod Banks and recalculate the ECC prior to withdrawing Control Rods.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.43.b(6) Table 1-8 77 steps is 1079.4 pcm. 122 steps is 877.0 difference is 202.4 pcm. With &lt;300 pcm difference between ECC and predicted at the eightfold position, there is no action, and the startup will continue with no additional action required.</div>
