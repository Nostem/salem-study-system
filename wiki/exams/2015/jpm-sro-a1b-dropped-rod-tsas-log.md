<strong>Estimated Time:</strong> 20 minutes<br><br>
<strong>Initial Conditions:</strong><br>
1. Unit 2 is at EOL in a 300 day run at 100% power.<br>
2. Control Rod 2SA4 dropped fully into the core at 1015, the reactor did not trip.<br>
3. The crew has implemented S2.OP-AB.ROD-0002, Dropped Rod.<br>
4. Control Rod 2SA4 has been declared inoperable, and Reactor Engineering reports it will be at least into the next shift before they determine how to recover the rod.<br>
5. The 3rd NCO has just completed a QPTR at 1025. The highest reading quadrant is 1.031.<br>
6. Computerized LCO tracking is not being used.<br><br>
<strong>Initiating Cue:</strong><br>
As CRS, determine <strong>ALL</strong> TSAS(s) that apply and make the appropriate log entries on OP-SA-108-115-1001, Operability Assessment and Equipment Control Program, Attachment 4-1, and Form 1, Section 1.0. Creation or documentation of notifications is not required.

<div style="margin-top:12px;padding:10px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;">
<strong style="color:#22c55e;">Key Decision Point:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">The discriminating step is the LCO determination — the applicant must recognize that a single dropped/inoperable rod at 100% power simultaneously trips <strong>three</strong> Technical Specification Action Statements, not just one: <strong>3.1.3.4.b</strong> (Rod Drop Time / inoperable rod), <strong>3.1.3.1.c.3</strong> (Movable Control Assemblies — rod inoperable), and <strong>3.2.4.a</strong> (QPTR — the 1.031 quadrant reading exceeds the 1.02 QPTR limit). All three carry a 1-hour action time. On Form 1 the SRO records the most limiting Action Time, and notes the inoperability will span beyond one shift (so the condition cannot be cleared on shift). Missing any one of the three TSAS — particularly the QPTR entry driven by the 1.031 reading — fails the task.</span>
</div>
