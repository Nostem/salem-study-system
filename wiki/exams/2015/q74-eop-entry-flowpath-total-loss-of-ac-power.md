<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q74 — Correct EOP Entry and Flowpath on a Total Loss of AC Power (OP-AA-101-111-1003)</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">194001 2.4.1 (4.6/4.8)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; Salem Unit 1 is operating at 100% power.<br>
&bull; 1A EDG is C/T for maintenance.<br>
&bull; A 500KV switchyard malfunction causes a loss of offsite power, and 1B and 1C EDGs do not load due to 1B and 1C 4KV vital buses locking out on Bus Differential.<br><br>
Of the following, which one states a procedure entry and its correct flowpath allowed by the rules of procedure IAW OP-AA-101-111-1003 Use of Procedures?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Enter EOP-LOPA-1 directly and perform immediate actions, which states to trip the Rx then trip the Turbine.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Enter EOP-LOPA-1 directly and perform immediate actions, which states to trip the Rx and confirm the Rx trip, then trip the Turbine.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Enter EOP-TRIP-1 and perform immediate actions, which states to trip the Rx and confirm the Rx trip, trip the Turbine, initiate SI, then transition to EOP-LOPA-1 based on no vital buses energized.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Enter EOP-TRIP-1 and perform immediate actions, which states to trip the Rx and confirm the Rx trip, trip the Turbine, initiate SI ONLY if conditions warrant, then transition to EOP-LOPA-1 based on no vital buses energized.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(10) Either TRIP-1 or LOPA-1 can be entered upon a total loss of all AC power. If entered, the flowpath for TRIP-1 does not reach the SI evaluation step before the kickout to LOPA-1 on no 4kv vital buses energized, so both TRIP-1 distracters are incorrect. LOPA-1 does not confirm the Rx trip (since there is no power to do anything about it anyways).</div>
