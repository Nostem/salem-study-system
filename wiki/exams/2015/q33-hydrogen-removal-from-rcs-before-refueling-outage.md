<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Which of the following methods is used prior to cooldown to remove hydrogen from the RCS after a unit shutdown to enter a refueling outage, and why is hydrogen removed?<br><br>
<span class="blank">_(1)_</span> degassification is used to remove hydrogen from the RCS to prevent <span class="blank">_(2)_</span>.

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> <em>(10 CFR 55.41.b(3,5))</em> Both chemical (addition) and mechanical (replacement of H2 with N2) degasses are performed during a shutdown/cooldown. Mechanical is most effective at NOP/NOT (see P&amp;L 2.1.5).</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A is incorrect because chemical degass is performed when RCS temp is less than 250 (see pre-req 3.1.7).</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> B is incorrect because of wrong reason for mechanical degas.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C is incorrect because chemical degass is performed to initiate crud burst (addition of H2O2).</div>
