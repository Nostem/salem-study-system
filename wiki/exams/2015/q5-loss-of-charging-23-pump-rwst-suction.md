<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q5 — Loss of Charging: 23 Charging Pump Aligned to RWST</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">022 AK3.02 (RO 3.7 / SRO 3.7)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
&bull; Unit 2 is operating at 100% power.<br>
&bull; 21 charging pump is in service.<br>
&bull; 22 charging pump is CIT.<br>
&bull; Subsequently, 21 charging pump trips.<br>
&bull; 23 charging pump is placed in service after its suction is aligned to the RWST.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies a subsequent action which will be directed by S2.OP-AB.CVC-0001, Loss of Charging, and why?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Shutdown Unit 2 due to boration of the RCS from the RWST.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Restore normal letdown to establish adequate VCT level for normal 23 charging pump operation.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Place Excess Letdown in service to establish adequate VCT level for normal 23 charging pump operation.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Open 2CV464 Charging Cross-Tie Isolation valve to prevent requiring a Unit 2 shutdown due to boration of the RCS from the RWST.</div>
</div>

<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(10) When the inservice charging pump stopped, letdown automatically isolated with all charging pump breakers open. If 23 charging pump is placed in service from the RWST, it means it was not able to be placed in service with VCT as suction source on step 3.7. The note prior to placing 23 charging pump in service from step 1 of RWST states that a unit S/D will be required because of borating the RCS from the RWST.</div>
