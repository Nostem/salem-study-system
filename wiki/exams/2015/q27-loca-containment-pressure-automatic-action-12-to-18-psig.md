<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(7) Phase A isolation occurs on any SI signal, which at the very least would have occurred at 4 psig in containment if not sooner. Feedwater Isolation occurs on any SI or SG NR level &gt;67%. Main steamline Isolation occurs at 15 psig. Containment Ven isolation occurs any SI, RMS alarm of associated monitors, Phase B. The SI signal would have already actuated.</div>
