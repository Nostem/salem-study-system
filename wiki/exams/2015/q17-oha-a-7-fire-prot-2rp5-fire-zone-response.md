<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q17 — Correct Response When Assessing Affected Fire Zone(s) on 2RP5 (OHA A-7 FIRE PROT FIRE)</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">000067 AK3.02 (2.5/3.3)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Both Salem Units are operating normally at 100% power when Unit 2 receives OHA A-7 FIRE PROT FIRE.<br><br>
Which of the following is a correct response when assessing the affected Fire Zone(s) on 2RP5, and why?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> If ANY rows "Fire" light is illuminated ensure at least one Fire Pump is running to supply fire protection water by verifying OHA A-15 FIRE PUMP 1/2 RUN.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> If a fire is indicated in the Relay Room select Fire Outside Control Area in both control rooms to prevent smoke from entering control rooms.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> If ANY CO2 / Halon Discharge lights are lit then ensure EDG control room ventilation has automatically stopped to prevent egress of gas to adjoining areas.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> If fire indication for BOTH zones 59 and 74 are received, then open 2FP147 Fire Protection Containment Isolation to provide normally isolated fire protection water to containment.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer & Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> <em>(10 CFR 55.41.b(10,4))</em> D is correct because the fire protection line to containment is normally isolated and is pressurized if both the zone 59 and 74 alarms are received.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A is incorrect because the FIRE light can illuminate if a manual fire pull box is activated, which only gives indication and does not initiate fire protection water flow.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> B is incorrect because for a fire in Relay Room (physically located outside CR but using same AC system) Fire Outside Control Area would not be selected.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C is incorrect because Halon supplied to relay rooms would not indicate stopping EDG supply ventilation.</div>
<div style="margin-top:6px;padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;color:#9ca3af;font-size:11px;"><strong>K/A statement:</strong> Knowledge of the reasons for the following responses as they apply to Plant Fire on Site: Steps called out in the site fire protection plan, FPS manual, and fire zone manual.</div>
