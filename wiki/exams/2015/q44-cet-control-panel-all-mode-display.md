<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q44 — What the "ALL" Mode Displays at the Train A CET Control Panel</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">017 K4.02 (RO 3.4 / SRO 3.4)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
A loss of off-site power resulted in a reactor trip. The operating crew is attempting to confirm natural circulation flow but neither SPDS NOR the Plant Computer is operating. You are the 3rd NCO and have been assigned to monitor and log CET's at the CET Control Panel.<br><br>
Which of the following describes the "ALL" Mode at the Train A CET Control Panel?<br><br>
It displays......
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Train A CET temperatures in sequential order.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Train A CET temperatures from lowest to highest reading.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> any Train A CET &gt;700°F, then remainder of Train A CETs from lowest to highest.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> the two highest reading Train A CET's in each quadrant then sequentially display all Train A CETs.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer & Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> <em>(10 CFR 55.41.b(7))</em> Table C of CFST-1 states that in ALL Mode the display will progress through the first and second highest CETS in each quadrant, then sequentially display all cETs assigned to that channel.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A is incorrect because does not display in sequential order without first displaying the 2 highest in each quadrant.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> B is incorrect because it doesn't display lowest to highest.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C is incorrect because the 700°F notes in choice C is criteria for purple path CFST for Core Cooling.</div>
<div style="margin-top:6px;padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;color:#9ca3af;font-size:11px;"><strong>K/A statement:</strong> Knowledge of In-Core Temperature Monitor System design feature(s) and or interlock(s) which provide for the following: Sensing and determination of location core hot spots.</div>
