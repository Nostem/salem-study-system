<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">073 A1.01 (RO 3.5 / SRO 3.5)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; Unit 1 is operating at 100% power.<br>
&bull; Control room operators are preparing to perform a Containment Pressure Relief IAW S1.OP-SO.CBV-0002, CONTAINMENT PRESSURE-VACUUM RELIEF SYSTEM OPERATION.<br>
&bull; Containment radiation levels are NORMAL for 100% power operation with no failed fuel.<br><br>
After opening the 1VC5 and 1VC6 CONT PRESS/VAC RELIEF ISOL valves to initiate the pressure relief, which choice describes how the respective radiation monitors indication will respond?<br>
&bull; 1R12A - Containment Gas Effluent<br>
&bull; 1R41B - Plant Vent Noble Gas Intermediate Range<br>
&bull; 1R41D - Plant Vent Noble Gas Release Rate
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 1R12A rises; 1R41B rises; 1R41D rises.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 1R12A rises; 1R41B constant; 1R41D constant.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 1R12A constant; 1R41B constant; 1R41D rises.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 1R12A constant; 1R41B rises; 1R41D constant.</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(11)1R12A is sampling containment atmosphere, so it will NOT rise when the pressure relief is started. 1R41B is an intermediate range monitor that normally does not have sample flow through it. It's sample flow will start when the lo range 1R41A monitor nears its high end of monitoring range. It's indication will not change during a pressure relief with NORMAL containment radiation levels. The R41D provides the gaseous effluent release rate (uCi/sec) by combining (product of) the on-range R41A through R41C with plant vent flow(cc/sec). It will rise when the pressure relief is initiated, and also provides automatic termination of release on hi gaseous effluent.</div>
