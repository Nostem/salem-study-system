<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q13 — 21RH18 and 2RH20 Position on Loss of Control Air During SBO</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">055 EA2.01 (RO 3.5 / SRO 3.6)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
&bull; Salem Unit 1 is operating at 100% power.<br>
&bull; Salem Unit 2 is in Mode 4 with 21 loop of RHR in SDC mode.<br>
&bull; Subsequently, a loss of all off site power occurs.<br>
&bull; ALL Unit 2 EDGs fail to start or trip shortly after starting, and ALL Unit 2 4KV vital buses are de-energized.<br>
&bull; The RO reports 21RH18 RHR HX FLOW CONT VALVE indicates 6% open and 2RH20 RHR HX BYP VALVE indicates 15% open, and questions their actual position.<br><br>
Which of the following describes the operation of the 21RH18 and 2RH20?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> BOTH the 21RH18 and 2RH20 fail AS IS on a loss of air and the indication is correct.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> The 21RH18 fails open and the 2RH20 fails shut, and the board indication shows last known position before the loss of power.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> BOTH the 21RH18 and 2RH20 fail SHUT on a loss of air, and the board indication shows how far they shut before losing all air pressure for operation.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> BOTH the 21RH18 and 2RH20 fail OPEN on a loss of air, and the board indication shows how far they opened before losing all air pressure for operation.</div>
</div>

<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(7) The 21RH18 and the 2RH20 fail AS IS on a loss of control air, and both are supplied air exclusively from the "A" header. Unit 2 ECAC supplies the "A" air header. With the Unit 2 ECAC also failing to start, control air pressure will bleed away fairly rapidly. The console indication will indicate actual valve position since 115VB power should be available from the inverters for at least 2 hours following a LOPA. The valve positions given in the stem are what would be expected with a loss of RHR in SDC mode.</div>
