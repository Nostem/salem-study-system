<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
How does the INITIAL Tavg response for a large steam line break (SLB) compare to the Tavg response for a large feed line break (FLB)?<br><br>
(Initial Tavg response is before automatic protective actions occur)
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Rises for a SLB and lowers for a FLB.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Lowers for a SLB and rises for a FLB.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Rises for both a SLB and a FLB, rises more for a SLB.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Lowers for both a SLB and a FLB, lowers more for a FLB.</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(5) A steam line break will cause MORE steam to be drawn from the SG, causing Tc to go down, causing Tavg to go down. A Feedline break will send LESS cold water into the SG so it's Tc will rise, and Tavg will rise. K/A match because the operational implication of the SG depressurization (similar to a steam line break) is that an operator would have to use diverse and alternate indication to discern what is actually happening to the SG.</div>
