<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; A SBLOCA has occurred.<br>
&bull; The crew is performing the actions of EOP-LOCA-2, POST LOCA COOLDOWN AND DEPRESSURIZATION.<br>
&bull; SI pumps have been stopped.<br>
&bull; Normal Charging is aligned.<br>
&bull; The crew is depressurizing the RCS using normal spray.<br><br>
Which of the following describes the strategy for controlling RCS subcooling during the depressurization?<br><br>
Subcooling will be…
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> minimized to reduce RCS break flow.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> maximized to ensure continued RCP operation.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> maximized to prevent a challenge to the core cooling critical safety function.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> minimized to ensure pressurizer level remains above the lower limit to allow heater operation during the RCS cooldown.</div>
</div>

<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer & Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> <em>(55.41.b(10))</em> Strategy of step 31 is to depressurize and attempt to minimize subcooling so that break flow is reduced, due to the minimal makeup provided by charging pumps.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> B is incorrect because RCP operation is not required for this event, although desired.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C is incorrect because core cooling should not be challenged on loss of subcooling at these temps and pressures(this point in the cooldown).</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> D is incorrect because PZR heater operation may be required to reduce the rate of increase in pressurizer level, but is not the reason for minimizing subcooling.</div>
