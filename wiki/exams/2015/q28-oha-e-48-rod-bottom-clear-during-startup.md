<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(6) There are 3 Rod Bottom Bypass Bistable Modules, for Control Banks B, C, and D only<br>1)Blocks OHA E-48, ROD BOTTOM for its own bank when entire bank is &lt;35 steps<br>2)When all banks are on bottom, OHA E-48, ROD BOTTOM alarm is illuminated.<br>3)When Control Bank A is &gt;20 steps alarm is cleared (Control Banks B, C, D, bypassed)<br>4)If no bypass, alarm would stay in until bank D was &gt;20 steps. Bypassing control banks B, C, D until &gt;35 steps gives indication of a dropped rod on any shutdown bank and control bank and each bank &gt;35 steps.</div>
