<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Choose the answer which contains ONLY actions directed to be performed IAW S2.OP-IO.ZZ-0007, Cold Shutdown to Refueling, BEFORE Rx Vessel Head detensioning would be initiated for a refueling outage starting in November.<br>
I. The Rx shall be subcritical for at least 168 hours.<br>
II. Direct, continuous communication between the control room and refuel floor is established.<br>
III. The RCS is drained to &lt;104' elevation.<br>
IV. Unit CRS AND SM approval.
</div>
