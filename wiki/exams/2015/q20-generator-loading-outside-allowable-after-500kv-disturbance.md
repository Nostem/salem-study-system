<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; Salem Unit 1 is offline.<br>
&bull; Salem Unit 2 operating at 95% power, 1150 Mwe, with its Power System Stabilizer (PSS) out of service.<br>
&bull; Unit 2 Main Generator gas pressure is 75 psig.<br>
&bull; Hope Creek is operating at 100% power, with its PSS out of service.<br>
&bull; The Hope Creek 5-6 breaker is out of service.<br>
&bull; Subsequently, a 500KV grid disturbance results in lower than normal grid voltage.<br><br>
If a power reduction was required, which of the following identifies Main Generator loading which is outside the allowable for Salem Unit 2 IAW A-5-500-EEE-1686, Artificial Island Operating Guide?<br><br>
Trip-A-Unit is NOT armed.<br><br>
Salem Unit 2 operating at <span class="blank">______</span> Mwe with MVAR loading <span class="blank">______</span> out.
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(4) With Unit 1 O/S and the HC 5-6 breaker O/S, the correct curve is 2S2H-5-6 on page 291. With both Units PSS O/S, the red dashed line will be used for allowable generator excitation. A is incorrect because the PSS is O/S. If either units PSS was IN service, then it would be correct. The 2 distracters with higher MVARS are both within the limit. Since there are two different Mwe loading conditions, and the choices for each are high/low, the answer cannot be obtained by ruling out 2 of the choices because there would have to be 2 correct answers for them to be correct. Not a direct lookup because several different figures are given.</div>
