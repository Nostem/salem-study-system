<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">022 A3.01 (RO 4.2 / SRO 4.2)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; Unit 2 is operating at 100% power.<br>
&bull; A Main Steamline break inside containment occurs.<br>
&bull; The Rx was tripped, but the MSLI failed to shut ANY MSIV.<br>
&bull; Safety injection was initiated successfully.<br>
&bull; Containment pressure has risen steadily and is currently 11 psig.<br>
&bull; RCS pressure is 1700 psig and lowering slowly.<br>
&bull; Safeguards reset actions have just been completed in 2-EOP-LOSC-2, Multiple Steam Generator Depressurization.<br><br>
Which of the following describes how the Containment Cooling system will respond if containment pressure were to rise to &gt;15 psig?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Both Containment Spray pumps will start and Containment Spray valves will reposition.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Neither Containment Spray pump will start, and Containment Spray valves will reposition.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Both Containment Spray pumps will start, and Containment Spray valves will NOT reposition.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Neither Containment Spray pump will start, and Containment Spray valves will NOT reposition.</div>
</div>

Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(9,7) Containment Spray Pump Sequencing<br>1)If the SSPS Containment HI-HI Pressure signal is not present when the SEC initially tries to start the Spray Pumps, the SEC contact will re-open<br>2)Once the SEC has completed the last step of its loading sequence, the CS Pump start contact is re-closed<br>a)If Hi-Hi Containment Pressure occurs the CS Pumps will auto start<br>b)If the SEC has been reset, the CS Pumps will NOT respond to a HI-HI Containment Pressure until the SEC is again actuated</div>
