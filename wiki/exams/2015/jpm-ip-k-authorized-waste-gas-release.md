<strong>Location:</strong> In-Plant (Waste Gas panels 104 and 105)<br>
<strong>Time-Critical:</strong> No<br>
<strong>Alternate Path:</strong> No<br>
<strong>Estimated Time:</strong> 15 minutes<br><br>
<strong>Initial Conditions:</strong><br>
Both Salem Units are operating at 100% power.<br>
21 Waste Gas Decay Tank is in service at 90 psig.<br>
22 Waste Gas Decay Tank is in Standby at 15 psig.<br>
23 Waste Gas Decay Tank is O/S at 15 psig.<br>
24 Waste Gas Decay Tank is O/S at 15 psig.<br><br>
<strong>Initiating Cue:</strong><br>
You are directed to place 24 Waste Gas Decay Tank (WGDT) in service and remove 21 Waste Gas Decay tank from service IAW Section 5.2 of S2.OP-SO.WG-0003, GASEOUS WASTE DISPOSAL SYSTEM OPERATION.<br><br>
Once 21 WGDT is removed from service, you are directed to align 21 WGDT for Holdup IAW S2.OP-SO.WG-0008, DISCHARGE OF 21 GAS DECAY TANK TO PLANT VENT in preparation for sampling and release of 21 WGDT. Prerequisites and P&amp;L's for BOTH procedures have been performed.
</div>

<strong style="color:#34d399;">Task Standard:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">1. Swap in-service WGDT from 21 to 24.<br>2. Align 21 WGDT for HOLDUP IAW Att 1 of S2.OP-SO.WG-0008.</span>

<div style="margin-top:12px;padding:10px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;">
<strong style="color:#22c55e;">Key Decision Point:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">There are three discriminating critical elements. First, before placing 24 GDT in service the applicant must verify <strong>24 GDT pressure is &gt;10 psig AND &lt;92 psig (2PIS1039)</strong> and no release is in progress — pressing the <strong>GAS DECAY TANK 24 MANUAL SELECT pushbutton on the 104 panel</strong> is the action that actually swaps the in-service tank (24 in-service light ON, 21 light OFF). Second, the in-service swap is not complete until <strong>21WG35 (GDT STOP VALVE TO HUT) is closed</strong> for the tank being removed and 24WG35 is opened — this is what redirects new waste gas to 24 GDT. Third, to align 21 GDT for holdup the applicant must perform the <strong>Attachment 1, Section 1.0 valve lineup of S2.OP-SO.WG-0008 and hang a Red Blocking Tag on 21WG31 (GDT INLET VALVE)</strong> in the closed position, isolating 21 GDT so it can be sampled and released without receiving additional gas. Placing 24 in service without verifying its pressure band, failing to close 21WG35, or failing to isolate/tag 21WG31 for holdup fails the task.</span>
</div>
