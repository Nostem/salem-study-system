<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; Unit 1 is at 100% power.<br>
&bull; 11, 13, and 16 SW pumps are in service, with 15 SW pump selected to Auto.<br>
&bull; Subsequently, a loss of the 500 KV Switchyard occurs.<br>
&bull; 1A 4KV Vital Bus has de-energized due to a Bus Differential relay actuation.<br>
&bull; Unit 1 has initiated a MANUAL Safety Injection (SI).<br><br>
Which of the following identifies the Service Water Pumps which will be running 2 minutes after the SI has been initiated?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 11 and 14.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 12 and 13.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 13 and 15.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 14 and 16.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(7) This question meets the K/A because SW pumps are powered from opposite vital buses when Unit 1 is compared to Unit 2. U1 pumps are C,C,B,A,A, whereas U2 are A,A,B,B,C,C. The Lead pump on B bus is always 14 unless 14 is not available. With 1A bus deenergized, 15 and 16 SW pumps have no power. The SW pump selected to auto (15) will not start on low pressure (nor will it have power) as it will be locked out by SEC initiation.</div>
