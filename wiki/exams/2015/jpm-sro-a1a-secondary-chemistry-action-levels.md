<strong>Initial Conditions:</strong><br>
While operating at 100% power, Salem Unit 1 has experienced a rise in Sodium levels in 22B main condenser hotwell. Chemistry samples have been reported at 0800 on September 11th:<br>
- S/G Blowdown pH: 10.1<br>
- S/G Blowdown Chlorides: 38 ppb<br>
- S/G Blowdown Sodium: 51 ppb<br>
- S/G Blowdown Sulfate: 22 ppb<br>
- S/G Blowdown Cation Conductivity: 0.7 uS/cm<br>
- All other applicable conditions fall in the "normal" range.<br><br>
<strong>Initiating Cue:</strong><br>
Using the assumptions that:<br>
- The Secondary Chemistry conditions remain at these levels and cannot be corrected.<br>
- The Condensate Polisher is bypassed.<br>
- SGBD is aligned to the Flashtank at 39k / loop. Chemistry wants 39k / loop to be maintained.<br>
- The SGBD Demineralizer is not the source of the impurities.<br>
- All other systems are in their normal alignment.<br>
Determine the actions required over the next 24 hours based on these conditions. Place your response on this JPM tear-off sheet.<br>
Assume that any required plant power changes will only be the minimum required and take the entire length of time allowed.
</div>

<strong style="color:#22c55e;">Key Decision Point:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">Steps 3.16 and 3.29 are the discriminating steps. The applicant must enter Attachment 4 (CHEMISTRY ACTION LEVELS) with the SGBD Sodium value of 51 ppb and recognize it places the unit in Chemistry Action Level 2 (not Level 1 or Level 3). The non-obvious determination at Step 3.29 is the time limit: because this is <strong>Unit 1 with alloy 600TT SG tubing</strong>, power must be reduced to &le; 30% within <strong>12 hours</strong> of entering Action Level 2 — NOT the 24-hour limit that applies to Unit 2. Entering at 0800 September 11th, the &le; 30% power must be achieved by 2000 September 11th.</span>
