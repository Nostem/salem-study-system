<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; Salem Unit 2 is 7 days into a refueling outage.<br>
&bull; The core is partially offloaded with 7 bundles remaining in the Rx.<br>
&bull; Fuel movement is in progress, and S2.OP-IO.ZZ-0010 SPENT FUEL POOL MANIPULATIONS is in effect.<br>
&bull; SFP temperature is 120 deg. F.<br>
&bull; 21 SFP becomes air bound, trips on motor OL, and can NOT be restarted.<br>
&bull; 22 SFP pump will NOT start.<br>
&bull; SFP hi level alarm is in alarm.<br>
&bull; SFP heatup rate is 12 deg F/ hr.<br><br>
If SFP cooling can NOT be restored, which of the following choices describes an adverse consequence of this loss of Spent Fuel Pool cooling?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Rx cavity overflow due to rising SFP level if the Gate Valve remains open.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Increased production of radioactive waste liquid as the tell-tale drains flow rises to the FHB sump.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Increased radiation levels at the FHB charcoal filter due to Spent Fuel off-gassing at temps &gt; 150 deg. F.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Inability to place a raised Spent Fuel bundle into any location in the pool due to rising radiation level on 2R32 Fuel Handling Crane Area Monitor.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> Distractor b is incorrect because the pool would overflow into the ventilation system, not come over the physical wall of the pool.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> Distractor d is incorrect because it is always possible to lower a SF bundle.</div>
