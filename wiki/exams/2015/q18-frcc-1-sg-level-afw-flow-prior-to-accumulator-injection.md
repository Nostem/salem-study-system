<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; Operators are performing actions in 2-EOP-FRCC-1, Response to Inadequate Core Cooling.<br>
&bull; Containment pressure is 2 psig and stable.<br><br>
Which of the following describes the SG level and AFW flow requirements PRIOR to initiating the SG Depressurization to Inject ECCS Accumulators?<br><br>
SG NR level in at least one SG must be greater than <span class="blank">______</span>
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 9% OR total AFW flow must be &gt;22E4 lbm/hr.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 15% OR total AFW flow must be &gt;22E4 lbm/hr.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 9% AND total AFW flow available of &gt;22E4 lbm/hr.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 15% AND total AFW flow available of &gt;22E4 lbm/hr.</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(10) Step 13 asks if any SG NR level is &gt;9% (15% adverse). Since containment pressure is stated as 2 psig, it is below the 4 psig at which adverse numbers would be used. If SG NR level is &gt;9% in at least one SG, the step for asking if &gt;22E4 lbm/hr AFW flow is present is bypassed. If SG NR level is &lt;9%, then it requires 22E4 lbm/hr. So you need at least 9% OR actual total AFW flow &gt;22E4 lbm/hr. The availability is plausible as it is used in other Emerg procedures, but in this case a secondary heat sink is required, which is defined as &gt;9% or &gt;22E4 lbm/hr flow.</div>
