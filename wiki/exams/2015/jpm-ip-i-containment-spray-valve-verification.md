<div style="margin-top:12px;padding:10px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;">
<strong style="color:#22c55e;">Key Decision Point:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">The discriminating step is the <strong>PPE determination</strong>. The initiating cue states PPE requirements are <strong>RED</strong> for the flushing evolution, and the applicant must independently look up SA-AA-2113 (Chemical Safety) to identify the <strong>RED-category PPE for Sodium Hydroxide [NaOH] 0-50%</strong> — the spray additive tank fluid being flushed. The eductor line and Spray Additive Tank discharge contain concentrated NaOH, so the correct PPE set (Neoprene UltraFlex gloves, chemical goggles/face shield, ChemMAX TYCHEM chemical suit, butyl boots) must be selected before opening any drain/vent valve. The valve manipulation sequence is then straightforward: open 1CS31 (RWST supply to eductors) and 1CS40 (drain), flush 3 minutes, sample via 1CS61, and on a satisfactory sodium result (&lt;10 ppm) restore by closing/locking 1CS31. Selecting inadequate PPE for the RED chemical hazard, or failing to lock 1CS31 closed on restoration, fails the task.</span>
</div>
