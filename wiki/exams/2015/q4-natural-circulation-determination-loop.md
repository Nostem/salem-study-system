<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">015 AK1.01 (RO 3.9 / SRO 3.9)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
&bull; Unit 2 is in Mode 3 following a shutdown after a 200 day run.<br>
&bull; Core burnup is 18000 MWd/MTU.<br>
&bull; All RCP's are in operation.<br>
&bull; Main steam dumps are in MS PRESSURE CONTROL-AUTO @ 1005 psig.<br>
&bull; A transformer fault results in the total loss of off-site power.<br><br>
15 minutes after the transformer fault, with NO operator action, the following indications are present:<br>
&bull; All RCS WR Thot's are 559&deg;F and rising slowly.<br>
&bull; All RCS WR Tcold's are 547&deg;F and stable.<br>
&bull; All SG pressures are 1015 psig and stable.<br>
&bull; All SG NR levels are 70% and stable.<br>
&bull; PZR level is 23% and rising slowly.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies:<br>
1) The status of natural circulation<br>
2) The action(s) that will be performed by the control room IAW S2.OP-AB.RC-0004 Natural Circulation based on that determination of natural circulation status
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 1) Natural circulation NOT established<br>2) Raise the steam dumping rate.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 1) Natural circulation NOT established<br>2) Raise the feeding rate to all steam generators.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 1) Natural circulation IS established<br>2) Start AFW pumps to maintain steam generator narrow range level in the normal band.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 1) Natural circulation IS established<br>2) Maintain in established natural steam generator narrow range level in the normal band and adjust MS-10's to enhance heat removal.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(10) The question makes the K/A based on the Loss of RC Flow portion of the K/A. AB.RC-0004 step 3.6 identifies ALL the conditions that must be met for natural circulation to be occurring. With RCS Thots still rising, it is NOT occurring. Step 3.7 directs the operator to feed the SGs to maintain them within +/- 5% of programmed band. Programmed band plus 5% = 38%, so feeding in this situation is not directed. Steam dumping is directed to maintain or lower CET temps.</div>
