<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the conditions for Unit 2:<br>
&bull; Rx is in Mode 3, NOT, NOP.<br>
&bull; RWST concentration - 2450 ppm<br>
&bull; 21 BAT concentration - 6650 ppm<br>
&bull; 22 BAT concentration - 6650 ppm<br>
&bull; 22 BAT level - 43%<br><br>
Which of the following describes the LOWEST level for 21 BAT that meets or exceeds the operability requirements for the BAT?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 43%.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 54%.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 92%.</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(6,7) TS 3.1.2.6 requires a certain amount of borated water available. The BASTs are normally cross connected so the TOTAL volume of the 2 tanks is what is required to be above the limit. With RWST boron concentration of 2450, and both BAT tanks at 6650, the intersection is ~93.5%. If 1 tank is at 43 % the other tank must be at 50.5%. To preclude picking the wrong answer because of interpolation, the correct answer of 54% is 3.5% higher than required.</div>
