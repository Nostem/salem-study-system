<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">061 K5.03 (2.6/2.9)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
With both 11 and 12 AFW pumps in service providing 6E4 lbm/hr flow to each SG, what would be the response if both 13AF21 and 14AF21 were shut fully?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 11 AFW pump automatic recirc valve would open.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> The single automatic recirc valve for the MDAFW pumps would open.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> The Pressure Override Circuit would actuate to prevent dead heading 11 AFW pump.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Normal MDAFW pump recirc flow would rise through the orificed continuous recirculation line.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer & Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> <em>(10 CFR 55.41.b(7,8))</em> The MDAFW pumps each has its own dedicated recirc line and associated automatic recirc valve, which opens to maintain aFW pump flow &gt;180 gpm. With the 6E4 lbm/hr in stem for each of the 2 SGs being supplied from 11 AFW pump that = 240 gpm, the valve would initially be closed and open when flow lowers &lt;180 to prevent the increased pump head from causing pump damage from overheating.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> B is incorrect because if it is thought there is a common recirc line.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C is incorrect because the Pressure Override circuit acts to close the AF21s id AFW pump pressure lowers to far to prevent runout of pump.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> D is incorreect but plausible because that is how the recirc line for the TDAFW pump is setup for continuous recirc.</div>
