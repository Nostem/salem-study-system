<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q11 — Crew Action for a Single Stuck Rod After Trip During SG Tube Leak</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">G2.4.16 (RO 3.5 / SRO 4.4)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
&bull; Unit 2 is performing a controlled shutdown and cooldown from 100% power due to a 5-gpm tube leak on 22 SG IAW S2.OP-AB.SG-0001, Steam Generator Tube Leak.<br>
&bull; After completing the Immediate Actions of EOP-TRIP-1, Reactor Trip or Safety Injection, following the Rx trip from 20% power, the RO reports that control rod 2D2 is stuck in the fully withdrawn position.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies the action, if any, the crew will perform in response to the stuck rod?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Initiate a rapid boration for 35 minutes during performance of EOP-TRIP-2.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Initiate a rapid boration for 35 minutes in S2.OP-AB.SG-0001 after exiting the TRIP series procedures.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> No actions are required for a single stuck rod because SDM for the cooldown to 503 degrees is adequate.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> No actions are required for a single stuck rod until the Auto SI Block is performed during RCS depressurization to 1900 psig.</div>
</div>

<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41 b(10) Question meets K/A because knowledge is required of the relationship between AB performance in the initial response to the tube rupture and EOP performance which addresses stuck rods. Boration for a single stuck rod is not performed in the EOP series, but IS performed in the AB for tube rupture to meet SDM requirements for the initial cooldown to 500&deg;F. Step 3.28 in AB SG states to trip the turbine, then trip the Rx at 20% power during the shutdown. The 5 gpm size of the leak will allow for a controlled shutdown, and will also allow the crew to transition to TRIP-2. There are no SDM diagnostic steps in TRIP-2 that would cause a transition to SGTR-1. AB SG would be re-entered at step 3.27 following exit of TRIP-2, and 3.28 directs rapid boration for each stuck rod for 35 minutes. The rapid boration will be initiated before any depressurization starts in 3.29, so the distracter regarding depressurization is incorrect.</div>
