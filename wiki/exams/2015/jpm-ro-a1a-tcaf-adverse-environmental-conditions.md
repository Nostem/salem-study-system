<strong>Initial Conditions:</strong><br>
- Both Salem Units are operating at 100% power.<br>
- All Unit 2 Circulators are in service.<br>
- 13B and 12A Circulators are O/S.<br>
- A report is received which states that wind speeds of 48 mph from direction 200&deg; are expected onsite 8 hours from now.<br>
- The CRS enters SC.OP-AB.ZZ-0001, Adverse Environmental Conditions.<br>
- Current wind speed is 16 mph, and is expected to rise linearly over the next 8 hours.<br>
- River temperature is 58&deg;F.<br>
- Outside air temperature is 65&deg;F.<br>
- The latest rolling weekly detritus loading is 2200 kg/million m<sup>3</sup>.<br>
- River level over the next 8 hours is predicted to be: T-0 89, T+1 89', T+2 90', T+3 90', T+4 91', T+5 92', T+6 93', T+7 94', T+8 94'.<br>
- The current time is 1100.<br><br>
<strong>Initiating Cue:</strong><br>
- Determine the required status of the reactors for the remainder of the shift as shown below by determining the Circulating Water Degradation Index (CWDI), Attachment 3, for BOTH units as directed in Section 3.11.<br>
- Assume the units will be maintained in the HIGHEST Mode allowed.

<div style="margin-top:12px;padding:10px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;">
<strong style="color:#22c55e;">Key Decision Point:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">The discriminating steps are the CWDI rating determination on Attachment 3 and step 3.11.2. After tallying detritus, circulator, wind-speed, and river-level points hour by hour, the applicant must determine the CWDI reaches a RED rating at T+6 (1700) for Unit 1 and T+7 (1800) for Unit 2 — the two units differ because Unit 1's out-of-service circulator (12A) adds 1 circulator point/hour that Unit 2 does not have. Step 3.11.2 then requires placing each unit in at least MODE 2 a full 2 hours before its RED time, yielding Unit 1 in MODE 2 by 1500 and Unit 2 by 1600. Mis-counting the per-hour points or forgetting the 2-hour lead time gives the wrong required Mode entry time.</span>
</div>
