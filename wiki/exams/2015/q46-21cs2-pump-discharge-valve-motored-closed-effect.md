Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(8,10) When RWST level reaches 15.2', operators will beging alignment to Cold Leg Recirc IAW LOCA-3. 22 Containment Spray pump is stopped first if both Containment Spray pumps are operating. When RSWT level reaches lo-lo setpoint, the remaining CS pump (21) will be stopped, and 21CS36 opened to supply recirculation spray flow.</div>
