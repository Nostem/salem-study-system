<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q54 — AFW Pump Status After a 21 SGFP Trip at 24% Power (Both SGFPs Tripped)</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">061000 K6.02 (2.6/2.7)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; Unit 2 is performing a normal shutdown at 20% per hour IAW the IOP's.<br>
&bull; 22 SGFP is shutdown.<br>
&bull; Current Rx power is 24%.<br>
&bull; 21 SGFP trips.<br><br>
With NO operator action, what will be the status of the Auxiliary Feed Pumps?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> ONLY the MDAFW pumps start immediately upon the trip of 21 SGFP.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> ONLY the MDAFW pumps start when SG NR level in 1/4 S/G's lowers to 14%.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> The MDAFW pumps AND the TDAFW pump start immediately upon the trip of 21 SGFP.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> The MDAFW pumps AND the TDAFW pump start when NR level in 1/4 S/G's lowers to 14%.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b (4) MDAFW pumps auto start when both SGFPs are tripped as shoen on logic drawing 221064. The TDAFW pump does not. The MDAFW pumps also auto start on 2/3 NR level channels in one SG lowers to 14%, but in this case they will already be running. The TDAFW pump starts on 2/3 NR level channels on 2/4 SGs.</div>
