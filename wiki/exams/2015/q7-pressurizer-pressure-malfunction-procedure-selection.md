<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
&bull; Unit 2 is operating normally at 100% power steady state.<br>
&bull; Console alarm RC PRESS DEVIATION HI, annunciates.<br><br>
Subsequently, the board operator then observes the following indications:<br>
&bull; PZR pressure master controller output dropping slowly<br>
&bull; Both PZR spray valves closed<br>
&bull; All PZR heaters energized<br>
&bull; PZR pressure channel I (selected for control) dropping slowly<br>
&bull; PZR pressure channels II, III, &amp; IV rising slowly<br><br>
Which of the following procedures should be used for these conditions?
</div>
