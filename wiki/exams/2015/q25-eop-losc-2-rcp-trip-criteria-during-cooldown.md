<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; Unit 1 has experienced a MSLB at the mixing bottle.<br>
&bull; MSLI has failed to close ANY MS167.<br>
&bull; Operators have transitioned out of 1-EOP-LOSC-1, Loss of Secondary Coolant.<br>
&bull; RCS pressure is 1345 psig and dropping slowly.<br>
&bull; RCS Tcs are dropping.<br><br>
Which of the following describes Reactor Coolant Pump strategy, and why?<br><br>
RCPs should…
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> be tripped to minimize the heat input into the RCS.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> continue to be run, because RCP pressure dependent trip criteria are only applicable during a LBLOCA.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> continue to be run, since RCP pressure dependent trip criteria is not used when a cooldown is in progress.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> be tripped so that 2 phase flow doesn't develop if they were to be tripped later in the event, leading to higher peak clad temperatures.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(10,3)The RCP trip criteria with regards to pressure in a LOSC condition is for pump protection only. The Generic issues segment of the ERG executive volume describes the SBLOCA scenarios where pumping coolant out the break then stopping RCP's leads to peak clad temps in excess of 2200 degrees. That is not applicable here. What is more important in the LOSC is forced flow. LOSC-2 basis document page 12.</div>
