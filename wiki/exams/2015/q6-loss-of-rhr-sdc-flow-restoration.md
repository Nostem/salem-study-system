Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(10,8) With a single loop of RHR in service for SDC, the loop cross-tie valves 21/22RH19 are open. They would only have been shut if BOTH loops were in SDC mode, to split out the RHR loops to verify minimum flow requirements are being maintained (S2.OP-SO.RHR-0001, page 36). There is no direction to close either cross tie valve in Attachment 2 of AB.RHR when swapping the ECCS loop to SDC mode. There is also no direction to shut either SJ49 RHR to RCS valves, since cooling flow to all 4 RCS loops is desired.
