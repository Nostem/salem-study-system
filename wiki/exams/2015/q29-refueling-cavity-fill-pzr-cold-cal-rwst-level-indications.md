<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; Unit 2 is in MODE 6.<br>
&bull; The Reactor vessel head is removed and on its storage stand.<br>
&bull; The Refueling Cavity is being filled from the RWST IAW S2.OP-SO.SF-0003, FILLING THE REFUELING CAVITY. (Assume RWST level was at 40.5' when the unit was shutdown)<br>
&bull; Refueling cavity water level is 110' and rising.<br><br>
Which choice identifies the indications which will be present on the Control Room Console?<br><br>
PZR Cold Calibrated level is ...

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(3,)The refueling cavity at 110' will hold ~112000 gallons. The RWST normal operating level is a minimum of 40.5 feet prior to refueling (364500 gal). Subtract and the total left in RWST will be 253000 gallons, which will be ~27.5'. The corresponding PZR cold cal level will be &gt; the 0% level at the 108' 11" elevation in containment. This question does not require memorization of tank levels, but rather an understanding of the physical connections and relative elevations of the systems.</div>
