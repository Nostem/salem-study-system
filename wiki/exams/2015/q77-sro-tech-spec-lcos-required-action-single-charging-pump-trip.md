<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; Unit 2 is operating at 100% power.<br>
&bull; 21 charging pump is in service.<br>
&bull; 22 and 23 charging pumps are operable.<br>
&bull; Subsequently 21 charging pump trips due to a breaker malfunction.<br>
&bull; The CRS enters S2.OP-AB.CVC-0001 and places 23 charging pump in service.<br><br>
Which of the following:<br>
1) Identifies ALL the Tech Spec LCO(s) which will be entered<br>
2) What the required action(s) is/are if 21 charging pump remains inoperable for the next 4 days?<br><br>
The CRS will enter.....
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> <span class="blank">_(1)_</span> LCO 3.1.2.2.b for not having required boration flowpaths, LCO 3.1.2.4 for not having required charging pumps, and LCO 3.5.2.a for not having required ECCS subsystems available. <span class="blank">_(2)_</span> Be in Mode 3 and borated to a SDM equivalent to at least 1% delta k/k within 78 hours of pump trip.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> <span class="blank">_(1)_</span> LCO 3.1.2.2.b for not having required boration flowpaths, LCO 3.1.2.4 for not having required charging pumps, and LCO 3.5.2.a for not having required ECCS subsystems available. <span class="blank">_(2)_</span> Be in Mode 4 within 84 hours of pump trip.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> <span class="blank">_(1)_</span> LCO 3.5.2.a ONLY for not having required ECCS subsystems available. <span class="blank">_(2)_</span> Be in Mode 3 and borated to a SDM equivalent to at least 1% delta k/k within 78 hours of pump trip.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> <span class="blank">_(1)_</span> LCO 3.5.2.a ONLY for not having required ECCS subsystems available. <span class="blank">_(2)_</span> Be in Mode 4 within 84 hours of pump trip.</div>

Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.42.b(2) This question is SRO level based on NUREG-1021, ES401, Attachment 2, page 17, II.B, 1st bullet for application of required actions. Inoperability of a single charging pump in Modes 1-3 only results in entry into the ECCS LCO. The 2 other LCOs would be entered upon inoperability of the second charging pump. The required action is to restore within 72 hours or be in Hot Shutdown within next 12 hours. The incorrect action in the distracters is for when 2 charging pumps are inoperable.</div>
