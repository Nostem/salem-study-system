An ALERT has been declared at Salem due to the Unit 2 reactor failing to trip when a manual trip signal was initiated. The Rx tripped when the second trip handle was used.<br>

The Emergency News Center (ENC) has not activated yet.<br><br>

You are the Secondary Communicator. You are directed to perform EP-SA-111-F8, Attachment 8 Secondary Communicator Log.

<div style="font-size:12px;color:#9ca3af;line-height:1.7;">
The Secondary Communicator backs up the Primary Communicator during an emergency: completing the Secondary Communicator Log (EP-SA-111-F8, Attachment 8), assisting with the off-site notifications (state and local authorities), making the required NRC notification, and activating / supporting the Emergency News Center (ENC) when directed. The task is administrative (log/form completion) and is performed in the Classroom for this exam.
</div>

<div style="margin-top:12px;padding:10px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;">
<strong style="color:#22c55e;">Key Decision Point:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">The applicant must correctly classify the event context — an ALERT with the unit stable in MODE 3 and the ENC not yet activated — and perform the Secondary Communicator duties from EP-SA-111-F8 in support of the Primary Communicator's 15-minute off-site notifications. The companion Primary Communicator JPM (2019 RO-A4, EP-SA-111-F6) shows the time-critical 15-minute notification flow that the Secondary Communicator supports.</span>
</div>
