Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.43.b(6,5) This question is SRO level based on the knowledge of internal effects on core reactivity of a dropped rod while exactly critical, and the procedural direction to insert all rods based on becoming subcritical. The bases for that insertion is that performing a dropped rod recovery when the problem has been fixed, which would normally be done at power, would NOT be done if the Rx were subcritical, because withdrawing that rod would constitute an approach to criticality and is required to be performed IAW the appropriate startup procedure.</div>
