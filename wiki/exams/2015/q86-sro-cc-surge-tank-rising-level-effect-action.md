<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q86 (SRO 11) — CC Surge Tank Rising Level Effect and Control Room Action</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">008 A2.02 (RO 3.8 / SRO 3.7)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; Unit 2 is operating at 75% power.<br>
&bull; 21 Waste Holdup Tank (WHUT) is being processed via the Portable Liquid Radwaste Processing System.<br>
&bull; Console alarm SURGE TANK LEVEL HI-LO for the CC system alarms.<br>
&bull; Surge tank levels are 59.3% and 59.2% on Channels A and B, and rising slowly.<br>
&bull; No other alarms are present.<br><br>
Which of the following describes the effect if Surge Tank Level were to continue to rise, and what action the control room will take in response to the rising level?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 2CC149 CC SURGE TANK VENT VLV will auto close to prevent overflow. Stop processing 21 WHUT as directed in the S2.OP-AR.ZZ-0011, Control Console 2CC1.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Overflow of the Surge Tank will contaminate the Waste Holdup System with chromates. Close 2CC149 CC SURGE TANK VENT VLV from 2CC2 as directed in the S2.OP-AR.ZZ-0011, Control Console 2CC1.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 2CC149 CC SURGE TANK VENT VLV will auto close to prevent overflow. Open the CC Surge Tank Drain Valve from 2CC1 as necessary to maintain Surge Tank Level &lt;100% as directed in S2.OP-AB.CC-0001, Component Cooling Abnormality.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Overflow of the Surge Tank will contaminate the Waste Holdup System with chromates. Direct a NEO to locally drain the Surge Tank to 55 gallon drum as necessary to maintain Surge Tank Level &lt;100% as directed in S2.OP-AB.CC-0001, Component Cooling Abnormality.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.43.b(5) This question is SRO level based on having to asses the conditions given in stem and determine which action in which procedure will be taken. The 2CC149 auto closes on hi radiation in the CCW systen, nnot high pressure, to prevent radiation release from the CCW system. The Surge Tank will be locally drained. The overflow from the tank will go to the Waste Holdup system and contaminate the WHUT. WHUT processing will be stopped in both the AB.CC and ARP procedures. Locally draining the tank is also directed by both procedures.</div>
