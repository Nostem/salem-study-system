<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(7) As level in the reference leg lowers, the pressure of the actual weight of water in the PZR would appear to be greater than it was, as it is pushing against a lower reference pressure from the reference leg. This would result in indicated PZR level rising. The rising level would cause an automatic response of the Master Flow Controller to lower charging flow, which would cause actual PZR level to lower.</div>
