<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q35 — Applicable Tech Spec LCO if 22 Charging Pump Trips</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">G2.2.42 (RO 3.9 / SRO 4.6)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; Unit 2 is operating at 100% power with 22 charging pump in service.<br>
&bull; 23 charging pump is out of service and operable.<br><br>
Which of the following Tech Spec LCO's would be applicable if 22 charging pump were to trip?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 3.5.4 - Seal Injection Flow.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 3.5.2 -ECCS Subsystems &gt;350°F.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 3.1.2.4 -Charging Pumps - Operating.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 3.1.2.2 - Boration Flowpaths - Operating.</div>
</div>

<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer & Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> <em>(10 CFR 55.41.b(6,10))</em> RO candidates are responsible for "Above the Line" knowledge of Tech Spec LCO's. B is correct because the LCO states 2 complete trains of ECCS are required, and 22 charging pump (hi head ECCS) is required for the B train of ECCS.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A is incorrect because the LCO is for maximum seal injection flow which is set by adjusting manual seal injection throttle valves. Plausible because many procedures direct establishing 6-12 gpm seal injection flow per pump not to exceed 40 gpm total.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C is incorrect because only 2 charging pumps are required to be operable, and the LCO would not be entered until 2 of 3 charging pumps were inoperable.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> D is incorrect because 2 boration flow paths from RWST through charging pumps remain available, as does the Boric Acid tank flowpath with one BAT pump and one charging pump.</div>
