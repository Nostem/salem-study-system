<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q10 — Crew Response to 2R19C SGBD Rad Alarm During EOP-TRIP-2</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">000037 AA2.01 (3.0/3.4)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
&bull; Unit 2 experiences a Rx trip from 100% power when a Main Generator trip causes a Main Turbine trip.<br>
&bull; Upon transition to TRIP-2, Reactor Trip Response, the PO reports OHA A-6 RMS HI RAD has annunciated, and Radiation Monitor 2R19C - 23SG Blowdown, is in alarm with rising counts.<br><br>
Which of the following describes how the crew should proceed?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Initiate SI and go to TRIP-1, Reactor Trip or Safety Injection.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Enter S2.OP-AB.SG-0001, Steam Generator Tube Leak, while continuing in TRIP-2.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Do NOT initiate any procedures based solely on a 2R19 alarm immediately following a Rx trip.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Enter S2.OP-AB.RAD-0001, Abnormal Radiation, to verify validity of alarm while continuing in TRIP-2.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(10) AB.SG says in note under entry conditions that R19s are not accurate immediately following a unit trip and should not be used as the sole basis for entering it. However, AB.RAD should be entered and the alarm verified as directed at step 3.2. The SI is not warranted without corroborating indications.</div>
<div style="margin-top:6px;padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;color:#9ca3af;font-size:11px;"><strong>K/A statement:</strong> Ability to determine and interpret the following as they apply to Steam Generator Tube Leak: Unusual readings of the monitors; steps needed to verify readings.<br><em>Note: the 2015 NRC worksheet provides a single "Explanation of Answers" (above); it does not contain separate per-distractor justifications, so none are fabricated here.</em></div>
