<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; 2C Emergency Diesel Generator (EDG) is operating in parallel with the 500KV grid for a 24 hour endurance run IAW S2.OP-ST.DG-0014, 2C DIESEL GENERATOR ENDURANCE RUN, following a complete overhaul.<br>
&bull; Cumulative run times for all individual EDG load limits are less than 10% of rated.<br>
&bull; While operating at 2525 KW three hours into the test, the operator mistakenly adjusts 2C EDG speed control resulting in MW loading rising to 2610 KW.<br><br>
What are the consequences, if any, of continued EDG operation at this KW load?<br><br>
Operation for the remaining 21 hours of the test...

<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> will result in exceeding the 30 minute load limitation for 2C EDG.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> will result in exceeding the 2 hour load limitation for 2C EDG.</div>

Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(8) The EDG load limitations are maximum of: 2600KW continuous, 2600-2750KW for 2000 hours, 2750-2860KW for 30 minutes, and 2860-3100KW for 2 hours. With the EDG operating at 2610KW for 21 hours, the EDG will not exceed any limits, operation between 2600 (cont) and 2750(2000 hours) KW, since the stem stated that the cumulative run time for ALL EDG load limits was &lt;10%, which would be 210 hours for this limit.</div>
