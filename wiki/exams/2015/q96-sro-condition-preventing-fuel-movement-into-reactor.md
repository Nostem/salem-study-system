<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">194001 2.2.1 (4.5/4.4) — SRO</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br>
&bull; Unit 2 is in MODE 6 with core reload in progress.<br>
&bull; 10 fuel assemblies have been moved into the Rx.<br>
&bull; Rx cavity level is 26' above the RPV flange.<br>
&bull; 21 RHR loop is in service in Shutdown Cooling.<br>
&bull; 22 RHR loop is O/S and available.<br><br>
Which one of the following would prevent continuation of fuel movement into the reactor?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Loss of Control Air to containment.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Racking down the 22 RHR pump 4KV breaker.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Both 100' elevation containment airlock doors are opened.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> With both SRNIs operable, only ONE is capable of providing audible indication in the control room.</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ A. Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.43(7, 6) This question is SRO level because of the knowledge require for fuel handling procedures, and the ability to continuously apply that knowledge when operating the fuel handling equipment. The requirement for SRNI's is BOTH operable and providing VISUAL indication in the Control room, with ONE providing AUDIBLE indication in the control room. The manipulator crane is air powered for gripping, so the loss of air to containment would preclude being able to perform core alts. Only ONE RHR loop is required to be in operation in MODE 6, 2 RHR loops are required to be OPERABLE when &lt;23' level above the flange.</div>
