&bull; Unit 2 was operating at 100% reactor power when a Reactor Trip and Safety Injection were initiated due to lowering Pressurizer pressure.<br>
&bull; Five minutes after the SI actuation, containment humidity and pressure have just begun rising.<br><br>
Assuming NO operator actions were taken, which of the following would result in those conditions?

Correct.</strong> <em>Explanation of Answers (verbatim from source):</em> 55.41.b(9)The failure of a RCP #1 seal will not be seen in containment outside of closed systems. The excess seal leakoff flow past the #2 seal will be seen as a rise in RCDT level. The PZR safety failing open will cause the PRT to pressurize and the rupture disk to rupture, causing the saturated steam in the PRT to continuously be vented to containment. The Spray valve failing open would cause the lowering PZR pressure, bit would not cause changing containment conditions.</div>
