# 2016 NRC Operating Exam

## Overview

- **Exam:** SALEM 2016 NRC Exam — 15-01 ILOT (Op-Test 15-01 NRC)
- **Examination Date:** 12/12/2016 (per outline Form ES-301-2)
- **Facility:** Salem Units 1 & 2
- **Admin JPMs (RO):** 4 — A1-1, A1-2, A2, A3
- **Admin JPMs (SRO):** 6 entries across 4 admin items — A1-1, A1-2, A2, A3, A4-1 (ESG-1), A4-1 (ESG-2)
- **Simulator JPMs:** 8 — Sim a–h
- **In-Plant JPMs:** 3 — IP-i (Annual), IP-j, IP-k
- **Simulator Scenarios:** 3 — ESG-1, ESG-2, ESG-3

The 15-01 ILOT class had a **2017 retake** of the simulator portion only — see [[2017 NRC Operating Exam]] (scenarios ESG-7, ESG-8). The 2017 retake reused this class's JPM administration; only Section C (simulator scenarios) was re-administered.
