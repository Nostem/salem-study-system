<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Salem Unit 1 is operating at 100% power when a loss of ALL AC power occurs.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies a consequence if ALL AC power remains deenergized for at least one day?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Containment temperature rise to near saturation conditions as RCS inventory is released which will complicate recovery when AC power is restored.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Containment degradation will result from sustained pressure above 15 psig after RCDT reliefs lift and remain open.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Loss of ECCS pumped injection capability coupled with RCP seal leakage will result in core uncovery.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> PZR PORVs cycling would ultimately result in SBLOCA.</div>
</div>

RCS inventory will be released to containment, however, the containment is designed for a LBLOCA in which all the mass in the RCS is released to the containment and long term recovery is not affected.</div>
