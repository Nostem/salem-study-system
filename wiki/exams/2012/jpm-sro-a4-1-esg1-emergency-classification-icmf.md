<strong>Initial Conditions:</strong><br>
1. You have a maximum of 5 minutes to review the Emergency Operating Procedures used during this scenario to refresh your memory of all events/paths. At the end of your review you will become the Shift Manager (SM). Inform the Evaluator when you are ready to assume SM duties. You may continue to reference the procedures or to look at the control board but "the clock will be running." If there are multiple ECG calls, classify the most severe.<br><br>
<strong>Initiating Cue:</strong><br>
You are the Duty SM. Classify the event, complete the correct ECG Attachment and provide an ICMF to the Primary Communicator.<br>
This is a Time Critical JPM.

<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">1. Complete the ICMF with the classification of ALERT under EAL RB2.L and provide it to the Primary Communicator within 15 minutes of Start Time.<br>OR<br>2. This is the classification if the cavitating charging pump is not recognized and it fails.</span>

<div style="margin-top:12px;padding:10px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;">
<strong style="color:#22c55e;">Key Decision Point:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">The expected classification for the ESG-1 scenario is an <strong>ALERT under EAL RB2.L</strong> (Loss of the RCS Barrier — a single-barrier loss, which is an Alert). The discriminating element is recognizing the <strong>cavitating charging pump</strong>: if the candidate recognizes and trips the cavitating charging pump, the event remains an <strong>ALERT (RB2.L)</strong> on ECG Attachment 2 (EP-SA-111-F2); if the cavitating charging pump is NOT recognized and it fails, the Containment barrier also degrades and the classification escalates to a <strong>Site Area Emergency (SAE) under EAL RB2.L AND CB5.L</strong> on ECG Attachment 3 (EP-SA-111-F3). Description of Event = "Loss of the Reactor Coolant System Barrier" (Alert) or "Loss of the Reactor Coolant System Barrier, Loss of the Containment Barrier" (SAE), per the EAL Description Table. ICMF Section III is checked for whether a radiological release is in progress; Section IV wind speed/direction is retrieved from SPDS. The two regulatory time-critical clocks are: (1) <strong>15 minutes from Start Time to classification/declaration</strong>, and (2) provide the completed ICMF to the Primary Communicator within 15 minutes of Start Time. Missing either window fails the task.</span>
