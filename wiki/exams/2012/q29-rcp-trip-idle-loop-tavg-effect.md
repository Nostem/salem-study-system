<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 1 is operating at 30% power returning from a mid-cycle outage.<br>
- Rod Control is in Manual.<br>
- 14 RCP trips.<br><br>
Which of the following describes how 14 RC Loop Tavg will be affected 5 minutes later with NO operator action when compared to pre-event Tavg in 14 RC Loop?<br><br>
14 RC Loop Tavg will be…
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Lower because backflow from the unaffected loops will cause Tc to rise and equal Thot.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Higher because backflow from the unaffected loops will cause Tc to rise and equal Thot.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Lower because less heat transfer will occur in 14 SG due to the loss of forced flow in that loop.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Higher because less heat transfer will occur in 14 SG due to the loss of forced flow in that loop.</div>
</div>

<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(3) With Rx power &lt;36%, the reactor will not trip upon a loss of a single RCP. 14 RCP will coast down, and flow will reverse in that loop. A differential pressure will exist across a loop in which a RCP is not running due to the head of other RCPs applied to the cold leg side of the vessel. This high pressure (cold leg side) will induce flow in an idle loop in the reverse direction. This means reactor coolant from the cold leg will flow back through the idle RCP and SG. This will lower Tavg in the idle loop.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Th and Tc in the affected loop will reverse, and the original Tc will be greater than Th. The 2 distracters with the Th/Tc equaling are incorrect because Tc will rise &gt;Th in that loop. (A also gives the wrong direction reasoning — Tavg lowers due to reverse flow back through the idle SG, not Tc/Thot equalizing.)</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> The 2 distracters with the Th/Tc equaling are incorrect because Tc will rise &gt;Th in that loop; Tavg lowers, not rises.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> Tavg in the idle loop lowers (reverse flow back through 14 SG), not rises, per the combined explanation.</div>
