<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q83 — LOCA Highest ECG Classification</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">000069A201 / AA2.01 (RO 3.7 / SRO 4.3)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Salem Unit 2 was operating at 100% power when a LOCA occurred.<br>
- Operators are performing actions in 2-EOP-LOCA-1.<br>
- 20 minutes after the Rx was tripped:<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- RWST level is 19 feet and lowering.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- RCS pressure is 350 psig and stable.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Containment pressure peaked at 16 psig, and is now 13 psig and dropping slowly.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- Containment radiation monitors read &lt; 1R / hr.<br>
&nbsp;&nbsp;- RVLIS Full Range is 100%.<br>
- 26 minutes after the Rx was tripped, the RWST lo-lo level alarm is received.<br>
- When checking containment sump level at the beginning of 2-EOP-LOCA-3, the RO reports that containment pressure is now reading 1.5 psig.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies the HIGHEST ECG classification for these conditions?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 4 point Alert.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 5 point Alert.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 7 point Site Area Emergency.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 8 point Site Area Emergency.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> A LOCA which results in RCS pressure lowering to 350 psig will result in subcooling being lost. This yields 5 points due to the loss of the reactor coolant System Barrier. Containment pressure rise followed by rapid, unexplained pressure drop (13 psig to 1.5 psig during a LOCA over 6 minutes) is 3 points for the loss of the containment barrier.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> The 4 point alert is if the loss of subcooling was not recognized and the loss of containment not recognized.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> The 5 point alert is if only the loss of the RCS barrier is recognized.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> The 7 point SAE is if the RCS barrier loss is only thought to be potential and not actual.</div>
