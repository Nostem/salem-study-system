<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">041 K3.02 (RO 4 / SRO 4)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 1 is operating at 100% power when a Main Turbine trip causes a reactor trip.<br>
- The Main Steam Dumps do NOT arm.<br><br>
Which of the following describes the effect this failure to arm will have on the Reactor Coolant System over the next 5 minutes?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> PZR PORVs will open to restore RCS pressure to 2235 psig.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> RCS pressure will rise and PZR spray will keep pressure below 2335 psig.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> RCS Tavg will stabilize at a temperature below 547°F based on reset curve of SG Safety Valves which have lifted.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> RCS Tavg will stabilize at a temperature above 547°F based on 11-14MS10 operation in conjunction with at least one SG Safety Valve having opened.</div>
</div>

<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(4,5,) With a reactor trip, core heat production will lower rapidly. The Steam Generator Atmospheric Reliefs, MS10s, will open to establish RCS temp ~551-552 °F. The RCS pressure will not rise enough to open the PORV's, much less the PZR Safeties, which are meant to relieve a loss of load with the Rx still at power. PZR Spray valves will open rapidly and fully as required to prevent PORV operation. [RCS pressure rises and PZR spray keeps pressure below 2335 psig.]</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Addressed by the combined explanation: the PORVs will not open — RCS pressure does not rise enough to reach the PORV setpoint because PZR spray opens to control pressure.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> Addressed by the combined explanation: the SG Atmospheric Reliefs (MS10s) open to establish RCS temp ~551-552°F; the Tavg distracters with no load temp for 547°F are incorrect.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> Addressed by the combined explanation: the SG Safeties will not open (the MS10 Atmospheric Reliefs relieve and reset before lowering pressure &lt;1005 psig — no load temp is not 547°F).</div>
