<div style="margin-top:12px;padding:10px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;">
<strong style="color:#22c55e;">Key Decision Point:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">The discriminating actions are the <strong>BAST volume and boron-concentration determinations</strong> against <strong>TS 3.1.2.6.a / 3.1.2.6.b and Figure 3.1-2</strong>. The applicant must read 49.5% level / 6610 ppm for each cross-connected BAST and use the TS volume-vs-concentration curve to confirm both are in the acceptable region, concluding the surveillance is <strong>SAT</strong>. The scenario sets the trap that a leak drove the cross-connected BASTs to their low-level alarm, so the applicant must verify the remaining inventory still satisfies TS rather than assuming the source is degraded. Missing a recorded value, mis-reading the TS Figure, or declaring UNSAT when the values are within limits fails the task.</span>
