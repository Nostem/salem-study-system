<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q22 — FRTS-1 Terminate SI and Start RCP Basis</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">WE08 EK1.02 (RO 3.4 / SRO 4.0)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 has experienced a steam line break inside containment.<br>
- Operators have entered FRTS-1, Response to Imminent Pressurized Thermal Shock.<br><br>
Why will the operators be instructed to terminate SI and start RCP(s) if possible?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> The soak required by FRTS-1 requires SI to be secured. RCPs are started to provide the ability to use spray to depressurize the primary.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> The soak required by FRTS-1 requires SI to be secured. RCPs are started to provide mixing of cold SI and warm reactor coolant water.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Safety Injection flow is a significant contributor to any cold leg temperature decrease or overpressure condition and must be terminated. RCPs are started to minimize temperature gradient across S/G tube sheets.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Safety Injection flow is a significant contributor to any cold leg temperature decrease or overpressure condition and must be terminated. RCPs are started to provide mixing of cold SI and warm reactor coolant water.</div>
</div>
