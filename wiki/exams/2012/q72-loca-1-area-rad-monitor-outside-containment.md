<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Which of the following Area Radiation Monitors is one that is checked in 2-EOP-LOCA-1, Loss of Reactor Coolant, when determining if a LOCA outside Unit 2 containment is occurring?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> 2R10A, Personnel Hatch Containment 100'.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 2R34, Mechanical Penetration 100'.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 2R47, Electrical Penetration.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 2R52, Liquid PASS Room.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(11) LOCA-1, step 16, checks for radiation outside containment by looking at 2R4, charging pump area, 2R41D plant vent process, 2R34, 1R3 Radio Chem lab area, 1R6A Sampling room, 1R20B counting room.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A is incorrect because 2R10A (Personnel Hatch Containment 100') is used to assist in determining a LOCA is occurring IN containment, not outside; it is not on the LOCA-1 step 16 list of monitors checked for a LOCA outside containment.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C is incorrect because 2R47 (Electrical Penetration) is not among the monitors (2R4, 2R41D, 2R34, 1R3, 1R6A, 1R20B) checked in LOCA-1 step 16 for a LOCA outside containment.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> D is incorrect because 2R52 (Liquid PASS Room) is not among the monitors (2R4, 2R41D, 2R34, 1R3, 1R6A, 1R20B) checked in LOCA-1 step 16 for a LOCA outside containment.</div>
