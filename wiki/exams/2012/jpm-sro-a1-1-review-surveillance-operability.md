<div style="margin-top:12px;padding:10px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;">
<strong style="color:#22c55e;">Key Decision Point:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">This is an alternate-path surveillance review. The discriminating recognition is that the recorded Minimum Flow of <strong>1638 gpm is wrong</strong> — the performer rounded down instead of rounding up per <strong>P&amp;L 3.4</strong>, so the correct minimum is <strong>1683 gpm</strong>. With the correct minimum, the actual measured Cooling Water Flow of <strong>1675 gpm falls below 1683</strong>, flipping the result from SAT to <strong>UNSAT</strong> — meaning 21 CFCU is NOT operable. The trap is the candidate who accepts the recorded minimum (1638) at face value and concludes the surveillance is SAT (since 1675 &gt; 1638). The candidate must catch the rounding error AND re-evaluate operability against the corrected value.</span>
</div>
