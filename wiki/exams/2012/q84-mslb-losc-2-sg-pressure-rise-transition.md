<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q84 — MSLB LOSC-2 SG Pressure Rise Transition</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">00WE12A201 / EA2.1 (RO 3.2 / SRO 4.0)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 has experienced a MSLB at the Mixing Bottle.<br>
- All attempts at MSLI have failed, and 21-24MS167s remain open.<br>
- Operators have just completed SI termination steps in EOP-LOSC-2, Multiple Steam Generator Depressurization, and PZR level is being maintained stable.<br>
- AFW flow to each SG is 1.0E4 lbm/hr.<br>
- The RO reports rising pressure in 22 SG.<br><br>
Which of the following describes how the CRS should proceed, and why?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Transition to EOP-LOSC-1 and stop RCPs if RCS pressure is &lt;1350 psig, since RCPs cannot be stopped in LOSC-2.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Transition to EOP-LOSC-1, Loss of Secondary Coolant, since one SG is now available for subsequent recovery actions.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Remain in EOP-LOSC-2 since returning to EOP-LOSC-1 will require a transition to EOP-LOCA-1 upon completion.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Remain in EOP-LOSC-2 until positive control can be established over the cooldown after the remaining Steam Generators have fully depressurized, then transition to EOP-LOSC-1.</div>
</div>

<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> LOSC-2 CAS states that upon a pressure rise in any SG except when performing SI termination in Steps 8-20, GO TO EOP-LOSC-1. The stem states that it is after Step 20. LOSC-1 Basis Document, page 7, states that.."Any cooldown operations that are performed as subsequent recovery actions will require at least one nonfaulted SG."</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Incorrect. The source explanation provides a single combined justification (shown in the correct answer above); it does not provide a separate per-distractor rationale. A transition to EOP-LOSC-1 (not remaining in / qualifying LOSC-2) is directed by the LOSC-2 CAS on the SG pressure rise after Step 20.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> Incorrect. The LOSC-2 CAS directs a GO TO EOP-LOSC-1 on the pressure rise — operators do not remain in EOP-LOSC-2.</div>
