<strong>Initial Conditions:</strong><br>
Unit 1 is operating at 100% power.<br>
Radiation Monitor 1R12A – Containment Noble Gas, failed its Source Check when preparing to perform a normal Containment Pressure Relief IAW S1.OP-SO.CBV-0002, Containment Pressure-Vacuum Relief System Operation.<br>
11 CVCS Monitor Tank is ready for release.<br>
14 Gas Decay Tank (GDT) is ready for release.<br><br>
<strong>Initiating Cue:</strong><br>
Prior to initiating the Containment Pressure Relief, Rad Monitor 1R41D, Plant Vent Release Rate, indication fails high, and is confirmed as an instrument failure. Determine the requirements, if any, which must be met to initiate: Containment Pressure Relief; 11 CVCS Monitor Tank release; 14 GDT release. List all compensatory actions, including times they must occur for each release to be initiated. If a release is prohibited from being initiated in this condition then state that. If no additional restrictions apply to any of the releases then state that.

<strong style="color:#22c55e;">Key Decision Point:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;">The discriminating skill is correctly mapping each effluent monitor failure to the right <strong>ODCM Table 3.3-13 instrument and Action</strong>, and distinguishing which pathways are affected. The combined failure of <strong>1R12A (Containment Noble Gas)</strong> and <strong>1R41D (Plant Vent Release Rate)</strong> drives the Waste Gas release into <strong>Action 31</strong> and the Containment Pressure Relief into <strong>Action 37</strong> — each permitting the release only after two independent samples are analyzed AND two qualified staff independently verify the release-rate calculations (and valving lineup for the gas tank). The trap is the <strong>liquid release</strong>: neither failed monitor is in the liquid pathway, so the 11 CVCS Monitor Tank release proceeds with no additional restriction. Applying gaseous-pathway restrictions to the liquid release, or missing the dual-verification compensatory actions, fails the task.</span>
