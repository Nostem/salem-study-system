<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q21 — FRHS-1 RCS Pressure vs SG Pressure Check</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">WE05 EK3.02 (RO 3.7 / SRO 4.1)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
FRHS-1, Response to Loss of Secondary Heat Sink, Step 3 asks, "Is RCS pressure greater than ANY intact or ruptured SG pressure".<br><br>
Which of the following statements is correct if the operator answers NO?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> IMMEDIATELY go to Step 23, Bleed and Feed Initiation, since there is no decay heat removal occurring through the SGs.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Return to Procedure in effect. Attempts to establish a secondary heat sink would be ineffective at reducing RCS temperature since SG pressure is higher than RCS pressure.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Return to Procedure in effect. The RCS has experienced a LOCA large enough that a secondary heat sink is NOT required because decay heat is being removed by break flow.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> IMMEDIATELY trip all RCPs to prevent further loss of reactor coolant through the LOCA, since a LOOP later in the event could cause a more severe loss of reactor coolant or two-phase RCS flow.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> The reason for checking RCS pressure &gt; intact or ruptured SG pressure is to check if there is a need to be worried about a secondary heat sink. if RCS pressure is below SG pressures, then a LOCA of sufficient size is present, and break flow will be removing decay heat, along with ECCS injection.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Distracter A is incorrect because the criteria for going to bleed and feed is SG WR level.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> Distracter B is incorrect because a secondary heat sink could actually be established, and could reduce RCS temperature by dumping steam from the SGs.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> Distracter D is incorrect it is not a CAS of FRHS but is reason for tripping RCPs in TRIP-1 or LOCA-1.</div>
