<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 1 is operating at 45% steady state power.<br>
- All Heater Drain Pumps are O/S.<br>
- All Steam Flows and Feed Flows are 40% and stable.<br>
- NI's indicate 45% on each channel and stable.<br>
- RCS Tavg/Tref deviation is 0.0°F.<br><br>
Which of the following is CLOSEST to the programmed value of SG Feed Delta-P IAW S2.OP-SO.CN-0002, Steam Generator Feed Pump Operation?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">

<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 60 psid.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 80 psid.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 150 psid.</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(4) SG Feed D/P (delta between feed pressure and SG pressure) is controlled by adjusting SGFP speed, and is programmed based on total % Steam Flow. Actual SGFP speed in rpm is only a result, not a controlled parameter. The K/A intent is met by asking how the feed pressure D/P is controlled, which itself controls SGFP speed. 50 is the minimum D/P from 0-15%. 150 psig is the 100% D/P. 60 psid is if candidate used linear scale from 0%-100% steam flow.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> 50 is the minimum D/P from 0-15%.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> 60 psid is if candidate used linear scale from 0%-100% steam flow.</div>
