<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">000015K210 / AK2.10 (RO 2.8 / SRO 2.8)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 is operating at 30% power, steady state.<br>
- OHA D-29, 22 RCP BKR OPEN/FLO LO is received.<br>
- All 22 loop RC flows are 85% and dropping.<br>
- The red START bezel for 22 RCP is illuminated.<br>
- The reactor has NOT tripped.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies what has occurred?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> An ATWT.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 22 RCP shaft has seized.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 22 RCP shaft has sheared.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> The 22RC9, RC FLOW common low press tap isolation valve has developed a leak.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> With a RCP shaft shear, there is no event that would cause the RCP breaker to open. For this reason, that is why the START bezel will still be illuminated, even though loop flows are all dropping. C is correct because a sheared shaft would cause that loop flow to drop, even while the bezel indication showed the breaker is still closed.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Distracter A is incorrect because between 10%(P-10) and 36%(P-8), 1/4 RCS loop lo flow will NOT cause a Rx trip, the coincidence is 2/4.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> Distracter B is incorrect because a seized RCP shaft would cause its supply breaker to trip on overcurrent. The indication in the stem is that the breaker is closed.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> Distracter D is incorrect because there are 3 low pressure flow taps, and 1 common high pressure flow tap. A leak on the common low pressure tap isolation valve would not produce the single-loop flow drop with the breaker still indicated closed as described.</div>
