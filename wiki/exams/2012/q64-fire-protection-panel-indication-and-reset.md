<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 is operating at 100% power when the following occurs:<br>
- OHA A-7, FIRE PROT FIRE.<br>
- 2RP5 Fire Protection Panel, Zone 148 - Work Control Ops Ready Room lamp illuminates, as does the FIRE lamp for that row of alarms.<br>
- The audible coded fire alarm is broadcast over the plant PA system.<br><br>
For these conditions, which of the following identifies:<br>
1. What these indications mean.<br>
2. How the 2RP5 Fire Protection Panel is reset when the condition has cleared.
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> An active fire suppression system (water/CO2/Halon) has activated. Reset from the Control Room.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> An active fire suppression system (water/CO2/Halon) has activated. Reset from the Relay Room.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> A Fire alarm (smoke or heat) has activated. Reset from the Control Room.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> A Fire alarm (smoke or heat) has activated. Reset from the Relay Room.</div>

<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(4) 1) Early warning detectors provide the Control Room with early indication of fire, but do not cause a suppression system to actuate. Early warning Smoke Detectors and Fire Detectors installed in the plant are arranged to alarm to the Control Room by zone. a) If a detector on a zone actuates, the zone indicating light on Panel RP5 will illuminate along with the appropriate group "FIRE" light, and the coded fire alarm assigned to the zone will be broadcast over the station PA system. Once a zone actuates, the alarm remains illuminated until the zone is manually reset from the fire protection panels in the Relay Room.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Early warning Smoke/Fire Detectors do not cause a suppression system to actuate — the indication is a fire alarm (smoke or heat), not an active suppression actuation; and the zone is reset from the Relay Room, not the Control Room.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> The indication is a fire alarm (smoke or heat) from an early warning detector, not an active suppression system actuation (suppression is not caused by the detector).</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> The fire-alarm identification is correct, but the zone is reset from the fire protection panels in the Relay Room, not the Control Room.</div>
