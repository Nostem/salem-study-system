<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q24 — LOCA-5 Minimum SI Flow ECCS Pump Lineup</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">WE11 EA2.02 (RO 3.4 / SRO 4.2)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions for Unit 1:<br><br>
- A reactor trip and SI occurred at 0700 due to a 1500 gpm RCS LOCA.<br>
- RHR system problems have resulted in a loss of recirculation capability.<br>
- Current time is 1300 hours.<br><br>
Conditions present when transitioning to 1-EOP-LOCA-5, Loss of Emergency Recirculation, from 1-EOP-LOCA-1, Loss of Reactor Coolant, due to the loss of recirc capability are:<br><br>
- RCS subcooling is 10°F.<br>
- All RCPs are secured<br>
- 11 and 12 Charging Pumps are running<br>
- BIT flow - 350 gpm<br>
- RVLIS full range 95%<br>
- 11 SI Pump flow - 250 gpm<br>
- 12 SI Pump flow - 250 gpm<br>
- Containment pressure 4.1 psig<br><br>
Which of the following identifies the ECCS pumps that should be run following determination of Minimum SI Flow for Decay Heat Removal?<br><br>
Assume:<br><br>
- Equal flow from each Charging Pump, and each pump will supply half the original total flow if the other charging pump is secured.<br>
- Each SI pump flow remains constant if the other SI pump is secured.<br>
- RCS subcooling remains between 10°F - 45°F for the duration of this question.
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> ONE charging pump and BOTH SI pumps.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> ONE Charging pump and ONE SI pump.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> ONE Charging pump only.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> ONE SI pump only.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> A 1,500 gpm RCS LOCA will deplete the RWST in 2.3 hours. The transfer to CL Recirc will already have been performed. During step 14 of LOCA-5, charging pumps will be reduced to ONE centrifugal, and SI pumps will be reduced to ONE. Starting at Step 19 of LOCA-5, with RCP's secured with &lt;50 degrees subcooling, will use Figure A to determine the ECCS flow required vs. time after trip. 6 hours equals 360 minutes, which is ~225 gpm, but definitely LESS THAN 250 gpm. With the stem stating that charging pump flows remain the same, a single charging pump will be insufficient to supply the required flow. A single SI pump, however, supplying 250 gpm will supply sufficient flow.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Worksheet provides a single combined explanation. Per LOCA-5 steps 14 and 19, ECCS is reduced to ONE charging pump and ONE SI pump, then to the minimum needed for decay heat removal — running BOTH SI pumps is more than the minimum SI flow required.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> Running ONE charging pump in addition to ONE SI pump exceeds the minimum SI flow required for decay heat removal at this time after trip.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> A single charging pump (with charging flow remaining the same per the stem) is insufficient to supply the ~225 gpm (LESS THAN 250 gpm) required at 6 hours after trip per Figure A.</div>
