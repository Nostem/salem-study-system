<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q45 — First Action to Restore Minimum Complement for Containment Integrity</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">022 A1.02 (RO 3.9 / SRO 3.9)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 has experienced a LBLOCA coincident with a loss of off site power.<br>
- 2C 4KV vital bus locked out on bus differential.<br>
- 2B SEC did not actuate.<br><br>
Assuming one train of ECCS equipment is operating, which of the following identifies the FIRST action which will restore the minimum complement of equipment to assure containment integrity is maintained IAW Salem FSAR?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Resetting 2C SEC.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Depressing START PB for 21 CFCU.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Depressing START PB for 22 AND 24 CFCUs.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Rotating key switch to ON for 21 Containment Spray pump.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> 5541.b.(8) FSAR Section 6 and 15 both state that the minimum complement of Containment Spray Pump/CFCUs required to ensure containment integrity along with a train of ECCS in operation is 1 CS pump and 3 CFCUs. With the conditions in the stem, only 21 CS pump will be running on A bus, C bus will be deenergized because a bus differential signal locks out all power to the bus. Additionally, the power supplies to the CFCUs are A,B,C,B,C for 21-25 CFCUs, so only 21 CFCU will be in operation. C is correct because that will restore the 3 CFCUs needed.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A is incorrect because the SEC can't start any loads until the bus has power. [2C SEC feeds the locked-out 2C bus, so resetting it restores nothing.]</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> B is incorrect because it is already running. [21 CFCU is on A bus and already in operation.]</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> D is incorrect because 21 CS pump will already be running.</div>
