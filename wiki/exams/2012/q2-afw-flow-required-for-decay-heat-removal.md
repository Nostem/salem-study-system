<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q2 — AFW Flow Required for Decay Heat Removal</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">007 EK1.06 (RO 3.9 / SRO 3.9)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 1 has performed a manual Rx trip from 100% power.<br>
- No SI was required.<br>
- 11 SG NR level is 9.1% and rising slowly.<br>
- 12-14 SG NR levels are 8.9% and rising slowly.<br>
- Total AFW flow is 24E4 lbm/hr.<br><br>
Which of the following describes what AFW flow is required for decay heat removal?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> At least 22E4 lbm/hr must be maintained until ALL SG NR levels are at least 9%.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> At least 22E4 lbm/hr must be maintained until at least one SG NR level is at least 15%.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> AFW flow may be reduced to less than 22E4 lbm/hr to isolate the SG tube leak indicated on 11 SG.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> AFW flow may be reduced to less than 22E4 lbm/hr because the secondary heat sink requirement is met.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> At step 3 of TRIP-2, Reactor Trip Response, operators are asked if total AFW flow is &gt;22E4 lbm/hr. The bases document states that this amount of flow is..."the minimum safeguards AFW flow requirement for heat removal plus allowances for normal channel accuracy (typically one AFW pump capacity at design pressure.)" The one SG with &gt;9% NR level satisfies the Heat Sink Functional Recovery entry which requires either 22E4 lbm/hr flow OR SG NR level &gt;9%. TRIP-2 states to maintain &gt;22E4 lbm/hr until at least one SG NR level is &gt; 9%, then maintain 9-33%.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Incorrect. The criterion is met when at least ONE SG NR level is &gt;9% (here 11 SG is 9.1%), not when ALL SG NR levels are at least 9%.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> Incorrect. 15% is the adverse-containment value; with no SI and no adverse conditions the 9% NR level (or 22E4 lbm/hr) criterion applies, and is already satisfied.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> Incorrect. The scenario is a post-trip with no SI required; there is no SG tube leak. AFW flow is reducible because the heat sink requirement is met, not to isolate a leak.</div>
