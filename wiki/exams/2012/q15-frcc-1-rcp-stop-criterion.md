<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q15 — FRCC-1 RCP Stop Criterion</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">074 EK3.04 (RO 3.9 / SRO 4.2)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Operators are performing actions in 2-EOP-FRCC-1, Response to Inadequate Core Cooling.<br>
- With no other RCPs in service, 23 RCP has been started IAW direction in FRCC-1 and Rx core temperature is lowering.<br>
- 23 RCP was the only RCP able to be started.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies why 23 RCP would be stopped IAW FRCC-1?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> RVLIS level has risen to &gt;57% which shows that the fuel is covered and injection flow is present.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> ALL SG NR levels have lowered &lt;9% which indicates insufficient heat transfer will be available in any RCS loop.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> At least two RCS Thots have lowered to &lt;350°F which indicates the core is cool and RCP forced circulation is no longer required.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 23 RCP #1 seal D/P has lowered to less than 250 psid which is less than the minimum required to prevent mechanical damage to RCP.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> RVLIS level must be &gt;57% at step 31 and be combined with at least 2 RCS Thots &lt;350 and all RCP's already stopped to exit FRCC-1 to LOCA-1.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> The step to isolate the ECCS accumulators is 28.1, just prior to stopping RCPs at step 29. The ECCS accumulators are isolated after intermittent RHR flow has been verified, since this means they have discharged based on RHR discharge pressure capacity. However, SI or Charging system flow is not checked until AFTER the running RCP is stopped in step 29 when RCS Thots (at least 2) are &lt;350°F.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> As discussed at Step 23, normal conditions for RCP operation are desired, but not required. The only thing that will prevent RCP start is no SG NR level (&lt;9%) based on potential creep failure of the high temperature SG tubes, but LOSS of SGNR level does not require stopping the RCPS.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> There is no concern for RCP damage based on seal D/P.</div>
