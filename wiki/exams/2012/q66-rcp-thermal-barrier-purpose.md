The purpose of the Reactor Coolant Pump (RCP) Thermal Barrier is to...

<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b.(3) The RCP thermal barrier prevents hot reactor coolant from flowing up the shaft to the radial bearing and RCP seal package when normal seal injection flow is lost. The normal flow of cool seal injection water past the radial barrier into the RCS normally performs that function. In the event of a loss of seal injection flow, reactor coolant would flow up across the tubes of the heat exchanger (cooled by CCW) and into the pump to provide the coolant and lubricant for the radial bearing and seals.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A is incorrect because the thermal barrier is not designed as a flow limiter.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> B is incorrect because the Thermal Barrier does not cool seal injection flow.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> D is incorrect because the thrust bearing is not in the controlled leakage seal package.</div>
