<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q34 — Effect of Air Line Failure to RHR HX Outlet FCV</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">005000K410 / K4.10 (RO 3.1 / SRO 3.1)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 is in MODE 5.<br>
- BOTH loops of RHR are in service for Shutdown Cooling.<br>
- RCS temperature is 190°F and stable.<br>
- Each loop is supplying 1800 gpm flow.<br>
- 2RH20 is 10% open.<br>
- Conditions to transition to MODE 4 are NOT met.<br>
- The air line supplying the 21RH18, RHR HX Outlet FCV breaks, and air is lost to 21RH18.<br><br>
Which of the following describes the initial effect this air line failure will have?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> There will be no effect on the RHR system.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> 2RH20 will have to be throttled in the open direction to prevent a RCS cooldown.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 22RH18 will have to be throttled in the closed direction to prevent a RCS cooldown.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 22RH18 will have to be throttled opened to ensure RCS temperature is maintained &lt;200°F.</div>
</div>
