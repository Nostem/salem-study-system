<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q81 — 12 SG NR Channel IV Fails High CRS Response</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">054 AA2.05 (RO 3 / SRO 3.5)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 1 is operating at 45% power.<br>
- 12 SG NR Channel I has been removed from service while undergoing a Channel Calibration IAW S1.IC-CC.RCP-0045, 1LT-529 #12 Steam Generator Level Protection Channel I.<br>
- 12 SG NR Channel IV fails high.<br><br>
Which of the following describes how the CRS should respond?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Enter S2.OP-AB.TRB-0001, Turbine Trip &lt;P-9. Place control rods in manual and insert to lower Rx power &lt;5%.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Enter S2.OP-AB.LOAD-0001, Rapid Load Reduction. Initiate load reduction to less than 50 Mwe.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Enter S2.OP-AB.CN-0001, Main Feedwater / Condensate System Abnormality. Trip the Rx.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Enter 2-EOP-TRIP-1, Reactor Trip or Safety Injection. Verify the Rx has tripped.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> A P-14 signal is generated by 2/3 NR level channels on 12 SG being &gt;67%. The P-14 signal trips the Main Turbine, trips the Main Feed pumps, and shuts the BF19s and 40s and 13s. (FW Isolation signal) The reactor does NOT trip on the Main Turbine trip &lt;P-9 (49% power). C is correct because AB CN directs a Rx trip if power is &gt;P-10 (10%) and a loss of both SGFPs has occurred which it has from the P-14 (2/3 SG NR level channels on 1 SG &gt; 67%).</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A is incorrect because AB.TRB does not direct a Rx trip is both SGFPs are tripped, only states to lower power &lt;5%. This is an incorrect action because it would force an automatic Rx on lo lo SG NR level.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> B is incorrect because the turbine is already tripped.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> D is incorrect because below P-9 the Rx has not tripped.</div>
