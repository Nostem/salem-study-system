<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q25 — LOSC-2 Required Action and Basis</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">00WE12K101 / EK1.1 (RO 3.4 / SRO 3.8)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 1 has experienced a MSLB at the Main Turbine inlet steam piping.<br>
- All attempts at Main Steamline Isolation have failed.<br>
- Operators have transitioned out of 1-EOP-TRIP-1, Reactor Trip or Safety Injection.<br>
- RCS cooldown rate is 120°/hr.<br>
- RCS pressure is 1300 psig and dropping.<br>
- Charging system SI flowmeter indicates 290 gpm.<br>
- The RCS cooldown is NOT being controlled.<br><br>
Which choice identifies an action that must be performed IAW 1-EOP-LOSC-2, Multiple Steam Generator Depressurization, and why?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Trip all RCP's to reduce heat transfer to the SGs.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Reduce AFW to minimize cooldown while still keeping the SG tubes wet.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Stop BOTH RHR pumps to prevent damage to RHR pumps from continued operation above shutoff head.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Send operators to close all BF19's, BF40's, and BF22's to re-establish a secondary pressure boundary in any SG.</div>
</div>

<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> Once out of TRIP-1, no actions other than attempting to close MSLI valve are taken in LOSC-1 prior to going to LOSC-2. Maintaining &gt;1E4 lbm/hr to each S/G keeps tubes from drying out, among other things.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Do not trip RCP's because pressure is still dropping, and the reason is wrong.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> Don't stop RHR pumps because pressure is still dropping, reason is right.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> Doesn't matter if it's uncontrolled or not, we don't close BF22's, but the reason is right.</div>
