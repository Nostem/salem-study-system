<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 is in MODE 5 after refueling the Rx.<br>
- The Rx was shutdown 20 days ago.<br>
- 21 RHR loop is providing shutdown cooling.<br>
- 22 RHR loop is aligned for ECCS.<br>
- 21 RHR HX inlet temperature is 105°F and stable.<br>
- RCS pressure is 205 psig.<br>
- 21 RHR pump begins cavitating due to a valve being mispositioned during a tagging release.<br>
- The CRS enters S2.OP-AB.RHR-0001, Loss of RHR, and stops 21 RHR pump.<br>
- During performance of S2.OP-AB.RHR-0001, the crew has time for normal restoration and local venting of the RHR system.<br>
- 1 hour after the initial loss of RHR, 22 RHR pump is started.<br>
- The RO reports RHR flow is oscillating between 1,500-3,000 gpm.<br>
- The highest CET temperature is 184°F.<br><br>
Which of the following describes how the CRS should proceed?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Start 21 RHR pump and initiate Attachment 7, Hot Leg Injection.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Start 21 RHR pump and initiate Attachment 8, Cold Leg Injection.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Stop 22 RHR pump and initiate Attachment 7, Hot Leg Injection.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Stop 22 RHR pump and initiate Attachment 8, Cold Leg Injection.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> The conditions given in the stem indicate a RCS heatup rate of ~1.3 deg per minute. With the initial temp of 105, this will add 78 degrees and when the RHR pump is started RCS temp will be 183°. RHR flow is required to be stable between 1800-3000 after the venting and starting of the RHR pump to allow exiting the procedure with all other RHR system parameters normal.(page 12) With 1,500 gpm flow swings, this will be answered NO. Step 3.30 states to stop any running RHR pump. Step 3.32 (page 18)states to initiate an alternate method of Decay Heat Removal. With CET temps &lt;200°F, the preferred method is Attachment 8, Cold Leg injection. Attachment 7 Hot leg injection is not preferred due to RCS intact and &lt;200°F. With the procedure stating to stop all RHR pumps, starting 21 is not directed.</div>
