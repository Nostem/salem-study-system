<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q65 — CVI During Containment Vacuum Relief</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">103000A409 / A4.09 (RO 3.1 / SRO 3.7)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Operators are performing a Unit 2 Containment Vacuum Relief IAW S2.OP-SO.CBV-0002, Containment Pressure - Vacuum Relief System Operation.<br>
- 2VC5 and 2VC6 CONT VENT ISO DAMPERS are open.<br>
- The Vacuum Relief Damper is open.<br><br>
A valid Containment Ventilation Isolation signal from the RMS system occurs.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies how this will affect the Containment Vacuum Relief in progress?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> ONLY 2VC5 and 2VC6 will shut.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> ONLY the Vacuum Relief Damper will shut.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> 2VC5, 2VC6, and the Vacuum Relief Damper will shut.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> 2VC5, 2VC6, and the Vacuum Relief Damper remain open (CVI blocked during Vacuum Relief).</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(11) RMS initiated CVI signals close the VC1,4,5,6 and the Pressure Relief and Vacuum Relief Dampers. The CVI can be blocked if present to allow the commencement of the relief, but is NOT present in initial conditions in stem until it occurs after vacuum relief has been started.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A CVI signal closes the Vacuum Relief Damper in addition to 2VC5 and 2VC6 — not ONLY 2VC5 and 2VC6.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> A CVI signal closes 2VC5 and 2VC6 in addition to the Vacuum Relief Damper — not ONLY the Vacuum Relief Damper.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> The CVI can be blocked only if present before commencing the relief; it is NOT blocked in the stem conditions, so the dampers do not remain open — they shut.</div>
