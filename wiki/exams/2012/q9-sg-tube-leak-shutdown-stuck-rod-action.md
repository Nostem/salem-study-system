<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q9 — SG Tube Leak Shutdown Stuck Rod Action</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">G2.4.8 (RO 3.8 / SRO 4.5)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 will be performing a controlled shutdown and cooldown from 100% power due to a 5 gpm tube leak in 22 SG IAW S2.OP-AB.SG-0001, Steam Generator Tube Leak.<br>
- After completing the Immediate Actions of EOP-TRIP-1, Reactor Trip or Safety Injection, following the Rx trip, the RO reports that control rod 2D2 is stuck in the fully withdrawn position.<br><br>
Which of the following identifies the action, if any, the crew will perform in response to the stuck rod?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Initiate a rapid boration for 35 minutes during performance of EOP-TRIP-2.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Initiate a rapid boration for 35 minutes in S2.OP-AB.SG-0001 after exiting the TRIP series procedures.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> No actions are required for a single stuck rod because SDM for the cooldown to 503 degrees is adequate.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> No actions are required for a single stuck rod until the Auto SI Block is performed during RCS depressurization to 1900 psig.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> Step 3.26.H in AB.SG states to trip the turbine, then trip the Rx at 20% power during the shutdown. The 5 gpm size of the leak will allow for a controlled shutdown, and will also allow the crew to transition to TRIP-2. There are no SGTR diagnostic steps in TRIP-2 that would cause a transition to SGTR-1. AB.SG would be re-entered at step 3.27 following exit of TRIP-2, and 3.28 directs rapid boration for each stuck rod for 35 minutes. The rapid boration will be initiated before any depressurization starts in 3.29, so the distracter regarding depressurization is incorrect.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Incorrect. The rapid boration is initiated in AB.SG (step 3.28) after exiting the TRIP series procedures, not during performance of EOP-TRIP-2.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> Incorrect. An action IS required — AB.SG step 3.28 directs a 35-minute rapid boration for each stuck rod; it is not waived for a single stuck rod.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> Incorrect. The rapid boration (step 3.28) is initiated before depressurization starts (step 3.29), so the action is not deferred until the Auto SI Block during depressurization to 1900 psig.</div>
