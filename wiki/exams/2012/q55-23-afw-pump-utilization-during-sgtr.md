<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q55 — 23 AFW Pump Utilization During SGTR</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">061000K103 / K1.03 (RO 3.5 / SRO 3.9)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Salem Unit 2 is operating at 100% power.<br>
- 21 AFW pump is C/T for pump oil bubbler repair.<br>
- A 400 gpm tube rupture occurs on 23 SG.<br>
- The Rx is tripped and a SI initiated successfully.<br>
- 2A 4KV vital bus locks out on Bus Differential.<br><br>
Which of the following describes how 23 AFW pump should be utilized IAW 2-EOP-SGTR-1, Steam Generator Tube Rupture?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Lower 23 AFW pump speed to minimum and trip 23 AFW pump regardless of MDAFW pump status to terminate the unmonitored radioactive release from its steam exhaust.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Lower 23 AFW pump speed to minimum and trip 23 AFW pump. Do not restart until 23MS45 23 SG TO AF PUMP TURB STOP VALVE is shut.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Continue running 23 AFW pump since 22 AFW pump will only be supplying feed to a single SG.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Continue running 23 AFW pump because it is the only source of feed flow to the SGs.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(4,10,13) 23 Turbine Driven AFW pump is supplied from 21 and 23 Main steam lines. Each steam line has a tap upstream of the MSIV. Each line has its own isolation valve (21MS45 and 23MS45) and then its own check valve before the 2 lines combine into one line which feeds the TDAFW pump. During a SGTR, the TDAFW pump remains in service ONLY if it is the SOLE source of feed flow to the SG's. In the stem, 22 AFW pump will be running, since it is powered off of 2B 4KV vital bus, and it has received an auto start signal on SG level. B is correct per Steps 4.4, 4.5 and 4.7 of SGTR-1, which state to lower speed and trip if it is not the sole source of feed, and to not restart until 23MS45 has been shut.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A is incorrect but plausible because there IS an unmonitored release from 23 AFW pump since it exhausts directly to atmosphere, but is only secured if there is another source of feed.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C is incorrect because a single SG being fed is sufficient for heat sink status and 23 AFW pump will not continue to be run.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> D is incorrect because 22 AFW pump is powered from 2B 4KV vital bus and it remains energized. 21 AFW pump is powered from 2A 4KV vital bus which was lost.</div>
