<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q13 — Loss of 1D Vital Instrument Bus Indications on 1RP4</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">057 AA2.03 (RO 3.4 / SRO 3.8)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Salem Unit 1 is in MODE 2 performing a startup by control rods IAW S1.OP-IO.ZZ-0003, Minimum Load to Hot Standby.<br>
- Rx power is stable at 4%.<br>
- Vital Instrument Bus 1D inverter output breaker trips and deenergizes 1D 115VAC Vital Instrument Bus.<br><br>
One minute after the loss of 1D VIB, which of the following contains the indication(s) that will be illuminated on Reactor Status Panel 1RP4, with NO operator action?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Red Reactor Trip lamp.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Yellow RCP busses UV for "H" bus lamp.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Blue Over Power Rod Stop Manual Bypass for CH IV lamp.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Yellow High Flux PRNI CH IV for BOTH High Power and Low Power.</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ D. Correct.</strong> D is correct because the CH IV for BOTH the High power hi flux and low power high flux will be illuminated.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A is incorrect because the reactor has no trip demand from the loss of D VIB.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> B is incorrect because the CH IV indication is associated with "G" 4KV RCP group bus. 4 group buses are H,E,F,G from 11, 12, 13, and 14 RCPs.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C is incorrect because the over power block bypass must be manually aligned.</div>
