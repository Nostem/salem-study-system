<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q99 — Off-Site Fire Assistance Authorization in Radiological Emergency</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">194001G426 / 2.4.26 (RO 3.1 / SRO 3.6)</span>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
An explosion and fire at the RAP tank area has resulted in a possible large spill of radioactive water in the area. An Alert has been declared and all required facilities are activated and staffed. The Fire Department has determined that off-site assistance from the local fire department is needed.<br><br>
IAW S2.OP-AB.FIRE-0001, Control Room Fire Response, which choice identifies who must AUTHORIZE requesting off-site fire department assistance?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Security Duty Supervisor.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Nuclear Fire Protection Supervisor.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> SM / Emergency Duty Officer (EDO).</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Radiological Assessment Coordinator (RAC).</div>
</div>

<details>
<summary style="cursor:pointer;color:#60a5fa;font-size:12px;font-weight:600;padding:8px 0;">▶ Show Answer &amp; Explanation</summary>
<div style="margin-top:10px;padding:16px;background:#0a0e17;border:1px solid #1a2035;border-radius:6px;font-size:12px;">
<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ C. Correct.</strong> CAS ATT. 1, Fire Dept. Support, Caution prior to step 3.0 in AB.FIRE-1 states, "In the event of a radiological emergency, the Nuclear Fire Protection Supervisor should obtain permission from the EDO/SM prior to calling for off-site assistance." A is plausible because security is required to be notified whenever off-site assistance is requested (CAS 2.0). B is plausible because they will be leading the fire brigade and will be the person to request the off-site assistance through the EDO. D is plausible because during an Emergency the RAC is associated with the radiological aspect of the emergency.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A is plausible because security is required to be notified whenever off-site assistance is requested (CAS 2.0), but Security does not authorize the request.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ B.</strong> B is plausible because the Nuclear Fire Protection Supervisor will be leading the fire brigade and will be the person to request the off-site assistance — but in a radiological emergency they must obtain permission from the EDO/SM, who authorize it.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> D is plausible because during an Emergency the RAC is associated with the radiological aspect of the emergency, but the RAC is not the authorizing authority for off-site fire assistance.</div>
