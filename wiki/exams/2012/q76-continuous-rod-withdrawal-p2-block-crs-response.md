<span style="color:#60a5fa;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Q76 — Continuous Rod Withdrawal CRS Response</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">000001A203 / AA2.03 (RO 4.5 / SRO 4.8)</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- Unit 2 has performed a rapid downpower from 60% to 8% during a period of high Circ Water grassing.<br>
- Control rods remain in automatic.<br>
- The Main Turbine remains online.<br>
- Control rods begin to withdraw in automatic.<br>
- Rod speed is 16 spm with Tavg at 545°F.<br><br>
Which of the following describes how should the CRS respond?
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Enter S2.OP-AB.ROD-0003, Continuous Rod Motion, because control rod speed is higher than expected. Place control rods in manual and adjust Main Turbine load as necessary to return Tavg to within 1.5°F of program.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Enter S2.OP-AB.ROD-0003, because control rods should not be withdrawing. Place control rods in manual and adjust as necessary to return Tavg to within 1.5°F of program.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Lower turbine load to restore Tavg to within 1.0°F of program IAW S2.OP-AB.LOAD-0001, Rapid Load Reduction.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Trip the Main Turbine IAW S2.OP-AB.LOAD-0001.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> Rod control would normally be placed in manual upon reaching 20% power in AB.LOAD. The stem states that is has nOT been placed in manual. Permissive P-2 actuates at &lt;15% turbine load, and prevents automatic rod withdrawal. Control rods should NOT b.e withdrawing. B is correct.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> A is incorrect. Even if rod speed higher than expected (expected should be zero and its not), the action is incorrect because ROD-3 says to adjust control rods to restore Tavg, not adjust turbine load.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> C and D are incorrect because the rod withdrawal is unexpected, and should be addressed with the abnormal rod withdrawal procedure, not the load reduction procedure. Additionally, taking actions to adjust or trip the turbine do not address the rod failure.</div>
