<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Given:</strong><br>
Given the following conditions:<br><br>
- A Unit 2 plant startup is in progress.<br>
- Reactor power is stable at 18%.<br>
- The Main Generator is rolling unloaded at 1800 rpm.<br>
- Main Steam Dumps are controlling in AUTO in MS Pressure control.<br><br>
MS Dump Pressure setpoint is lowered 5 psig.<br><br>
With no other operator action, several minutes later you will notice:
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>A.</strong> Reactor Power is &lt;18%.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>B.</strong> Reactor power is &gt; 18%.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>C.</strong> Control rods have stepped in.</div>
<div style="padding:10px 14px;border-left:3px solid #1a2035;margin-bottom:4px;font-size:12px;color:#c8cdd5;background:#0d1220;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;"><strong>D.</strong> Control rods have stepped out.</div>
</div>

<div style="padding:8px 12px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:10px;color:#c8cdd5;"><strong style="color:#22c55e;">✓ B. Correct.</strong> 55.41.b(5) With steam dumps open in MS pressure control auto, lowering the setpoint will cause steam dumps to open to reduce steam header pressure to setpoint pressure. This will cause a higher steam flow and lower temperature, which causes higher Rx power.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ A.</strong> Addressed by the combined explanation: increased steam flow lowers Tavg, which adds positive reactivity (MTC) and raises reactor power above 18%, not below.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ C.</strong> Addressed by the combined explanation: Control rods are not placed in auto until &gt;P-2, which is 15% Turbine power, which is not online yet, so rods will be in manual and no operator action stated in stem.</div>
<div style="padding:8px 12px;border-left:3px solid #f87171;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;margin-bottom:6px;color:#9ca3af;"><strong style="color:#f87171;">✗ D.</strong> Addressed by the combined explanation: Control rods are not placed in auto until &gt;P-2 (15% Turbine power), which is not online yet, so rods remain in manual with no operator action stated.</div>
