<span style="color:#34d399;font-size:14px;font-weight:600;">Scenario 8 — 23 Charging Trip / Letdown Iso Failure / Loss of 2E Group Bus / 21 SGTL → SGTR / Stuck-Open PORV</span>
<span style="font-size:10px;padding:2px 8px;background:#0d1220;color:#6b7280;border-radius:3px;">Simulator | 5 Events | 2 Critical Tasks</span>
</div>

<div style="font-size:13px;color:#9ca3af;margin-bottom:14px;line-height:1.7;">
<strong>Initial Conditions:</strong> Salem Unit 2 in MODE 1 at 100% power, BOL. RCS Boron 1093 ppm; 1220 MWe. Pressurizer Level Channel III failed high last shift, and was removed from service IAW S2.OP-SO.RPS-0003, Placing Pressurizer Channel in Tripped Condition (TSAS 3.3.1.1, action 6, no expiration). 2PR6 is shut with power removed after a control circuit problem caused 2PR1 to partially open momentarily yesterday — no active troubleshooting of 2PR1 is in progress. Polisher is in service. SG Blowdown at 35K per loop to condenser. Unit 1 and Hope Creek at 100% power. No penalty minutes in the last 24 hrs. No discharges in progress. Most-limiting LCO: 3.4.5 action b for 2PR1 (55 hours remain until shutdown required). (Attachment 1 — Unit Two Plant Status; IC-233.)<br><br>
<strong>Turnover:</strong> The crew receives the unit at 100% power BOL. Pressurizer Level Channel III is out of service (removed from service last shift, TSAS 3.3.1.1 act 6). 2PR6 is shut with power removed (control circuit problem yesterday on 2PR1, no active troubleshooting). Most-limiting LCO is 3.4.5 action b for 2PR1 with 55 hours remaining.
</div>

<div style="margin-bottom:14px;">
<strong style="color:#c8cdd5;font-size:12px;">Major Events:</strong>
<ol style="font-size:12px;color:#9ca3af;margin-top:6px;">
<li><span class="hi">23 Charging pump trip with Letdown Isolation failure</span> (2CV4 fails to fully shut, remains 40% open) — crew enters AB.CVC-0001, isolates letdown by shutting 2CV2 and 2CV277, swaps to 22 (or 21) Centrifugal Charging pump, places Excess Letdown in service IAW S2.OP-SO.CVC-0003 if directed, CRS enters TSAS 3.6.3 Action 1 for INOPERABLE Containment Isolation Valve 2CV4</li>
<li><span class="hi">Loss of 2E 4KV Group Bus / Reactor Trip</span> (electrical fault on infeed breaker; OHA A-7 FIRE PROT FIRE for Zone 33 Turb Bldg West El 100; loss of 22 RCP causes RCS Low Flow Rx trip) — RO performs immediate actions of EOP-TRIP-1; SI not required; CRS transitions to EOP-TRIP-2; PO stops 21 and 22 SGFPs</li>
<li><span class="hi">21 SG Tube Leak (50 gpm)</span> — only 2R15 condenser air ejector rad monitor provides indication (R19 SGBD monitors O/S due to AFW auto-start blowdown isolation; R53 N-16 monitors ineffective with Rx S/D); CRS enters AB.SG-0001 in parallel with EOP-TRIP-2; chemistry identifies elevated activity in 21 SG; PO sets 21MS10 setpoint to 1045 psig and closes 21MS7, 21MS18, 21GB4; CRS dispatches operator to shut 21MS45</li>
<li><span class="hi">21 SG Tube Rupture</span> (leak escalates to 400 gpm) — RO initiates SI; Train B SI fails to actuate, RO successfully initiates Train A SI; immediate actions of EOP-TRIP-1; transition to EOP-SGTR-1; PO closes 21MS167 (and verifies 21MS7, 21MS18, 21GB4 closed) — <strong>CT#1</strong>; PO trips 23 AFW pump (since not the only source); cooldown to target temperature using steam dumps in MS Pressure Control at 25%; <strong>CT#2 — establish and maintain RCS temperature within target band</strong></li>
<li><span class="hi">Stuck-Open 2PR2 PORV after RCS depressurization</span> — 21 RCP trips during PZR spray (loss of normal spray capability since 23 RCP also stopped on 2E bus loss); RO opens 2PR2 PORV to depressurize RCS; 2PR2 fails to close when demanded; CRS directs RO to close PORV Block valve; RCS pressure rising; scenario terminates</li>
</ol>
</div>

If CRS directs Excess Letdown placement: PO verifies 2CC113 open, opens 2CC215, verifies 2CV132 shut, determines 2CV134 selected to VCT, opens 2CV278 and 2CV131, slowly throttles open 2CV132 while maintaining excess letdown temp &lt;195°F and pressure &lt;150 psig.</td>

<td style="padding:6px;color:#9ca3af;">PO reports 21 SG pressure is stable or rising. RO reports subcooling. RO reports PZR spray availability. (Note: 21 RCP is running. If crew decides normal spray is not available due to 23 RCP not running, they go directly to PORV depressurization actions. During validation spray was determined ineffective with 21 RCP running.) Crew reviews depressurization termination criteria using Table D. RO fully opens both PZR spray valves if normal spray is available. RO reports spray is lowering pressure OR reports pressure is not lowering. <strong>21 RCP electrical trip is inserted</strong> when spray is attempted to be used for pressure reduction. RO reports 21 RCP has tripped. CRS returns to step 19 and answers NO. RO closes both spray valves. RO reports 2PR2 is available. Crew reviews depressurization termination criteria using Table E. RO opens one PORV (2PR2) to depressurize RCS until termination criteria is met. <strong>2PR2 fails to close when demanded.</strong> RO reports 2PR2 will NOT close. CRS directs RO to close the PORV Block valve. RO reports PORV block valve is closed and RCS pressure is rising. Scenario terminates after the open PORV issue has been addressed.</td>

<div style="margin-top:12px;padding:10px;background:#052e16;border-left:3px solid #22c55e;border-radius:0 4px 4px 0;">
<strong style="color:#22c55e;">Critical Tasks:</strong><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;"><strong>CT#1 (CT-18):</strong> Isolate feed flow into and steam flow from 21 SG prior to a transition to SGTR-3 being required. <em>Basis:</em> Failure to isolate the ruptured SG causes a loss of differential pressure between the ruptured SG and the intact SGs. The fact that the crew allows the differential pressure to dissipate and, as a result, are then forced to transition to a contingency ERG constitutes an incorrect performance that "necessitates the crew taking compensating action that would complicate the event mitigation strategy."</span><br><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;"><strong>CT#2 (CT-19):</strong> Establish and maintain an RCS temperature so that transition from SGTR does not occur because temperature is too high to maintain minimum subcooling, or so low it causes transition to FRTS or FRSM. <em>Basis:</em> Failure to establish and maintain the correct RCS temperature during a SGTR leads to a transition from E-3 to a contingency ERG. This failure constitutes an incorrect performance that "necessitates the crew taking compensating action that would complicate the event mitigation strategy."</span><br><br>
<span style="color:#c8cdd5;font-size:11px;color:#6b7280;"><em>CT numbers in parentheses are the corresponding Westinghouse ERG Rev. 2-based Critical Task procedure WCAP-17711-NP.</em></span>
