# SC.OP-AB.ZZ-0001 — Adverse Environmental Conditions

> DRAFT — Reconstructed from exam materials

## Purpose

Site (common) abnormal procedure entered when adverse environmental conditions threaten plant operation — high winds, river grass / detritus loading, low river level, marine ingress, or biofouling. It directs evaluation of the Circulating Water Degradation Index (CWDI) and protective mode reductions when degradation of the ultimate heat sink (circulating / service water intake) is predicted.

## Key Actions / Information

The procedure (Attachment 3, Circulating Water Degradation Index) tallies points from three contributors over the next 8 hours and assigns a color rating that dictates required plant Mode:

- **Detritus Points** — based on the rolling weekly detritus (river grass) loading.
- **Circulator Points** — based on number of circulating water pumps available / out of service.
- **Wind Speed Points** — accrue each hour that sustained wind speeds of >30 mph from sector between 140 degrees (SE) and 240 degrees (WSW) are predicted to occur within the next 8 hours.
- **River Level Points** — based on predicted river level each hour.
