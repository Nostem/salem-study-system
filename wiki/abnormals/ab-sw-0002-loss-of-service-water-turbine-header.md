# AB.SW-0002 — Loss of Service Water-Turbine Header

## Purpose

Abnormal procedure for a loss of the Turbine Generator Area (TGA) Service Water header. Directs operators to control the 2ST901 (TURB LO CLR ST RET VLV) and 2ST1 (TG AREA SW PRESS CONT VLV), and ultimately to remove the Main Turbine from service to isolate the TGA header if required.

## Key Actions / Information

**Exam & operating coverage:**

<div class="callout callout-exam">
