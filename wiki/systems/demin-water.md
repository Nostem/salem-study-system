# Demin Water

## Function & Design Basis

The Demineralized Water Makeup System produces the makeup water required for the high-purity water systems in the station from demineralizers (UFSAR §9.2.3). Water from wells on the station property is used as makeup to the Demineralizer System (UFSAR §9.2.3).

## Key Components

- **Demineralizers:** Produce the high-purity makeup water for the station's high-purity water systems from station well water (UFSAR §9.2.3).
- **Demineralized water storage tanks:** Two <span class="hi">500000-gallon</span> outdoor demineralized water storage tanks are provided; shown on Plant Drawings 205213 and 205246 (UFSAR §9.2.3).
