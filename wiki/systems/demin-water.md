# Demin Water

## Function

Provides demineralized water for plant makeup and chemistry control.

## Alternate AFW Suction Source

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2016 Q53</div>
Demin Water is one of three <span class="hi-exam">alternate suction sources for the AFW pump suction header</span> (along with Fresh Water / Fire Protection and Service Water). Alignment is through the <span class="hi-exam">normally jacked-closed AF52 Alternate Suction Valves</span> per S2.OP-SO.AF-0001 Section 5.10.
</div>
