# 115V AC

## Function

Vital instrument buses provide regulated AC power to protection system instrumentation. Fed from inverters under normal operation with automatic transfer to regulated AC supply as backup. (UFSAR 8.3.1.4)

## 115V AC Instrumentation Power

- Vital instrument buses provide power to protection system instrumentation
- Fed from inverters (normal) with automatic transfer to regulated AC supply (backup)
(UFSAR 8.3.1.4)

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2015 Q14</div>
2B VIB UPS source transfers: the static switch is moved from Normal to Alternate by placing the <span class="hi-exam">Test Transfer switch to ALT (SO.115-0012 Section 5.4)</span>; the Manual Bypass Switch in <span class="hi-exam">Bypass to Alternate</span> positions contacts <span class="hi-exam">B1, B2, B4 closed and B3, B5 open</span>. With the static switch in Bypass to Alternate, the VIB is fed from the <span class="hi-exam">AC Line Regulator (Alternate source)</span> — so if the 2B 230VAC-to-AC-Line-Regulator breaker opens, the <span class="hi-exam">2B VIB DEENERGIZES</span>. <span class="hi-trap">Automatic transfer is NOT available (static switch not in Normal), and the inverter output cannot reach the bus with B3/B5 open. Restoration is MANUAL: place the Static Switch in Normal and the Test Transfer switch to N (Sections 5.7.3/5.7.4) — NOT Isolate (Alternate).</span>
</div>

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2020 Q12</div>
Loss of 2A 115V Vital Instrument Bus affects VCT level instrument LT-112 (loss of indication, alarms, and auto makeup). LT-114 remains available but <span class="hi-exam">only via the plant computer</span> in the control room (NOT on the control console) and at Panel 216 in charging pump alley.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2020 Q88</div>
Loss of 2B VIB impact on ESFAS: Containment Spray Hi-Hi Containment Pressure bistables are <span class="hi-exam">energized to actuate</span>. Loss of the 2B VIB causes the associated bistable to remain de-energized → <span class="hi-exam">logic goes from 2/4 to 2/3</span>. Per TS 3.3.2.1, the inoperable channel is <span class="hi-exam">BYPASSED (not tripped)</span> to prevent spurious CS actuation. <span class="hi-trap">Most ESF bistables are de-energized to trip → tripped on inoperability. CS bistables are the exception — energized to actuate → bypassed on inoperability.</span>
</div>

<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2019 Q34</div>
Steam Dump I/P converters are powered by <span class="hi-exam">C/D 115 VAC Instrument Bus</span>. Loss of 115 VAC to the I/P converters causes steam dumps to fail closed.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2019 Q89</div>
LCO 3.8.2.1 with 2 inverters inoperable: LCO 3.8.2.1 addresses only <span class="hi-exam">one inverter inoperable (Required Action A)</span>. With <span class="hi-exam">2 inverters inoperable, there is no applicable Required Action → LCO 3.0.3 applies</span>. From the time the 2nd inverter becomes inoperable, the crew has 1 hour to begin + 6 hours to reach Hot Standby = <span class="hi-exam">7 hours maximum</span>. <span class="hi-trap">Trap: do not use the 30-hour restoration time from Required Action B — that applies only to one inoperable inverter. With two, LCO 3.0.3 is the default.</span>
</div>

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2016 Q20</div>
Loss of 2A 115VAC Vital Instrument Bus impact on AFW: <span class="hi-exam">23AF21 and 24AF21 fail closed (pressure override) because power to the AFW pump discharge pressure transmitter is lost</span>, and <span class="hi-exam">power is also lost to the AF21 valve controllers</span>. AB.115-0001 Step 13 dispatches an NEO to locally throttle 23AF21 and 24AF21. <span class="hi-trap">PRESSURE OVERRIDE DEFEAT cannot recover AF21 control because the loss of VIB also de-energizes the valve controllers themselves.</span>
</div>

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2016 Q39</div>
Vital Instrument Bus naming and SEC mapping: each train SEC (A, B, C) is powered from its respective Vital Instrument Bus — <span class="hi-exam">2AVIB24, 2BVIB27, 2CVIB9</span>. With SEC BLOCK switches inoperable, deenergizing a VIB removes the SEC inhibit so that a piece of equipment held off by an unsatisfied SEC permissive can be started locally. To start 21 Charging Pump (B), 21 SI Pump (A), and #2 ECAC (C), all three VIBs must be deenergized.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2015 Q42</div>
The 2B Vital Instrument Bus powers SSPS Train B; if it is deenergized, <span class="hi-exam">SSPS Train B slave relays would not actuate on a Safety Injection signal</span>. (SSPS Train A 45VDC power is supplied from A and D vital power, so Train A is unaffected.) See [[RPS/SSPS]].
</div>
