# TAC

## Function & Design Basis

The Turbine Auxiliaries Cooling System provides cooling water for the turbine generator auxiliary components (UFSAR §10.2.7). It is a single closed loop employing condensate-quality water as the coolant (UFSAR §10.2.7). The system circulates the coolant through the shell side of the main heat exchangers, where its heat is given up to service water on the tube side (UFSAR §10.2.7).

The components cooled by the system are (UFSAR §10.2.7):

1. Generator hydrogen coolers
2. Generator stator water coolers
3. Generator exciter coolers
4. Generator seal oil coolers
5. Turbine electro-hydraulic control fluid coolers
6. Gland seal steam condenser
7. Main bus air cooler
8. Feedwater sample coolers
9. Bleed steam coil drain pump mechanical seal coolers
10. Heater drain pump stuffing box water jacket
11. Condensate and heater drain pump motor upper/lower bearing coolers
12. Bleed steam coil drain tank pump lube oil coolers
13. Vacuum pump seal water coolers

## Key Components

The system consists of the following major components (UFSAR §10.2.7):

- **Makeup and expansion tank** — provides a makeup water source for the system from the main condensate cycle (UFSAR §10.2.7).
- **Main heat exchangers** — coolant flows through the shell side; heat is rejected to service water on the tube side (UFSAR §10.2.7).
- **Pumps** — circulate the coolant through the shell side of the main heat exchangers (UFSAR §10.2.7).

## Operation

The pumps circulate the condensate-quality coolant through a single closed loop. The coolant passes through the shell side of the main heat exchangers, where its heat is transferred to service water on the tube side (UFSAR §10.2.7). The makeup and expansion tank provides a makeup water source for the system from the main condensate cycle (UFSAR §10.2.7). The system is shown on Plant Drawing 205210 (UFSAR §10.2.7).
