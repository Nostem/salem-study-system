# SPAV

## Function & Design Basis

The Switchgear and Penetration Area Ventilation (SPAV) System is designed to maintain safe levels of temperature and cleanliness in the rooms it serves; standby equipment is included to assure these design conditions are maintained (UFSAR §9.4.6.1). The system is seismic Class I, and the seismic design and analysis methodologies used to qualify the ductwork and contained equipment are described in UFSAR §3.8.4.4.1 (UFSAR §9.4.6.1).

The system is designed to maintain the areas it serves between a temperature range of <span class="val-normal">65°F-110°F</span>, except for the following areas (UFSAR §9.4.6.1):

| Area | Temperature limit |
|------|-------------------|
| El. 64' Security Battery Room | <span class="val-normal">115°F</span> |
| El. 64' Elevator Machinery Room | (See note) |
| El. 84' Main Corridor | <span class="val-normal">115°F</span> |
| El. 84' Unit 1 Switchgear Room | <span class="val-normal">115°F</span> |

Note: the El. 64' Elevator Machinery Room limit is not applicable because there is no safety-related equipment in that area (UFSAR §9.4.6.1).

The design basis outdoor air temperature is <span class="val-normal">0°F</span> (winter) and <span class="val-normal">95°F</span> (summer). A 10°F daily temperature variation may be used for summer operations in accordance with ASHRAE methodology. These temperature values satisfy more than 99 percent of the conditions experienced at the site area annually (UFSAR §9.4.6.1).

## Key Components

An independent Ventilation System is provided for each unit's Switchgear Rooms, located on Elevations 84 feet and 64 feet, the Mechanical Penetration Area (El. 100'), and the Electrical Penetration Area (El. 78') of the Auxiliary Building. Most of the equipment is located in the penetration areas at Elevation 100 feet of each unit, and all ventilated areas and equipment are enclosed in seismic Class I structures. The Switchgear Ventilation System is shown on Plant Drawings 205248 and 205348 (UFSAR §9.4.6.2.1).

The ventilation system for each unit consists of the following equipment (UFSAR §9.4.6.2.2):

1. One 100-percent-capacity supply-air filtering unit having 70-82 percent ASHRAE weight arrestance efficiency to filter the outside and recirculated air (UFSAR §9.4.6.2.2).
2. Three 50-percent-capacity supply fans, supplying filtered air through supply ducts to the various areas (UFSAR §9.4.6.2.2).
3. Two 100-percent-capacity return/exhaust air fans, used to either exhaust air out of the switchgear rooms or return air for recirculation (UFSAR §9.4.6.2.2).
4. Two exhaust fans for the Mechanical Penetration Area (Elevation 100 feet) and Electrical Penetration Area (Elevation 78 feet) (UFSAR §9.4.6.2.2).
5. Three recirculation cooling units serving the north (mechanical) penetration area at Elevation 100 feet, each consisting of a filter, cooling coil, and fan (UFSAR §9.4.6.2.2).
6. Control and instrumentation (UFSAR §9.4.6.2.2).
7. Distribution ductwork and dampers for the supply air, exhaust air, or recirculation air (UFSAR §9.4.6.2.2).

The original system writeup also identifies a supply air roll filter and enclosure, recirculation duct, and control dampers as part of the SPAV System (UFSAR §9.4.6.2.1).

## Operation

Normally <span class="val-normal">two of the three supply fans operate with the third fan as a standby</span>. One of the return/exhaust fans operates with the second fan in standby, and both of the penetration area exhaust fans operate (UFSAR §9.4.6.2.2). Supply temperature is monitored, and the outside air intake dampers, recirculation damper, and exhaust damper modulate to provide a relatively constant supply air temperature to all areas of the system (UFSAR §9.4.6.2.2).

Temperature-based alignment (UFSAR §9.4.6.2.2):

- When supply air temperature is <span class="val-normal">≥ 75°F</span>, the system aligns to supply 100% outside air — the recirculation damper closed and the intake and exhaust dampers fully open.
- When supply air temperature is between <span class="val-normal">65°F and 75°F</span>, the intake dampers, recirculation damper, and exhaust damper modulate to temper the supply as required to maintain temperature.
- When supply air temperature is <span class="val-normal">≤ 65°F</span>, both penetration area exhaust fans stop; the two supply fans and the switchgear return/exhaust fan continue to operate at full flow, the recirculation damper is 100% open, and the outside air intake dampers are 100% closed. By procedure, the access door of the other unit's SPAV air handling unit housing is opened to equalize pressure in the electrical penetration area and mechanical equipment rooms.

Both switchgear rooms (El. 64' & 84') and the electrical penetration area (El. 78') are provided with a fire detection system. If the Fire Protection System is initiated for one of these areas, the supply and exhaust dampers for the affected area close. For the switchgear rooms, the main recirculating dampers close and the system operates as a once-through system supplying 100 percent fresh air; this prevents the tripping of other smoke detectors by recirculated smoky air. The ventilation system also provides a means for purging smoke from the fire area as soon as air supply is re-established (UFSAR §9.4.6.2.2).

Design evaluation: the SPAV ventilation equipment is enclosed in a seismic Class I structure. The Equipment Room is directly accessible from the Relay Room of the control area. All other areas in the vicinity of the Switchgear Rooms are ventilated by systems completely independent of the SPAV System, so fire and smoke generated in such other areas would not impair the integrity or accessibility of the Switchgear Rooms (UFSAR §9.4.6.3).

Tests and inspections: the SPAV system is inspected, tested, and balanced upon installation, and normal operation serves as a continuous check on system operation. Operation of the dampers, including those associated with initiation of the Carbon Dioxide Fire Protection System, can be checked periodically (UFSAR §9.4.6.4).
