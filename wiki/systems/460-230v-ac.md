# 460/230V AC

## Function

Steps down 4160V vital bus power via unit substations to feed motors, MOVs, heaters, and smaller loads throughout the plant. (UFSAR 8.3.1.3)

## 460V/230V Systems

- Step-down from 4160V vital buses via unit substations
- Feed motors, MOVs, heaters, and smaller loads
- Pressurizer heater buses (EP and GP) backed by EDGs for natural circulation support
(UFSAR 8.3.1.3)

PZR 22 Backup Heater Group transfer to 2A 460V Vital Bus (emergency feed). Initial conditions: <span class="hi-exam">2A EDG carrying 2A Vital Bus loaded to 2400 KW</span>. Sequence per S2.OP-SO.PZR-0010 Section 5.3: verify normal feed breaker <span class="hi-exam">2EX1EP2EPX (2EP 480V PRESSURIZER HEATER BUS MAIN BREAKER)</span> is OPEN at 78' Electrical Penetration; remove its Corry interlock key (tab must be pushed in to rotate); place 11 of 14 disconnects OFF (only 3 remain ON); place <span class="hi-exam">2AX1AX14X-1</span> emergency feed disconnect ON (78' Electrical Penetration, JAM key); insert interlock key into 2AX1AX14X (84' Swgr Rm) to enable emergency feed. The Corry Key Interlock between 2EX1EP2EPX and 2AX1AX14X enforces single-source feed (mutually exclusive breaker positions).

<div class="callout callout-exam">

A trip of 2C4D de-energizes the running ECAC. <span class="hi-trap">Trap: Station Air Compressors are powered from Group buses — breakers 2A4D, 2E6D, and 2H5D feed Group buses (Station Air Compressor power), not the ECACs.</span>

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2016 Q45</div>
Hydrogen Recombiner power supplies (Unit 1): <span class="hi-exam">11 Hydrogen Recombiner is powered from 1A 460V Vital Bus; 12 Hydrogen Recombiner is powered from 1B 460V Vital Bus</span>. The 1A and 1B 460V Vital Buses are powered from their respective 4KV Vital Buses. With <span class="hi-exam">1A 4KV Vital Bus locked out on bus differential, 1A 460V loses power and 11 Recombiner is unavailable — only 12 Recombiner is available</span>. Reference: 1A and 1B Aux Building 460V Bus One-line drawings (601231, 601232).
</div>

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2015 Q47</div>
The Containment Iodine Removal Units (IRUs) are powered from the <span class="hi-exam">G and E non-vital 460VAC buses</span>. On a loss of offsite power the non-vital 460V buses deenergize, so <span class="hi-exam">neither 11 nor 12 IRU is available</span> (the loading state of a vital bus in Mode IV has no bearing on IRU operation). See [[Containment]].
</div>

<div class="callout callout-jpm">
<div class="callout-label">JPM — 2014 IP-j</div>
The 2EP 480V PZR Heater Bus can be fed from the <span class="hi-exam">2A 460V Vital Bus (EDG-backed) emergency feed breaker 2AX1AX14X</span> (84' Swgr Rm) when the normal group-bus supply is lost. The normal main breaker is <span class="hi-exam">2EX1EP2EPX</span> (78' Electrical Penetration). A Corry key interlock allows only one source at a time — the key must be removed from the de-energized 2EX1EP2EPX before it can be inserted to unlock the emergency feed 2AX1AX14X. To stay within vital bus capacity, only three of fourteen heater disconnects remain ON when on the emergency supply.
</div>
