# S2.OP-ST.DG-0003 — 2C Diesel Generator Surveillance Test

## Purpose

Surveillance test procedure for the 2C Emergency Diesel Generator. Its P&L documents the EDG load-limit schedule used to evaluate allowable run time at a given KW load.

## Key Actions / Information

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2015 Q55</div>
EDG load limitations (P&L 3.5) are maximum of: <span class="hi-exam">2600 KW continuous; 2600–2750 KW for 2000 hours; 2750–2860 KW for 30 minutes; 2860–3100 KW for 2 hours</span>. Operating at <span class="hi-exam">2610 KW</span> falls in the 2600–2750 KW (2000-hour) band, so 21 more hours has no adverse effect — with cumulative time for all load limits &lt;10%, ~210 hours remain available for that band.
</div>
