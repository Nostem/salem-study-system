# S1.OP-SO.WG-0011 — Discharge of 14 Gas Decay Tank

## Purpose

Procedure for releasing the contents of 14 Waste Gas Decay Tank (WGDT) to the plant vent in a controlled manner.

## Key Actions / Information

**Exam & operating coverage:**

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2016 Q98</div>
During a 14 WGDT release IAW S1.OP-SO.WG-0011: <span class="hi-exam">only a single WGDT is allowed to be released at a time</span> (page 3); <span class="hi-exam">the release procedure specifically allows a VCT purge to plant vent to occur during the WGDT release</span> (page 16); <span class="hi-exam">the release procedure specifically disallows transfer of gas between tanks when another tank is being released</span> (page 3). Waste LIQUID can be transferred between units, but waste GAS cannot.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2020 Q98</div>
During a GDT release, the following restrictions apply:
<ul>
<li>P&L 3.2: <span class="hi-exam">"DO NOT release more than one GDT at a time."</span></li>
<li>P&L 3.3: <span class="hi-exam">"DO NOT transfer Waste Gas from one GDT to another during the GDT Release."</span></li>
</ul>
A VCT Purge to the plant vent IS allowed during a GDT release — the discharge procedure does not prohibit it. <span class="hi-trap">Trap: waste gas cannot be transferred between units or between GDTs during a release, but VCT purge is permitted.</span>
</div>

<div class="callout callout-jpm">
<div class="callout-label">JPM — 2016 SRO-A3</div>
With 1R41D Plant Vent Release Rate failed AND no full operable channel set, ODCM Table 3.3-13 Instrument #1 (Waste Gas Holdup System) <span class="hi-exam">Action 31</span> applies. Release IS permitted, but the SRO must direct: <span class="hi-exam">(a) at least two independent samples of the tank contents are analyzed AND (b) at least two technically qualified members of the facility Staff independently verify the release rate calculations and discharge valving lineup</span>; otherwise suspend release of radioactive effluents via this pathway. The procedure also adds: per S1.OP-SO.WG-0011 Attachment 2 §3.0 Step 3.4.C (page 18), grab samples are obtained at least once per <span class="hi-exam">8 hours</span> AND analyzed for gaseous principal gamma emitters within <span class="hi-exam">24 hours</span>.
</div>
