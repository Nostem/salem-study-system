# S1.OP-ST.CS-0003 — Inservice Testing Containment Spray Valves

## Purpose

Provides the 92-day Inservice Test (IST) of the Unit 1 Containment Spray valves, including stroke timing of the tank discharge valves (Section 5.2) and the Spray Additive Tank (eductor) line flush in Attachment 3.

## Key Actions / Information

### Attachment 3 — CS Eductor Line Flushing

<div class="callout callout-jpm">
<div class="callout-label">JPM — 2015 IP-i</div>
Attachment 3 eductor flush sequence: ensure 1CS16 and 1CS17 (TANK DISCH, normally shut) are CLOSED, then <span class="hi-exam">UNLOCK AND OPEN 1CS31 (RWST SUPPLY TO EDUCTORS STOP VALVE)</span> and OPEN 1CS40 (SPRAY ADD TK DISCH LINE DRN). Flush to the drain header for <span class="hi-exam">3 minutes</span>, CLOSE 1CS40, then THROTTLE 1CS61 (CS SPRAY ADD TK DISCH SAMP VALVE) directing sample flow to a floor drain. Direct Chemistry to sample for sodium; <span class="hi-exam">WHEN Chemistry verifies &lt;10 ppm sodium</span>, ensure 1CS61 CLOSED and CLOSE AND LOCK 1CS31, then notify the Control Room. The Spray Additive Tank fluid is <span class="hi-exam">Sodium Hydroxide [NaOH] 0-50%</span>, so RED-category chemical PPE (per SA-AA-2113) is required when PPE requirements are RED.
</div>
