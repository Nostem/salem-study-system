# S2.OP-SO.WG-0003 — Gaseous Waste Disposal System Operation

## Purpose

Provides operation of the Gaseous Waste Disposal System, including placing a Gas Decay Tank (GDT) in service or standby and removing a GDT from service (Section 5.2).

## Key Actions / Information

### Section 5.2 — Swapping the In-Service Gas Decay Tank

**Exam & operating coverage:**

<div class="callout callout-jpm">
<div class="callout-label">JPM — 2015 IP-k</div>
Placing 24 GDT in service and removing 21 GDT (Section 5.2). Step 5.2.7.A — before placing 24 GDT in service, ENSURE: 24 GDT release NOT in progress; <span class="hi-exam">24 GDT pressure &gt;10 psig AND &lt;92 psig (2PIS1039)</span>; and 24WG31 (GDT INLET VALVE) is OPEN. Step 5.2.7.B — <span class="hi-exam">PRESS GAS DECAY TANK 24 MANUAL SELECT pushbutton (104 panel)</span> (24 in-service light ON, 21 OFF), ensure 24WG29 (GDT INLET CONT VALVE) OPEN, <span class="hi-exam">CLOSE WG35 (GDT STOP VALVE TO HUT) for the GDT removed (21WG35)</span>, and OPEN 24WG35. Step 5.2.2 — when removing 21 GDT, ensure the Gas Decay Tank Standby Selector switch (105 panel) is NOT in "21" and CLOSE 21WG35.
</div>
