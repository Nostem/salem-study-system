# S2.OP-ST.SJ-0006 — Inservice Testing Safety Injection Valves Mode 6

## Purpose

Mode 6 inservice test of Safety Injection valves, including the forward-flow test of the ECCS Accumulator discharge check valves (xxSJ55 / xxSJ56). Performed with the reactor vessel head removed and the refueling cavity flooded. Each accumulator is isolated, then its outlet valve (xxSJ54) is opened and the accumulator blown down to the RCS through its check valves while the blowdown time is measured with a stopwatch.

## Key Actions / Information

**Exam & operating coverage:**

<div class="callout callout-jpm">
<div class="callout-label">JPM — 2012 Sim-b</div>
Forward-flow test method per Attachments 2/3: close the accumulator outlet valve (e.g. 2AY2EP2D, 21SJ54), open it, and time the blowdown from start until accumulator pressure reaches <span class="hi-exam">35 psig</span>. Accumulator Blowdown Time (C) = Stopwatch Reading (A) − Disk Movement Time (<span class="hi-exam">1.15 seconds</span>). An Independent Verification of the calculation is required. The 22 Accumulator check valves (22SJ55/22SJ56) are graded <span class="hi-exam">UNSAT when blowdown time is &gt;27.79 seconds</span>.
</div>
