# S2.OP-IO.ZZ-0009 — Defueled to Refueling

## Purpose

Integrated Operating Procedure for transitioning Unit 2 from Defueled (all fuel removed from reactor vessel) to Refueling (Mode 6) during a refueling outage.

## Key Actions / Information

**Exam & operating coverage:**

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2016 Q95</div>
<span class="hi-exam">Step 5.1.6 specifies that Mode 6 is entered when the first fuel assembly is lowered into the reactor vessel.</span> <span class="hi-trap">Trap: Mode 6 is NOT entered when fuel movement is "imminent" (the word "imminent" is used in IOP-3 for entering MODE 2 when Control Bank A withdrawal is imminent), NOT when the first assembly is moved over the vessel, and NOT when the SM signs Attachment 1 authorizing Mode 6 entry.</span>
</div>
