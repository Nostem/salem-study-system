# S2.OP-SO.SF-0009 — Refueling Operations

## Purpose

System operating procedure governing fuel movement (core reload/offload) in the reactor during a refueling outage, including the fuel-handling equipment prerequisites and instrument requirements that must be continuously satisfied during core alterations.

## Key Actions / Information

**Exam & operating coverage:**

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2014 Q68</div>
P&amp;L 3.13 specifically requires <span class="hi-exam">suspension of transfer of fuel into the SFP when either the 21 or 22 SFP Cooling pump becomes INOPERABLE</span>. Loss of all oil in the bearing oil reservoir renders an SFP cooling pump INOPERABLE. (Rev 18)
</div>

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2015 Q96</div>
Prerequisites that must remain satisfied during fuel movement into the reactor: <span class="hi-exam">the manipulator crane is air-powered for gripping, so a loss of Control Air to containment would preclude performing core alterations</span>. Both SRNIs must be operable providing VISUAL indication with ONE providing AUDIBLE indication; only ONE RHR loop need be in operation in Mode 6 (two loops OPERABLE required when &lt;23' above the flange); racking down the standby (22) RHR pump breaker and opening containment airlock doors do not stop fuel movement.
</div>
