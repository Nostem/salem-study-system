# S2.OP-ST.CAN-0007 — Refueling Operations Containment Closure

> ⚠️ DRAFT — Reconstructed from exam materials

## Purpose

Provides surveillance test requirements for verifying containment closure capability during refueling operations and movement of irradiated fuel inside containment.

## Key Actions / Information

**Exam & operating coverage:**

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2012 Q67</div>
Confirms (per page 8) that <span class="hi-exam">BOTH 100' elevation containment airlock doors being open does NOT require suspending fuel movement</span> — the doors need only be CAPABLE of being shut. (Among the Q67 conditions, only Control Area Ventilation in FIRE OUTSIDE CONTROL AREA / Recirculation Mode requires suspension.)
</div>

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2016 Q68</div>
Per page 8, the <span class="hi-exam">100' elevation containment airlock doors are only required to be CAPABLE of being shut</span> during fuel movement — they may be open. <span class="hi-trap">Open airlock doors are NOT a condition that requires suspension of fuel movement, as long as the airlock retains the capability to be shut.</span>
</div>
