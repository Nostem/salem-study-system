# S1.OP-SO.SJ-0001 — Preparation of the Safety Injection System for Operation

## Purpose

Provides the lineup and valve positioning for placing the Safety Injection (ECCS) system in its standby readiness configuration, including the normal positions of BIT, VCT outlet, RHR-to-cold-leg, and containment sump isolation valves prior to an automatic SI actuation.

## Key Actions / Information

**Exam & operating coverage:**

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2012 Q36</div>
On a Safety Injection signal, the <span class="hi-exam">BIT inlet valves (1SJ4/5) and BIT outlet valves (1SJ12/13) are normally SHUT and receive an OPEN signal from SSPS</span> — if 1SJ12 AND 1SJ13 fail to reposition, BIT injection flow is blocked. By contrast: <span class="hi-trap">1CV40/41 (VCT Outlet) receive a CLOSE signal, but charging-pump suction automatically realigns to the RWST via 1SJ1/1SJ2 opening, so their failure does not block injection</span>; <span class="hi-exam">11SJ49/12SJ49 (RHR Discharge to Cold Leg) are normally OPEN at power and do not reposition during a LOCA</span>; <span class="hi-exam">11SJ44/12SJ44 (Containment Sump Isolation) open in EOP-LOCA-3 during transfer to cold-leg recirc and provide NO ECCS injection flow</span>.
</div>
