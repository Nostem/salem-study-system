# S1.OP-ST.CVC-0003 — Inservice Testing, 11 Charging Pump

> DRAFT — Reconstructed from exam materials

## Purpose

Quarterly (92-day) ASME inservice test (IST) surveillance for the 11 Centrifugal Charging Pump. Records pump differential pressure, flow, and bearing vibration, and strokes the associated test-return valve, then compares each measured parameter against the IST Acceptance Criteria in S1.RA-ST.CVC-0003 to determine pump operability. Step 5.3 performs the Acceptance Criteria review and dispositions the surveillance SAT / UNSAT.

## Key Actions / Information

<div class="callout callout-jpm">
<div class="callout-label">JPM — 2015 RO-A2</div>
Surveillance-review JPM. The applicant reviews a completed S1.OP-ST.CVC-0003 against the S1.RA-ST.CVC-0003 Acceptance Criteria (Step 5.3) and must catch two mis-classified data points:
<ul>
<li><strong>Pump Inboard Bearing Vibration Position P1H</strong> value of <span class="hi-exam"><span class="val-trip">0.710 in/sec</span></span> is incorrectly written in the "Alert Range SAT" column. Per S1.RA-ST.CVC-0003 page 5, a value <span class="hi-exam">&gt;0.70 in/sec is in the Required Action Range — UNSAT</span>.</li>
<li><strong>1SW185 stroke time</strong> of <span class="hi-exam">5.25 seconds</span> is incorrectly written in the "Required Evaluation Range SAT" column. Per S1.RA-ST.CVC-0003 page 7, a value between <span class="hi-exam">1.8 – 5.4 seconds is in the Acceptable Range</span> (SAT). The re-test of 1SW185 was therefore NOT required (the re-test is only required if the stroke time falls in the Required Evaluation Range SAT band per Section 5.4.2.K.1).</li>
</ul>
Because the P1H bearing vibration is in the Required Action Range, the overall surveillance is <span class="hi-exam">UNSAT IAW Acceptance Criteria 5.3.2</span> — INITIATE a NOTF to correct the unsatisfactory condition and RECORD the NOTF number / reason on Attachment 5.
</div>
