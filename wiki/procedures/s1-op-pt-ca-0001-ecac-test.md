# S1.OP-PT.CA-0001 — Emergency Control Air Compressor Test

## Purpose

Provides direction for performing periodic testing of the Emergency Control Air Compressors (ECACs) to verify operability and proper operation.

## Key Actions / Information

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2020 Q54</div>
Testing per this procedure requires knowledge of ECAC power supplies. <span class="hi-exam">#1 ECAC is powered from the 1C 460V Vital Bus</span>. A trip of 1C 4KV to 460V breaker 1C4D would result in loss of the #1 ECAC. <span class="hi-trap">#1 Station Air Compressor (SAC) is powered from 1H 4KV Group Bus — do not confuse SAC power supplies with ECAC power supplies.</span>
</div>

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2016 Q62</div>
Testing the Unit 2 ECAC under this procedure: <span class="hi-exam">Unit 2 ECAC is powered from the 2C 460V Vital Bus</span>. A trip of <span class="hi-exam">2C 4KV to 460V breaker 2C4D</span> de-energizes the running ECAC and impacts CA Header pressure. <span class="hi-trap">Trap: Station Air Compressors are powered from Group buses (e.g., 2A4D, 2E6D, 2H5D feed Group buses) — do NOT confuse SAC power supplies with ECAC power supplies. Only loss of the 2C 460V Vital Bus impacts the running ECAC.</span>
</div>

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2015 Q62</div>
PT.CA-001 P&amp;L 3.4 directs operators to <span class="hi-exam">manually trip the ECAC after 25 seconds of low oil pressure</span> — but this requires operator action, so with NO operator action assumed it does not apply (the ECAC remains running). See [[Control Air]].
</div>
