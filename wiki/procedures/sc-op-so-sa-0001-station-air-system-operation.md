# SC.OP-SO.SA-0001 — Station Air System Operation

> ⚠️ DRAFT — Reconstructed from exam materials

## Purpose

Common (shared between Units 1 and 2) system operating procedure for starting, swapping, and securing the Station Air Compressors (SACs) which supply the Salem Station Air header. The Station Air header in turn supplies the Control Air headers via reducing valves.

## Key Actions / Information

<div class="callout callout-jpm">
<div class="callout-label">JPM — 2016 Sim-h</div>
Used by AB.CA-0001 / S2.OP-AR.ZZ-0011 ARP for COMPR 2 TROUBLE: when an SAC trips, <span class="hi-exam">coordinate (as necessary) with Unit 1 and ENSURE operating OR START at least one SAC IAW SC.OP-SO.SA-0001</span>. Salem has three SACs (#1, #2, #3) that are common to both units; either control room can start any SAC. <span class="hi-trap">Next-to-load SAC (follow) does not auto-start until Station Air header pressure drops 5 psig below the 105 psig follow setpoint for ~5 seconds — there is a delay before automatic backup compressor start.</span>
