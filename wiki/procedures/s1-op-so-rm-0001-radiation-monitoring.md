# S1.OP-SO.RM-0001 — Radiation Monitoring System Operation

> ⚠️ DRAFT — Reconstructed from exam materials

## Purpose

System operating procedure for the Unit 1 radiation monitoring system, covering normal operation and source check evolutions on plant process radiation monitors.

## Key Actions / Information

**Exam & operating coverage:**

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2016 Q60</div>
Source check duration limit: <span class="hi-exam">activate the Source Check for 30 seconds or less to prevent solenoids from overheating</span>. Both the system operating procedure (S1.OP-SO.RM-0001) and surveillance procedure (S1.OP-ST.RM-0001) contain precautions limiting check source operation to 30 seconds or less. <span class="hi-trap">Trap: terminating early "just until indication of rising level is detected" prolongs source life but is NOT the procedural reason for the 30-second limit; some monitors do NOT automatically block alarm actions during a source check, but solenoid overheating is the cited precaution.</span>
</div>
