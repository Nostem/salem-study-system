# S2.OP-SO.SF-0004 — Draining the Refueling Cavity

## Purpose

System operating procedure for draining the refueling cavity back to the RWST following refueling. It is the technical reference that documents the RWST / refueling cavity inventory and elevation relationships used during cavity fill and drain evolutions.

## Key Actions / Information

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2015 Q29</div>
This procedure is the source reference for the RWST ↔ refueling cavity inventory relationship: the refueling cavity at <span class="hi-exam">110'</span> holds ~112000 gallons, and the RWST normal operating level is a minimum of <span class="hi-exam">40.5 feet</span> (364500 gal) prior to refueling. The PZR cold-calibrated 0% reference is at the <span class="hi-exam">108' 11"</span> elevation in containment, so once cavity level rises above that elevation the PZR cold cal indication reads on-scale (a small positive value) rather than off-scale.
</div>
