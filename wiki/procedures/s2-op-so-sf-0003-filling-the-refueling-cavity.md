# S2.OP-SO.SF-0003 — Filling the Refueling Cavity

## Purpose

System operating procedure for filling the refueling cavity from the RWST in preparation for refueling (Mode 6, reactor vessel head removed). Establishes the cavity water level over the reactor vessel for fuel movement.

## Key Actions / Information

**Exam & operating coverage:**

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2015 Q29</div>
Filling the refueling cavity from the RWST shifts a large inventory of water from the RWST into containment. With the cavity at <span class="hi-exam">110'</span> (holding ~112000 gallons) and the RWST starting at its normal pre-refueling minimum of <span class="hi-exam">40.5'</span> (364500 gal), the RWST drains to ~253000 gallons remaining ≈ <span class="hi-exam">27.5'</span>. Because the cavity water surface (110') is above the PZR cold-calibrated 0% reference elevation (108' 11" in containment), <span class="hi-exam">PZR Cold Calibrated level reads a small positive value (~3%), not off-scale</span>. The tested concept is the physical/elevation connection between the RWST, refueling cavity, and PZR cold cal level — not memorization of tank volumes.
</div>
