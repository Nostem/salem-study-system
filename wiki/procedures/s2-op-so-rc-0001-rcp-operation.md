# S2.OP-SO.RC-0001 — Reactor Coolant Pump Operation

## Purpose

System operating procedure for Reactor Coolant Pump startup, operation, and shutdown.

## Key Actions / Information

**Exam & operating coverage:**

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2015 Q30</div>
Opening <span class="hi-exam">1CV114 RCP Seal Bypass Valve</span> (step 5.2.1) requires <span class="hi-exam">RCS pressure between 100 and 1000 psig</span>; if RCS pressure is &lt;100 psig CV114 must be shut. Other prerequisites: RCP seal leakoff flow &lt;1 gpm, 11-14CV104 SEAL LEAKOFF valves open, seal water flow at least 6 gpm to each RCP.
</div>

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2015 Q31</div>
When starting an RCP, <span class="hi-exam">Oil Lift Pump discharge pressure &lt;500 psig will prevent the RCP from starting</span> when the START PB is depressed (lift-oil start interlock).
</div>

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2015 Q79</div>
EOP-SGTR-1 directs RCP restart <span class="hi-exam">via SO.RC-0001 (rather than starting a RCP directly), so all support systems and P&amp;Ls for starting a RCP must be met</span>. See [[RCPs]], [[EOP-SGTR-1 — Steam Generator Tube Rupture]].
</div>

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2019 Q1</div>
Per S2.OP-SO.RC-0001 Limitations, with one or more RCS Cold Leg temperatures &le;312&deg;F, <span class="hi-exam">at least one of</span> the following conditions shall be met before starting an RCP:<br>
1. Pressurizer level is less than 92%<br>
2. ALL Steam Generator secondary side temperatures are less than 50&deg;F above any RCS Cold Leg temperature<br>
Starting an RCP when the start criteria are NOT met will result in <span class="hi-exam">an RCS pressure transient caused by energy addition from the secondary system</span>. <span class="hi-trap">Only ONE condition needs to be met (not both). RCP motor degradation is not the concern — the concern is an RCS pressure transient from secondary energy addition.</span>
</div>
