# SC.CH-AB.ZZ-1104 — Response to Abnormal Secondary Chemistry Events

## Purpose

Chemistry department procedure for responding to abnormal secondary plant chemistry events, including increased sampling frequency to confirm and trend secondary chemistry parameters (Sodium, Chloride, Sulfate, Cation Conductivity, pH). Invoked by S2.OP-AB.CHEM-0001 (Step 3.11) when control room operators identify a secondary chemistry abnormality, so that Chemistry can establish the data needed to determine the applicable Chemistry Action Level.

## Key Actions / Information

**Exam & operating coverage:**

<div class="callout callout-jpm">
<div class="callout-label">JPM — 2015 SRO-A1.a</div>
When abnormal secondary chemistry is identified (rising 22B hotwell Sodium; SGBD Sodium <span class="hi-exam">51 ppb</span>), AB.CHEM-0001 Step 3.11 directs the SRO to <span class="hi-exam">notify Chemistry to initiate sampling IAW SC.CH-AB.ZZ-1104(Q)</span>. The confirmed sample data is then entered into AB.CHEM-0001 Attachment 4 (CHEMISTRY ACTION LEVELS), which placed Unit 1 in <span class="hi-exam">Chemistry Action Level 2</span>.
