# S2.OP-AR.DG-0003 — 2C Diesel Generator Alarm Response

## Purpose
Alarm Response Procedure for the 2C Emergency Diesel Generator. Provides operator actions for 2C EDG annunciator windows, including OHA J-20 (2C DG URGENT) and the associated 2C EDG local alarm panel windows for air start system and engine auxiliary conditions.

**Exam & operating coverage:**

<div class="callout callout-scenario">
<div class="callout-label">Scenario — 2014 #1</div>
A large air leak develops on the 2C EDG starting air supply line. <span class="hi-exam">OHA J-20, 2C DG URGENT</span> annunciates and the field operator reports both air start compressors running with Air Start Tank pressure lowering. The crew uses this ARP, then the CRS determines 2C EDG is inoperable based on lower-than-required Air Start Tank pressure and enters the appropriate Tech Spec. See [[2014 Scenario 1]] and [[2014 NRC Operating Exam]].
