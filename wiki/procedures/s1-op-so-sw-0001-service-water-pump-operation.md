# S1.OP-SO.SW-0001 — Service Water Pump Operation

## Purpose

Operating procedure for starting, stopping, and aligning the Service Water pumps, including the normal at-power lineup (one pump in AUTO, the rest in manual) and pump-to-vital-bus power assignments.

## Key Actions / Information

**Exam & operating coverage:**

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2015 Q61</div>
Normal at-power SW lineup has <span class="hi-exam">one SW pump in AUTO and the rest in manual</span>. On a single 4KV vital-bus UV / Bus Differential lockout, only that bus loads in blackout sequencing; the SW pumps on the lost bus stop, header pressure drops, and the AUTO pump starts. <span class="hi-trap">Unit 1 SW pump power supplies are reversed from Unit 2 (Unit 2: 21/22 on A bus, 25/26 on C bus).</span>
</div>

<div class="callout callout-scenario">
<div class="callout-label">Scenario — 2015 #3</div>
On a 25 SW pump discharge strainer high D/P, the CRS directs the local operator to collect the <span class="hi-exam">Trip/High D/P Observations IAW Attachment 2</span> of this procedure. With the strainer shaft NOT turning (manual backwash impossible) and D/P ~10.5 psid, the field data supports declaring the SW pump inoperable when strainer D/P exceeds <span class="hi-exam">10 psid</span>.
</div>
