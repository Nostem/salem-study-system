# S2.OP-SO.RC-0002 — Vacuum Refill of the RCS

## Purpose

System operating procedure for vacuum refill of the RCS and establishing a pressurizer steam bubble.

## Key Actions / Information

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2019 Q7</div>
Per S2.OP-SO.RC-0002 Section 5.4 ("Establishing Pressurizer Steam Bubble Without A Vacuum In RCS"), during Pressurizer degassing the crew will open and close 2PR1 (PZR PORV) to maintain <span class="hi-exam">PRT pressure less than a MAXIMUM of <span class="val-alarm">10 psig</span></span>. <span class="hi-trap">25 psig is a plausible distractor (below the 100 psig rupture disc setpoint) but the actual procedural limit is 10 psig.</span>
</div>

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2012 Q37</div>
PRT response while drawing a bubble after a vacuum refill: operators perform a <span class="hi-exam">10-15 minute PZR vent (Step 5.3.28) with PZR level 40-60% (Step 5.3.5)</span>. Venting air and non-condensables by opening the PZR PORVs <span class="hi-exam">slowly raises PRT pressure</span> (minimal liquid carryover). <span class="hi-trap">The PORVs are controlled in MANUAL — they do NOT cycle automatically. RCP bumps are performed BEFORE the vacuum is used.</span>
</div>
