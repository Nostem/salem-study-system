# EOP-SGTR-5 — SGTR without Pressurizer Pressure Control

## Purpose

Provides guidance for recovery from a steam generator tube rupture when the normal means of controlling RCS pressure to terminate primary-to-secondary break flow is unavailable — that is, when neither normal pressurizer spray nor a pressurizer PORV is available to depressurize the RCS to the ruptured SG pressure. EOP-SGTR-1 directs a transition to EOP-SGTR-5 when the RCS depressurization step cannot be performed because depressurization capability has been lost (loss of normal spray and no available PORV).

## Key Actions / Information

- Entered from [[EOP-SGTR-1 — Steam Generator Tube Rupture]] at the RCS depressurization step when no depressurization path is available (normal spray lost and PORV unavailable).
- Establishes an alternate strategy to control RCS pressure and terminate break flow into the ruptured SG without using normal pressurizer pressure control.
