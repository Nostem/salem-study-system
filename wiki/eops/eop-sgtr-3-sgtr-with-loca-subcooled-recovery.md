# EOP-SGTR-3 — SGTR with LOCA Subcooled Recovery

## Purpose

Provides guidance for recovery from a steam generator tube rupture when a concurrent LOCA is indicated by loss of subcooling after SI has been terminated. The procedure addresses the condition where subcooling can be restored by restarting ECCS pumps (subcooled recovery path).

## Key Actions / Information

**Exam & operating coverage:**

<div class="callout callout-exam">
<div class="callout-label">Exam — 2020 Q79</div>
Per EOP-SGTR-1 CAS: <span class="hi-exam">If SI has been terminated and RCS subcooling reaches 0°F, start ECCS pumps as necessary to restore subcooling and GO TO EOP-SGTR-3</span> (SGTR with LOCA – Subcooled Recovery). <span class="hi-trap">Do NOT confuse with EOP-SGTR-4 (Saturated Recovery) — SGTR-3 is the correct transition when ECCS pumps can restore subcooling. SGTR-4 applies when saturated conditions persist and subcooled recovery is not achievable.</span>
</div>

<div class="callout callout-scenario">
<div class="callout-label">Scenario — 2016 #2</div>
EOP-SGTR-3 entered from EOP-SGTR-1 because the RCS depressurization PORV (<span class="hi-exam">2PR2</span>) fails to shut after demand and the corresponding Block Valve <span class="hi-exam">2PR7 fails 90% open</span> (the open limit extinguished but the closed limit did not illuminate). With the stuck-open PORV path established, the loss of RCS pressure is treated as a SGTR with concurrent LOCA — CRS transitions to SGTR-3 to address the loss of RCS pressure with the stuck-open PORV. Scenario terminates after the transition to SGTR-3 is announced.
</div>
