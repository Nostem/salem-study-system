# Gaps — Stator Cooling Water

UFSAR Chapter 10 has no dedicated stator water cooling subsection; the system is covered only within §10.2 Turbine Generator. The following structural elements a system writeup would normally cover are not sourced in §10.2:

- Pump configuration — number of stator cooling water pumps, running/standby arrangement, ratings — checked UFSAR §10.2.2 / §10.2.7, existing callouts; not in §10.2 (callouts confirm a "standby Stator Cooling Water pump" exists but give no count or ratings)
- Flow rates — stator cooling water flow rate / normal operating flow — checked UFSAR §10.2; not sourced (only "low stator winding water flow" runback logic is given, no numeric setpoint)
- Pressure / temperature setpoints — numeric low-water-pressure, outlet-temperature, and flow runback setpoints for the Unit 2 stator water turbine runback scheme — checked UFSAR §10.2.2.4; only the 2-of-3 logic and the stator-current limits (79% at 2 min, 23% at 3.5 min) are given, not the individual trip setpoints
- Water purity / conductivity control — deionizer, conductivity limits, makeup source for stator cooling water — checked UFSAR §10.2.2 / §10.2.7; not in §10.2
- Stator winding temperature limits / alarm setpoints — checked UFSAR §10.2.2.5; UFSAR lists stator winding temperatures as a monitored parameter but gives no numeric limit (2015 Scenario #2 callout gives an operational "&lt; 10°F over assigned limit" indication, not a sourced setpoint)
- Unit 1 vs Unit 2 stator runback applicability — UFSAR §10.2.2.4 item 7 lists the generator stator water turbine runback as Unit 2 only; whether Unit 1 has an equivalent stator-water runback path is not stated in §10.2
- Tech Spec coverage — no Tech Spec LCO is identified for stator cooling water (non-safety-related turbine auxiliary); checked wiki/tech-specs — none applicable
