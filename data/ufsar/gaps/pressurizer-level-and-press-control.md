# Gaps — pressurizer-level-and-press-control (catalog system 011)

UFSAR §7.7.2.4 (Pressurizer Pressure Control) and §7.7.2.5 (Pressurizer Level Control) are
qualitative control-logic writeups ("a given value", "a given setpoint"); numeric pressure
setpoints live in UFSAR Table 5.2-1 (already in the article, cross-checked against the
pressurizer system article — no disagreement). The topics below are not answered by any of the
four sources (UFSAR §7.7.2.4/§7.7.2.5/§5.5.10.3, TS, system descriptions, existing callouts) for
this article and are recorded as gaps rather than fabricated.

- 011 A2.12 — Operation of auxiliary spray — checked UFSAR §7.7.2.4, §5.5.10.3.4 (auxiliary CVCS-to-spray-line path noted in pressurizer article, not in this article's sections); no sourced operating detail for auxiliary spray in this article's UFSAR scope
- 011 A4.02 / 011 K5.11 / 011 K5.24 — Letdown pressure control valve operated via manual controller; demand-signal-to-valve-position correlation — checked UFSAR §7.7.2.5 (covers charging/level only, not letdown pressure control valve), no sourced value
