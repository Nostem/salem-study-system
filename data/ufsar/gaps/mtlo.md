# Gaps — mtlo (Main Turbine Lube Oil; UFSAR §10.2 Turbine Generator)

Note: the mtlo article is scoped to the main-turbine LUBE OIL subsystem only (slug/title/aliases), while catalog system 045 covers the entire Main Turbine Generator. The K/A topics below are the lube-oil/bearing-oil-relevant subset; the broader MT/G K/As (EHC, voltage regulator, synchronizing, hydrogen, stator cooling, steam dumps, etc.) belong to the main-turbine/turbine-generator article, not here.

- 045 K2.02 — Electrical power supplies to the MT/G lube oil pumps (specific buses / EDG loading for the ac auxiliary and dc emergency pumps) — checked UFSAR §10.2.2.1 (states ac motor-driven and dc motor-driven only, no bus identification), §10.2.2.4, existing callouts; no sourced bus/power-supply value found
- 045 K4.21 — Changeover from the auxiliary bearing oil pump to the shaft-driven main oil pump as turbine speed increases (changeover speed/pressure setpoint) — checked UFSAR §10.2.2.1 (qualitative: shaft pump supplies oil during normal operation, ac pump during turning gear/startup/shutdown), no changeover setpoint stated
- 045 K4.19 — Low-speed rotation by turbine turning gear to prevent "set" in the shaft, and its lube oil dependency — checked UFSAR §10.2.2.1, §10.2.6 (turning gear mentioned only in context of bearing oil supply and hydrogen gas changing); turning-gear "set" prevention not described in §10.2
- 045 K4.36 / K5.35 — MT/G coastdown and connection to turning gear at zero speed; requirement that a lube oil pump be running before turning gear engagement — checked UFSAR §10.2.2.1; not stated in §10.2 (operating-procedure detail)
- 045 K4.48 — Trip of MT/G and lube oil pumps by the Fire Protection System (FPS) — checked UFSAR §10.2.2.4 (turbine protective devices list does not include an FPS lube-oil-pump trip); not in §10.2
- 045 K5.20 — Effect of temperature on lube oil viscosity — checked UFSAR §10.2.2.1, §10.2.2.5; no viscosity-vs-temperature discussion in §10.2
- 045 K5.21 — Purpose of the turbine lube oil lift pump (to hold the MT/G off the main bearing at low rotation speeds) — UFSAR §10.2.2.1 states the hydraulic lift pump supplies a small quantity of high pressure oil to selected bearings during startup, but does not state the "lift the shaft off the bearing" purpose explicitly; recorded as a partial gap (qualitative function captured, stated purpose not sourced in §10.2)
- 045 K6.08 / K1.21 / K3.06 — Quantitative effect of conditions/malfunctions on the turbine lube oil system, and bearing-oil-system interconnections/effect-of-loss values (reservoir capacity, pump flow/pressure ratings, header pressure setpoints) — checked UFSAR §10.2.2.1, §10.2.2.4, §10.2.2.5, existing callouts (only the 12 psig ABOP/HP-seal-oil-BU auto-start setpoint and 9 mils vibration trip criterion are sourced, both from the 2017 ESG-7 scenario callout); no UFSAR-sourced flow/pressure/capacity ratings found
- Lube oil cooling source — checked UFSAR §10.2.7 (Turbine Auxiliaries Cooling System lists generator seal oil coolers and turbine electro-hydraulic control fluid coolers, but does NOT explicitly list a main turbine lube oil cooler); cooling-water source for the main bearing lube oil coolers not sourced in §10.2
