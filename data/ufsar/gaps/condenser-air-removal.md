# Gaps — condenser-air-removal (catalog system 055)

Note: Salem's Main Condenser Evacuation System (UFSAR §10.4.2) uses water-ring rotary
vacuum pumps with an air ejector as the primary stage. It has NO steam-jet air ejectors
or steam-supplied (CARS) sealing steam. Several catalog-055 K/As assume a steam-jet-air-
ejector / sealing-steam design and have no Salem-specific UFSAR home; these are recorded
below rather than fabricated.

- 055 A1.03 — Condenser air removal alarms and lights (specific annunciators / indicating lights) — checked UFSAR §10.4.2, existing callouts; only the Control Room rad-monitor alarm on the common discharge header is sourced; no specific alarm/light list found
- 055 A2.01 — Operator response to loss of Circulating Water System (CWS) — checked UFSAR §10.4.2, §10.4.5, existing callouts; loss-of-CWS response not sourced in the air-removal section (CWS is §10.4.5)
- 055 A2.02 — Operator response to loss of gland seal / gland exhaust — checked UFSAR §10.4.2, §10.4.3 (Turbine Gland Sealing); not an air-removal function, no sourced response in §10.4.2
- 055 A3.02 — Steam to CARS (steam supply to condenser air removal) — checked UFSAR §10.4.2; Salem air ejector uses atmospheric air, not steam, as motive fluid; no steam-to-CARS feature exists
- 055 A4.01 — Sealing steam control-room operation — checked UFSAR §10.4.2; no sealing steam in the water-ring vacuum pump design; not applicable
- 055 A4.03 — Steam to CARS control-room operation — checked UFSAR §10.4.2; no steam-to-CARS feature exists at Salem
- 055 A4.04 — Realign CARS exhaust path (control-room operation) — checked UFSAR §10.4.2, S2.OP-SO.AR-0001 referenced in callouts; exhaust routes to a common header then plant vent, but no manual exhaust-path realignment procedure is sourced in the UFSAR
- 055 K1.04 — Interconnection with S/G — checked UFSAR §10.4.2; no direct air-removal/S/G interconnection sourced (relationship is indirect, via condenser/secondary chemistry)
- 055 K1.05 — Interconnection with polishing demineralizers — checked UFSAR §10.4.2, §10.4.6; no air-removal/condensate-polisher interconnection sourced
- 055 K1.09 — Interconnection with auxiliary steam — checked UFSAR §10.4.2; no auxiliary-steam interconnection sourced (water-ring pumps use TAC cooling and demin seal water, not aux steam)
- 055 K1.10 — Interconnection with HVAC systems — checked UFSAR §10.4.2; no air-removal/HVAC interconnection sourced
- 055 K3.01 — Effect of loss of air removal on the main condenser — checked UFSAR §10.4.2, existing callouts; qualitatively covered by callouts (loss of vacuum / rising backpressure), but no UFSAR design-basis statement quantifying the effect was located
- 055 K5.04 — S/G chemistry related to main condenser air in-leakage — checked UFSAR §10.4.2, §10.4.6, §10.3.5; condenser air in-leakage detection is noted but the specific S/G-chemistry linkage is not sourced in §10.4.2
- 055 K5.05 — Sources/impacts of high radiation related to S/G tube leakage — checked UFSAR §10.4.2, §11.2.3.1; the air-removal discharge rad monitor is sourced, but the S/G-tube-leakage source/impact discussion is not in §10.4.2 (belongs to radiation monitoring / SG tube leak material)
