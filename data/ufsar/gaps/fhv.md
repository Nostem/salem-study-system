# Gaps — fhv (catalog system 034)

Note on scope: catalog system 034 is "Fuel Handling Equipment / Refueling," but `wiki/systems/fhv.md`
is specifically the Fuel Handling Area VENTILATION system (UFSAR §9.4.3). The large majority of the 034
K/A topics (fuel-handling machine load/travel limits, position/speed, dropped or mispositioned fuel,
dropped cask, transfer car stuck, refueling water level, NIS/shutdown-monitor neutron levels,
RHR cooling flow loss, fuel-handling machine malfunction/overload) belong to the fuel-handling
EQUIPMENT/refueling article, not to this ventilation article, and are out of scope for §9.4.3.
Only the ventilation-relevant residual topics are recorded below.
